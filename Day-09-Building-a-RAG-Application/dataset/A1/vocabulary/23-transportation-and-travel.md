# Chapter 23: Transportation and Travel

## Learning Objectives

By the end of this chapter, you can:
- Name common forms of transportation.
- Talk about simple journeys.
- Ask basic questions about tickets and travel.

## 1. Types of Transportation

| Word | Meaning |
|---|---|
| car | A road vehicle for people |
| bus | A large road vehicle for many passengers |
| train | A vehicle that travels on railway tracks |
| subway | An underground city train system |
| taxi | A car that carries passengers for money |
| bicycle | A vehicle with two wheels and pedals |
| motorcycle | A two-wheeled motor vehicle |
| airplane | A vehicle that flies |
| boat | A small vehicle that travels on water |
| ship | A large boat |
| truck | A large vehicle used to carry goods |
| van | A medium-sized vehicle for people or goods |
| scooter | A small two-wheeled vehicle |

## 2. Travel Vocabulary

| Word | Meaning |
|---|---|
| trip | A journey to a place |
| journey | Traveling from one place to another |
| vacation | A period of time away from work or school |
| ticket | A document that allows you to travel |
| passenger | A person traveling in a vehicle |
| driver | A person who drives a vehicle |
| seat | A place to sit |
| luggage | Bags and suitcases used for travel |
| suitcase | A large bag for clothes and other travel items |
| map | A picture that shows places |
| station | A place where trains or buses stop |
| stop | A place where a bus or train picks up passengers |
| traffic | Vehicles moving on roads |

## 3. Travel Verbs

| Verb | Example |
|---|---|
| travel | I travel by train. |
| drive | My father drives a car. |
| ride | She rides a bicycle. |
| fly | We fly to another city. |
| walk | I walk to school. |
| take | I take the bus every morning. |
| arrive | The train arrives at noon. |
| leave | The bus leaves at eight. |
| wait | We wait for the train. |

## 4. Transportation Expressions

- by car
- by bus

## 5. Example Sentences

- I go to work by bus.
- She travels by train.
- We take a taxi to the airport.
- My brother rides his bicycle to school.
- The train arrives at nine.
- They travel by airplane.
- I walk to the grocery store.

## 6. Short Dialogue

**Alex:** How do you go to work?

**Mia:** I take the subway.

**Alex:** How long does it take?

**Mia:** About twenty minutes.

**Alex:** Do you like traveling by subway?

**Mia:** Yes, it is fast and easy.

## 7. Useful Questions

| Question | Example Answer |
|---|---|
| How do you travel? | I travel by train. |
| Where is the bus stop? | It is near the park. |
| How much is a ticket? | It is five dollars. |
| When does the train leave? | It leaves at ten. |
| Where is your luggage? | It is next to my seat. |

## 8. Exercises

### Exercise A: Choose the Correct Answer

1. A ______ travels on railway tracks.
   - a) train
   - b) bicycle
   - c) truck

2. You can travel through the air by ______.
   - a) boat
   - b) airplane
   - c) bus

3. A person who drives a vehicle is a ______.
   - a) passenger
   - b) driver
   - c) tourist

4. You carry clothes in a ______.
   - a) suitcase
   - b) map
   - c) ticket

### Exercise B: Fill in the Blanks

Use: `bus`, `train`, `ticket`, `bicycle`, `luggage`

1. I ride my ______ to school.
2. We need a ______ to travel.
3. The ______ stops at the station.
4. I take the ______ to work every day.
5. My ______ is heavy. It has many clothes.

### Exercise C: True or False

1. A passenger drives every vehicle.
2. A bicycle has two wheels.
3. A suitcase is used for travel.
4. An airplane travels on railway tracks.

### Exercise D: Speaking Practice

Answer these questions:

1. How do you usually go to work or school?
2. Do you like traveling by train?
3. What do you put in your suitcase?
4. Where do you want to travel?
