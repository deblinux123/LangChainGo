# Chapter 31: School and Education

## Learning Objectives

By the end of this chapter, you can:
- Name common school objects and places.
- Talk about subjects and classes.
- Describe simple school activities.

## 1. People at School

| Word | Meaning |
|---|---|
| student | A person who studies |
| teacher | A person who teaches |
| classmate | A student in the same class |
| principal | The person who leads a school |
| professor | A teacher at a college or university |
| librarian | A person who works in a library |
| school bus driver | A person who drives a school bus |
| school nurse | A nurse who works at a school |

## 2. School Objects

| Word | Meaning |
|---|---|
| book | Something you read |
| notebook | A book with blank or lined pages |
| pen | A tool used for writing with ink |
| pencil | A tool used for writing or drawing |
| eraser | Something used to remove pencil marks |
| ruler | A tool used to measure or draw straight lines |
| backpack | A bag carried on your back |
| desk | A table used by a student |
| chair | Something used for sitting |
| board | A surface used by a teacher for writing |
| computer | An electronic device used for work and learning |
| dictionary | A book or resource that explains words |

## 3. School Places

| Word | Meaning |
|---|---|
| classroom | A room where students have lessons |
| library | A place where people read or borrow books |
| cafeteria | A place where students eat |
| playground | An outdoor area where children play |
| gymnasium | A large room for sports and exercise |
| office | A room where school staff work |
| hallway | A passage between rooms |
| laboratory | A room for scientific activities |

## 4. School Subjects

| Subject | Meaning |
|---|---|
| English | The study of the English language |
| math | The study of numbers and calculations |
| science | The study of the natural world |
| history | The study of the past |
| geography | The study of places and the earth |
| art | The study and practice of creative activities |
| music | The study of sounds and instruments |
| physical education | A school subject about sports and exercise |
| computer science | The study of computers and programming |

## 5. School Activities

| Verb or Phrase | Example |
|---|---|
| study | I study English every day. |
| learn | We learn new words. |
| read | She reads a book. |
| write | He writes in his notebook. |
| ask a question | The student asks a question. |
| answer a question | I answer the teacher's question. |
| do homework | We do homework after school. |
| take a test | They take a test on Friday. |
| take notes | I take notes in class. |
| raise your hand | Please raise your hand. |

## 6. Example Sentences

- I am a student.
- My teacher is very kind.
- We study English and math.
- I write with a blue pen.
- My notebook is in my backpack.
- The students are in the classroom.
- We have science class on Monday.
- I do my homework after school.

## 7. Short Dialogue

**Teacher:** Good morning, everyone.

**Students:** Good morning!

**Teacher:** Please open your books to page ten.

**Student:** Excuse me. Can you repeat that, please?

**Teacher:** Open your books to page ten.

**Student:** Thank you.

## 8. Useful Expressions

- What is your favorite subject?
- I like English.
- Can you help me, please?
- Can you repeat that?
- How do you spell this word?
- I don't understand.
- May I ask a question?
- What page are we on?
