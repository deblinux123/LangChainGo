# Chapter 30: Sports and Exercise

## Learning Objectives

By the end of this chapter, you can:
- Name common sports.
- Talk about exercise and physical activities.
- Describe your favorite sport.

## 1. Team Sports

| Sport | Meaning |
|---|---|
| soccer | A game played by two teams that try to kick a ball into a goal |
| basketball | A game in which players throw a ball into a high basket |
| volleyball | A game in which teams hit a ball over a net |
| baseball | A game played with a bat and a ball |
| football | A sport played with an oval ball in American football |
| hockey | A sport played with sticks and a puck or ball |
| rugby | A team sport played with an oval ball |

## 2. Individual Sports

| Sport | Meaning |
|---|---|
| tennis | A game played with rackets and a ball |
| badminton | A racket sport played with a shuttlecock |
| swimming | Moving through water |
| running | Moving quickly on foot |
| cycling | Riding a bicycle |
| boxing | A sport in which two people fight with their fists |
| golf | A game played with clubs and a small ball |
| gymnastics | Physical exercises and movements |

## 3. Exercise Vocabulary

| Word | Meaning |
|---|---|
| exercise | Physical activity |
| workout | A period of exercise |
| gym | A place where people exercise |
| coach | A person who trains athletes |
| team | A group of players |
| player | A person who plays a sport |
| match | A sports competition |
| game | An activity or competition with rules |
| race | A competition to see who is fastest |
| ball | A round object used in many sports |
| racket | Equipment used to hit a ball |
| net | A material barrier used in some sports |
| goal | A point scored in some sports |
| score | The number of points in a game |

## 4. Exercise Verbs

| Verb | Example |
|---|---|
| run | I run every morning. |

| kick | He kicks the ball. |
| throw | She throws the ball. |
| catch | I catch the ball. |
| hit | He hits the ball with a racket. |

## 5. Example Sentences

- I play soccer with my friends.
- She plays tennis on Saturday.
- He swims every morning.
- Our team wins the match.
- They exercise at the gym.
- My brother likes basketball.
- I go running three times a week.

## 6. Short Dialogue

**Sam:** What is your favorite sport?

**Alex:** My favorite sport is basketball.

**Sam:** Do you play it often?

**Alex:** Yes. I play with my friends twice a week.

**Sam:** Where do you play?

**Alex:** At the sports center near my home.

## 7. Useful Expressions

- I like playing soccer.
- I go swimming every week.
- My favorite sport is tennis.
- Our team won the game.
- I exercise at the gym.
- Do you play basketball?
- Let's go running!

## 8. Exercises

### Exercise A: Choose the Correct Answer

1. You use a racket to play ______.
   - a) tennis
   - b) soccer
   - c) swimming

2. You throw a ball into a basket in ______.
   - a) golf
   - b) basketball
   - c) boxing

3. A person who trains a sports team is a ______.
   - a) coach
   - b) passenger
   - c) waiter

4. A competition to see who is fastest is a ______.
   - a) race
   - b) lesson
   - c) meal

### Exercise B: Fill in the Blanks

3. Our ______ trains the team.
4. We have a tennis ______ on Saturday.
5. I like to ______ in the pool.
