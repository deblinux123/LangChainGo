# Chapter 28: Daily Activities and Routines

## Learning Objectives

By the end of this chapter, you can:
- Describe your daily routine.
- Name common everyday activities.
- Talk about when you do different things.

## 1. Morning Activities

| Phrase | Meaning |
|---|---|
| wake up | Stop sleeping |
| get up | Leave your bed |
| make the bed | Arrange the bed after sleeping |
| take a shower | Wash your body under a shower |
| brush your teeth | Clean your teeth |
| wash your face | Clean your face |
| get dressed | Put on clothes |
| comb your hair | Make your hair neat |
| eat breakfast | Have the first meal of the day |

## 2. Activities During the Day

| Phrase | Meaning |
|---|---|
| go to work | Travel to your workplace |
| go to school | Travel to your school |
| study English | Learn the English language |
| have lunch | Eat the midday meal |
| work | Do your job |
| read a book | Look at and understand written words |
| exercise | Do physical activity |
| go shopping | Buy things |
| meet friends | Spend time with friends |

## 3. Evening Activities

| Phrase | Meaning |
|---|---|
| go home | Return to your home |
| cook dinner | Prepare the evening meal |
| eat dinner | Have the evening meal |
| watch TV | Watch television |
| listen to music | Hear music |
| use a computer | Work or spend time on a computer |
| relax | Rest and feel comfortable |
| go to bed | Get into bed |
| fall asleep | Begin sleeping |

## 4. Time Expressions

| Expression | Example |
|---|---|
| every day | I exercise every day. |
| in the morning | I drink coffee in the morning. |
| in the afternoon | I study in the afternoon. |
| in the evening | I watch TV in the evening. |
| at night | I sleep at night. |
| on weekdays | I work on weekdays. |
| on weekends | I visit my family on weekends. |
| usually | I usually wake up at seven. |
| sometimes | I sometimes cook dinner. |
| never | I never drink coffee at night. |

## 5. Example Sentences

- I wake up at seven o'clock.
- She brushes her teeth every morning.
- He goes to work by bus.
- We have lunch at noon.
- They study English in the evening.
- I usually go to bed at eleven.
- My brother sometimes exercises after work.

## 6. Short Dialogue

**Alex:** What time do you wake up?

**Sara:** I wake up at seven.

**Alex:** What do you do in the morning?

**Sara:** I take a shower, eat breakfast, and go to work.

**Alex:** What do you do in the evening?

**Sara:** I cook dinner and read a book.

## 7. Useful Expressions

- I wake up early.
- I go to work at eight.
- I have lunch at noon.
- I come home in the evening.
- I usually exercise after work.
- I go to bed late.
- I relax on weekends.

## 8. Exercises

### Exercise A: Choose the Correct Answer

1. You ______ when you stop sleeping.
   - a) wake up
   - b) go to bed
   - c) fall asleep

2. You ______ your teeth with a toothbrush.
   - a) cook
   - b) brush
   - c) wear

3. You eat your first meal in the ______.
   - a) morning
   - b) evening
   - c) night

4. You ______ when you want to rest.
   - a) relax
   - b) drive
   - c) shout

### Exercise B: Put the Activities in a Logical Order

Write numbers 1–5.

- ___ Eat breakfast.
- ___ Wake up.
- ___ Go to work or school.
- ___ Brush your teeth.
- ___ Get dressed.

### Exercise C: Fill in the Blanks

Use: `usually`, `evening`, `lunch`, `bed`, `exercise`

1. I eat ______ at noon.
2. I go to ______ at eleven o'clock.
3. I ______ wake up at seven.
4. I watch TV in the ______.
5. I ______ three times a week.

### Exercise D: Speaking Practice

Describe your daily routine. Include:
- What time you wake up.
- What you do in the morning.
- What you do during the day.
- What you do in the evening.
- What time you go to bed.
