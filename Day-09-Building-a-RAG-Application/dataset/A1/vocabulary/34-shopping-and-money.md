# Chapter 34: Shopping and Money

## Learning Objectives

By the end of this chapter, you can:
- Name common shopping places and items.
- Ask about prices.
- Use simple money and shopping expressions.

## 1. Shopping Places

| Word | Meaning |
|---|---|
| store | A place where people buy things |
| supermarket | A large food store |
| mall | A large shopping center |
| market | A place where people buy and sell things |
| bookstore | A store that sells books |
| pharmacy | A store that sells medicine |
| bakery | A store that sells bread and cakes |
| clothing store | A store that sells clothes |

## 2. Money and Prices

| Word | Meaning |
|---|---|
| money | What people use to buy things |
| cash | Money in coins and bills |
| coin | A small piece of metal money |
| bill | Paper money |
| price | How much something costs |
| dollar | A unit of money |
| cent | One hundredth of a dollar |
| card | A payment card |
| change | Money returned after payment |
| wallet | A small item for carrying money |

## 3. Shopping Words

| Word | Meaning |
|---|---|
| customer | A person who buys something |
| cashier | A person who takes payment |
| receipt | A paper showing what you bought |
| basket | A container for shopping |
| cart | A larger container with wheels |
| size | How big or small something is |
| sale | A time when prices are lower |
| discount | A lower price |
| product | Something that is made or sold |
| brand | The name of a product maker |

## 4. Shopping Verbs

| Verb | Example |
|---|---|
| buy | I buy a shirt. |
| sell | They sell fresh fruit. |
| pay | I pay by card. |
| cost | How much does it cost? |
| choose | She chooses a dress. |
| try on | I try on the shoes. |
| need | I need some milk. |
| want | I want a new bag. |

## 5. Useful Shopping Expressions

- How much is this?
- How much are these?
- Can I pay by card?
- Do you have a smaller size?
- I am just looking, thanks.
- Can I try this on?
- Where is the cashier?
- Can I have a receipt?
- It is too expensive.
- That is a good price.

## 6. Example Sentences

1. I need to buy some bread.
2. This shirt costs twenty dollars.
3. Can I pay in cash?
4. The store has a big sale.
5. She buys vegetables at the supermarket.
6. I need a shopping cart.
7. The cashier gives me a receipt.

## 7. Short Dialogue

**Customer:** Excuse me. How much is this shirt?

**Cashier:** It is thirty dollars.

**Customer:** Do you have a smaller size?

**Cashier:** Yes, here you are.

**Customer:** Great. Can I pay by card?

**Cashier:** Of course.

## 8. Exercises

### Exercise A: Choose the Correct Answer

1. You buy bread at a:
   - a) bakery
   - b) pharmacy
   - c) bookstore

2. A person who takes payment is a:
   - a) teacher
   - b) cashier
   - c) doctor

3. A paper showing what you bought is a:
   - a) receipt
   - b) wallet
   - c) basket

### Exercise B: Fill in the Blanks

Use: `price – cash – receipt – supermarket`

1. I buy food at the ______.
2. What is the ______ of this bag?
3. I pay with ______.
4. Please give me the ______.

### Exercise C: Match the Expressions

1. How much is it?
2. Can I try it on?
3. Can I pay by card?

a. Asking to use a payment card

b. Asking about the price

c. Asking to wear something before buying

### Exercise D: Speaking Practice

1. Where do you usually shop?
2. Do you prefer cash or cards?
3. What do you buy every week?
4. What is expensive in your city?
