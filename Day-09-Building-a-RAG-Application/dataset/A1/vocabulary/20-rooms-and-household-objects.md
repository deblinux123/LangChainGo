# Chapter 20: Rooms and Household Objects

## Learning Objectives

By the end of this chapter, you can:
- Name common rooms in a home.
- Identify objects found in different rooms.
- Describe simple household activities.

## 1. Rooms in a Home

| Word | Meaning |
|---|---|
| living room | A room where people relax and spend time together |
| bedroom | A room where people sleep |
| kitchen | A room where people prepare food |
| bathroom | A room with a toilet and usually a sink |
| dining room | A room where people eat meals |
| hallway | A passage that connects rooms |
| garage | A place where people keep a car |
| balcony | A small outdoor area connected to a building |
| basement | A room or area below ground level |
| laundry room | A room where people wash and dry clothes |

## 2. Objects in Different Rooms

| Room | Objects |
|---|---|
| Living room | sofa, television, coffee table, lamp |
| Bedroom | bed, pillow, blanket, wardrobe |
| Kitchen | stove, refrigerator, sink, microwave |
| Bathroom | shower, bathtub, toilet, towel, mirror |
| Dining room | dining table, chair, plates, glasses |
| Laundry room | washing machine, dryer, basket |

## 3. Household Activities

| Phrase | Example |
|---|---|
| make the bed | I make my bed every morning. |
| wash the dishes | She washes the dishes after dinner. |
| do the laundry | We do the laundry on Saturday. |
| clean the floor | He cleans the floor. |
| take a shower | I take a shower every morning. |
| cook dinner | My father cooks dinner. |
| watch television | We watch television in the living room. |
| set the table | Please set the table for dinner. |

## 4. Example Sentences

- I sleep in the bedroom.
- We cook food in the kitchen.
- There is a television in the living room.
- My towels are in the bathroom.
- The washing machine is in the laundry room.
- We eat dinner in the dining room.

## 5. Short Dialogue

**Mia:** Where is the bathroom?

**Leo:** It is next to the bedroom.

**Mia:** Thank you. Where is your washing machine?

**Leo:** It is in the laundry room.

**Mia:** Your home is very nice!

**Leo:** Thank you!

## 6. Useful Expressions

- Where is the kitchen?
- The bathroom is upstairs.
- The television is in the living room.
- I clean my bedroom every week.
- We eat in the dining room.
- The laundry room is downstairs.
