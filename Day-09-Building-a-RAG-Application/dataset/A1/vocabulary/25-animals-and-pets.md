# Chapter 25: Animals and Pets

## Learning Objectives

By the end of this chapter, you can:
- Name common animals.
- Talk about pets.
- Describe animals using simple adjectives.

## 1. Common Pets

| Word | Meaning |
|---|---|
| dog | A common pet that can bark |
| cat | A small pet that can meow |
| rabbit | A small animal with long ears |
| hamster | A small animal often kept in a cage |
| fish | An animal that lives in water |
| bird | An animal with feathers and wings |
| parrot | A colorful bird that can copy sounds |
| turtle | A slow animal with a hard shell |
| guinea pig | A small animal often kept as a pet |

## 2. Farm Animals

| Word | Meaning |
|---|---|
| cow | A farm animal that gives milk |
| horse | A large animal that people can ride |
| sheep | A farm animal that gives wool |
| goat | A farm animal that can climb |
| pig | A farm animal with a round body |
| chicken | A farm bird that lays eggs |
| duck | A bird that often swims |
| donkey | An animal similar to a small horse |

## 3. Wild Animals

| Word | Meaning |
|---|---|
| lion | A large wild cat |
| tiger | A large wild cat with stripes |
| elephant | A very large animal with a trunk |
| giraffe | A tall animal with a long neck |
| monkey | An animal that can climb trees |
| bear | A large, strong wild animal |
| wolf | A wild animal similar to a dog |
| fox | A small wild animal with a long tail |
| zebra | An animal with black and white stripes |
| snake | A long animal with no legs |

## 4. Animal Body Parts and Sounds

| Word | Meaning |
|---|---|
| tail | The back part of some animals |
| wing | A body part used by birds to fly |
| feather | A soft covering on a bird |
| fur | Thick hair on an animal |
| paw | An animal's foot |
| beak | A bird's hard mouth |
| trunk | The long nose of an elephant |
| bark | The sound a dog makes |
| meow | The sound a cat makes |
| roar | A loud sound made by animals such as lions |

## 5. Animal Adjectives

| Word | Meaning |
|---|---|
| big | Large in size |
| small | Little in size |
| fast | Moving quickly |
| slow | Moving without speed |
| strong | Having a lot of physical power |
| dangerous | Able to cause harm |
| friendly | Kind and pleasant |
| cute | Attractive or sweet-looking |
| wild | Living naturally without being kept as a pet |
| domestic | Living with or near people |

## 6. Example Sentences

- My dog is very friendly.
- Cats have four legs and a tail.
- Elephants are very big.
- A giraffe has a long neck.
- Birds have wings and feathers.
- The lion can roar.
- My rabbit is small and cute.

## 7. Short Dialogue

**Mia:** Do you have a pet?

**Tom:** Yes, I have a dog.

**Mia:** What is its name?

**Tom:** Its name is Max.

**Mia:** Is it friendly?

**Tom:** Yes. It is very friendly and playful.

## 8. Exercises

### Exercise A: Choose the Correct Answer

1. Which animal can bark?
   - a) Dog
   - b) Fish
   - c) Rabbit

2. Which animal has a long trunk?
   - a) Tiger
   - b) Elephant
   - c) Horse

3. Which animal has black and white stripes?
   - a) Zebra
   - b) Bear
   - c) Fox

4. Which animal gives us wool?
   - a) Sheep
   - b) Chicken
   - c) Duck
