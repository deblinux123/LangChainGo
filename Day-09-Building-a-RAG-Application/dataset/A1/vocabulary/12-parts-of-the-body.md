# Chapter 12: Parts of the Body

## Learning Objectives

By the end of this chapter, you can:
- Name common parts of the body.
- Describe simple physical features.
- Understand basic sentences about the body.

## 1. Head and Face

| Word | Meaning |
|---|---|
| head | The top part of the body |
| hair | The material that grows on your head |
| face | The front part of your head |
| eye | You use it to see |
| ear | You use it to hear |
| nose | You use it to smell |
| mouth | You use it to eat and speak |
| tooth | One of the hard parts in your mouth |
| teeth | Plural of tooth |
| lip | One of the two soft parts around your mouth |
| neck | The part between your head and shoulders |

## 2. Upper Body

| Word | Meaning |
|---|---|
| shoulder | The part between your neck and arm |
| arm | The upper limb of your body |
| elbow | The middle joint of your arm |
| hand | The end of your arm |
| finger | One of the five parts of your hand |
| thumb | The short, strong finger |
| chest | The front upper part of your body |
| back | The rear part of your body |
| stomach | The front part of your body below your chest |

## 3. Lower Body

| Word | Meaning |
|---|---|
| leg | The lower limb of your body |
| knee | The middle joint of your leg |
| foot | The end of your leg |
| feet | Plural of foot |
| toe | One of the five parts of your foot |
| ankle | The joint between your foot and leg |
| hip | The part at the side of your body above your leg |

## 4. Example Sentences

- I have two eyes.
- She has long hair.
- My hands are cold.
- He has a small nose.
- My feet hurt.
- I wash my face every morning.
- My brother has brown eyes.

## 5. Useful Expressions

| Expression | Example |
|---|---|
| Wash your hands | Wash your hands before dinner. |
| Brush your teeth | I brush my teeth twice a day. |
| Comb your hair | She combs her hair every morning. |
| Touch your nose | Touch your nose with one finger. |
| Raise your hand | Please raise your hand. |

## 6. Short Dialogue

**Doctor:** What is the problem?

**Patient:** My head hurts.

**Doctor:** Do you have a fever?

**Patient:** No, I don't.

**Doctor:** Please open your mouth.

**Patient:** Okay.

## 7. Exercises

### Exercise A: Choose the Correct Answer

1. You use your ______ to see.
   - a) ears
   - b) eyes
   - c) hands

2. You have five ______ on each hand.
   - a) fingers
   - b) toes
   - c) knees

3. The plural of "foot" is ______.
   - a) foots
   - b) feet
   - c) feets

4. You brush your ______.
   - a) teeth
   - b) elbows
   - c) shoulders

### Exercise B: Fill in the Blanks

Use: `nose`, `hands`, `knees`, `hair`, `mouth`

1. I wash my ______ before eating.
2. She has long ______.
3. Your ______ is between your eyes.
4. Open your ______, please.
5. My ______ hurt after running.

### Exercise C: True or False

1. We use our ears to hear.
2. We have ten fingers on one hand.
3. The neck is between the head and shoulders.
4. We use our feet to write.

You learned common words for the head, face, upper body, and lower body. Practice using these words in simple sentences about yourself and other people.
