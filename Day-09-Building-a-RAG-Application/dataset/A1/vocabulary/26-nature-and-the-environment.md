# Chapter 26: Nature and the Environment

## Learning Objectives

By the end of this chapter, you can:
- Name common things in nature.
- Describe simple natural places.
- Talk about basic environmental actions.

## 1. Natural Places

| Word | Meaning |
|---|---|
| nature | The world of plants, animals, and natural places |
| forest | A large area with many trees |
| mountain | A very high area of land |
| hill | An area of land that is higher than the land around it |
| river | A large natural flow of water |
| lake | A large area of water surrounded by land |
| ocean | A very large area of salt water |
| sea | A large area of salt water |
| beach | An area of sand or small stones beside water |
| island | Land surrounded by water |
| desert | A very dry area with little rain |
| waterfall | Water that falls from a high place |
| field | An open area of land |
| cave | A large natural hole in a hill or mountain |

## 2. Nature Vocabulary

| Word | Meaning |
|---|---|
| tree | A tall plant with a trunk |
| flower | The colorful part of some plants |
| grass | A short green plant |
| leaf | A flat green part of a plant |
| branch | A part of a tree that grows from the trunk |
| root | The part of a plant under the ground |
| rock | A hard natural material |
| stone | A small piece of rock |
| soil | The top layer of earth where plants grow |
| air | The mixture of gases we breathe |
| water | A liquid essential for life |
| sky | The space above the earth |

## 3. Environmental Words

| Word | Meaning |
|---|---|
| environment | The natural world around us |
| pollution | Harmful materials in air, water, or land |
| trash | Things people throw away |
| recycling | Using materials again |
| plastic | A material used to make many products |
| clean | Free from dirt or pollution |
| dirty | Not clean |
| protect | Keep something safe |
| save | Keep something from being wasted |
| plant | Put a seed or young plant in the ground |

## 4. Environmental Actions

| Phrase | Example |
|---|---|
| plant a tree | We plant a tree in the garden. |
| recycle paper | I recycle paper at home. |
| save water | Turn off the tap to save water. |
| pick up trash | We pick up trash in the park. |
| protect nature | We should protect nature. |
| keep the park clean | Please keep the park clean. |

## 5. Example Sentences

- There are many trees in the forest.
- The river is long and clean.
- We can see mountains from our house.
- The beach is beautiful.
- I like walking in nature.
- We should recycle plastic and paper.
- Do not throw trash on the ground.
- Trees help keep the air clean.

## 6. Short Dialogue

**Alex:** Do you like going to the mountains?

**Sara:** Yes, I love nature.

**Alex:** What do you do there?

**Sara:** I walk, take pictures, and enjoy the fresh air.

**Alex:** Do you recycle at home?

**Sara:** Yes, I recycle paper and plastic.

## 7. Useful Expressions

- I love nature.
- The forest is beautiful.
- Let's plant a tree.
- Please don't throw trash here.
- We should save water.
- Keep the environment clean.
- Recycle paper and plastic.

## 8. Exercises

### Exercise A: Choose the Correct Answer

1. A large area with many trees is a ______.
   - a) forest
   - b) desert
   - c) beach

2. A natural flow of water is a ______.
   - a) mountain
   - b) river
   - c) field

3. A very dry place is a ______.
   - a) lake
   - b) ocean
   - c) desert

4. Using materials again is called ______.
   - a) recycling
   - b) pollution
   - c) wasting

### Exercise B: Fill in the Blanks

Use: `tree`, `trash`, `ocean`, `flowers`, `water`

1. We drink ______ every day.
2. Please put the ______ in the bin.
3. A ______ has a trunk and branches.
4. The ______ is very large and salty.
5. The garden has many beautiful ______.

### Exercise C: True or False

1. A lake is surrounded by land.
2. A desert usually has a lot of rain.
3. Recycling helps reduce waste.
4. Trees are part of nature.

### Exercise D: Speaking Practice

Answer these questions:

1. What natural places are near your city?
2. Do you like forests or beaches?
3. What can people do to protect nature?
4. Do you recycle at home?

**Exercise B:** 1. water, 2. trash, 3. tree, 4. ocean, 5. flowers
