# Chapter 08: Jobs and Occupations

## 1. Learning Objectives

- Name common jobs.
- Describe what people do at work.
- Ask about someone's occupation.
- Use "a" and "an" before job titles.
- Talk about workplaces.

|---|---|

## 5. Asking About Jobs

| Question | Answer |
|---|---|
| What do you do? | I am a teacher. |
| What is your job? | I am an engineer. |
| Where do you work? | I work in a hospital. |
| Is she a doctor? | Yes, she is. |
| What does he do? | He is a driver. |

## 6. A and An with Jobs

Use "a" before a consonant sound:

- a teacher
- a doctor
- a nurse
- a pilot

Use "an" before a vowel sound:

- an engineer
- an artist
- an actor
- an office worker

Examples:

- I am a student.
- She is an engineer.
- He is a doctor.

## 7. Examples in Context

1. My mother is a nurse.
2. My brother is an engineer.
3. A chef works in a restaurant.
4. My uncle is a police officer.
5. I am a student.
6. My friend works as a programmer.

## 8. Short Conversation

**Lisa:** What do you do?

**John:** I am a teacher.

**Lisa:** Where do you work?

**John:** I work at a school.

**Lisa:** Do you like your job?

**John:** Yes, I do. I like teaching children.
