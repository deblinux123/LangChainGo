# Chapter 13: Health and Common Illnesses

## Learning Objectives

By the end of this chapter, you can:
- Name common illnesses and symptoms.
- Say how you feel.
- Use basic expressions at a doctor's office.

## 1. Common Health Words

| Word | Meaning |
|---|---|
| health | The condition of your body and mind |
| doctor | A person who helps sick people |
| nurse | A person who cares for patients |
| hospital | A place where sick people receive medical care |
| clinic | A place where people see a doctor |
| medicine | Something used to treat an illness |
| patient | A person receiving medical care |
| appointment | An arranged time to see someone |
| pharmacy | A place where you can buy medicine |

## 2. Common Illnesses and Symptoms

| Word or Phrase | Meaning |
|---|---|
| headache | Pain in your head |
| stomachache | Pain in your stomach |
| toothache | Pain in a tooth |
| backache | Pain in your back |
| cold | A common illness affecting your nose and throat |
| cough | A sudden sound from your throat |
| fever | A high body temperature |
| sore throat | Pain in your throat |
| runny nose | A nose that produces a lot of liquid |
| tired | Needing rest or sleep |
| sick | Not feeling well |

## 3. Health Verbs

| Verb | Example |
|---|---|
| feel | I feel sick. |
| hurt | My back hurts. |
| cough | He coughs a lot. |
| rest | You should rest. |
| sleep | I need to sleep. |
| drink | Drink some water. |
| take | Take your medicine. |
| visit | I visit my doctor. |

## 4. Useful Expressions

- I feel sick.
- I have a headache.
- My stomach hurts.
- I have a cold.
- I need to see a doctor.
- Where is the nearest pharmacy?
- I feel better today.
- I don't feel well.

## 5. Example Sentences

- She has a bad cough.
- My brother has a fever.
- I need some medicine.
- He is at the hospital.
- We have a doctor's appointment today.
- I feel tired because I did not sleep well.

## 6. Short Dialogue

**Receptionist:** Good morning. Do you have an appointment?

**Patient:** Yes. My name is Alex.

**Receptionist:** What is the problem?

**Patient:** I have a sore throat and a cough.

**Receptionist:** Please take a seat.
