# Chapter 04: Days, Months, and Seasons

## 1. Learning Objectives

- Name the seven days of the week.
- Name the twelve months of the year.
- Identify the four seasons.
- Talk about birthdays and dates.
- Describe simple activities during different seasons.

## 2. Days of the Week

| Day | Short Form |
|---|---|
| Monday | Mon |
| Tuesday | Tue |
| Wednesday | Wed |
| Thursday | Thu |
| Friday | Fri |
| Saturday | Sat |
| Sunday | Sun |

Examples:

- I go to work on Monday.
- We visit our grandparents on Sunday.
- Friday is my favorite day.
- I study English every Tuesday.

**Remember:** Days of the week always begin with a capital letter.

## 3. Months of the Year

| Month | Short Form |
|---|---|
| January | Jan |
| February | Feb |
| March | Mar |
| April | Apr |
| May | May |
| June | Jun |
| July | Jul |
| August | Aug |
| September | Sep |
| October | Oct |
| November | Nov |
| December | Dec |

Examples:

- My birthday is in March.
- School starts in September.
- Christmas is in December.
- It is very cold in January in many places.

## 4. The Four Seasons

| Season | Common Weather | Example |
|---|---|---|
| spring | warm and rainy | Flowers grow in spring. |
| summer | hot and sunny | I swim in summer. |
| fall | cool and windy | Leaves fall in fall. |
| winter | cold and snowy | It sometimes snows in winter. |

**Note:** "Fall" is commonly used in American English. "Autumn" is also correct.

## 5. Talking About Time Periods

| Expression | Example |
|---|---|
| today | Today is Monday. |
| tomorrow | Tomorrow is Tuesday. |
| yesterday | Yesterday was Sunday. |
| this week | I am busy this week. |
| next week | I have a test next week. |
| last week | I visited my friend last week. |
| this month | I have a birthday this month. |
| next year | I want to travel next year. |

## 6. Useful Prepositions

- Use **on** with days: on Monday.
- Use **in** with months: in July.
- Use **in** with seasons: in winter.
- Use **in** with years: in 2026.

Examples:

- My class is on Wednesday.
- My birthday is in May.
- We go camping in summer.

## 7. Examples in Context

1. I work from Monday to Friday.
2. Saturday and Sunday are the weekend.
3. My sister's birthday is in August.
4. Winter is cold in my city.
5. We go to the beach in summer.
6. Spring is a beautiful season.

## 8. Short Conversation

**Emma:** What is your favorite season?

**Jack:** I like summer.

**Emma:** Why?

**Jack:** Because the weather is warm. I can swim.

**Emma:** When is your birthday?

**Jack:** It is in July.
