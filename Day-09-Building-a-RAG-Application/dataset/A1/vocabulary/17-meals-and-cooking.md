# Chapter 17: Meals and Cooking

## Learning Objectives

By the end of this chapter, you can:
- Name the main meals of the day.
- Talk about simple cooking activities.
- Understand basic kitchen vocabulary.

## 1. Meals of the Day

| Word | Meaning |
|---|---|
| breakfast | The first meal of the day |
| lunch | A meal eaten around the middle of the day |
| dinner | The main evening meal |
| snack | A small amount of food between meals |
| dessert | Sweet food eaten after a meal |

## 2. Cooking Verbs

| Verb | Meaning |
|---|---|
| cook | Prepare food using heat |
| bake | Cook food in an oven |
| boil | Cook food in hot water |
| fry | Cook food in hot oil |
| grill | Cook food on a grill |
| cut | Divide something with a knife |
| wash | Clean with water |
| mix | Put things together |
| add | Put something into a mixture |
| serve | Give food to someone |
| eat | Put food in your mouth |
| drink | Take liquid into your mouth |

## 3. Kitchen Vocabulary

| Word | Meaning |
|---|---|
| kitchen | A room where people prepare food |
| stove | Equipment used for cooking |
| oven | Equipment used for baking |
| refrigerator | Equipment that keeps food cold |
| microwave | Equipment that heats food quickly |
| pan | A flat cooking container |
| pot | A deep cooking container |
| plate | A flat dish for food |
| bowl | A deep round dish |
| cup | A small container for drinks |
| glass | A container often used for cold drinks |
| spoon | A tool used for eating or serving |
| fork | A tool with points used for eating |
| knife | A tool used for cutting |
| recipe | Instructions for preparing food |

## 4. Common Cooking Instructions

- Wash the vegetables.
- Cut the tomatoes.
- Add some salt.
- Mix the ingredients.
- Boil the water.
- Fry the eggs.
- Put the food in the oven.
- Serve the meal.

## 5. Example Sentences

- I have eggs for breakfast.
- We cook dinner at home.
- She bakes a cake on Sunday.
- He cuts the vegetables with a knife.
- Please put the plates on the table.
- The soup is in the pot.

## 6. Short Dialogue

**Mia:** What are you cooking?

**Tom:** I'm making vegetable soup.

**Mia:** What do you need?

**Tom:** I need carrots, potatoes, and onions.

**Mia:** Can I help?

**Tom:** Yes. Please wash the vegetables.
