# Chapter 02: Numbers and Counting

## 1. Learning Objectives

By the end of this chapter, you will be able to:

- Count from zero to one hundred.
- Read and write basic numbers.
- Understand ordinal numbers.
- Ask and answer questions about age and quantity.
- Use numbers in everyday situations.

## 3. Ordinal Numbers

Ordinal numbers tell us the position or order of something.

| Number | Ordinal |
|---:|---|
| 1 | first |
| 2 | second |
| 3 | third |
| 4 | fourth |
| 5 | fifth |
| 6 | sixth |
| 7 | seventh |
| 8 | eighth |
| 9 | ninth |
| 10 | tenth |
| 11 | eleventh |
| 12 | twelfth |
| 20 | twentieth |
| 21 | twenty-first |
| 30 | thirtieth |

Examples:

- Today is my first day at school.
- She lives on the second floor.
- My birthday is on the fifth of May.

## 4. Numbers in Everyday Life

| Situation | Example |
|---|---|
| Age | I am twenty years old. |
| Phone number | My phone number is 555-1234. |
| Money | The book costs ten dollars. |
| Quantity | I have three apples. |
| Time | It is seven o'clock. |
| Address | I live at number 25. |

## 5. Asking About Numbers

| Question | Example Answer |
|---|---|
| How old are you? | I am twenty years old. |
| How many books do you have? | I have five books. |
| How much is it? | It is ten dollars. |
| What is your phone number? | It is 555-1234. |
| What number is this? | It is number fifteen. |

## 6. Examples in Context

1. I have two brothers and one sister.
2. There are twenty students in my class.
3. My father is fifty years old.
4. I need three pencils.
5. This is my first English lesson.
6. The bus arrives at eight o'clock.

## 7. Short Conversation

**Anna:** How old are you?

**Tom:** I am twenty-one.

**Anna:** How many brothers do you have?

**Tom:** I have two brothers.

**Anna:** What is your phone number?

**Tom:** It is 555-1234.

## 8. Exercises

### Exercise A: Multiple Choice

1. What is 15 in words?
   - a) Fifty
   - b) Fifteen
   - c) Five

2. What is 40 in words?
   - a) Fourteen
   - b) Forty
   - c) Four

3. What is the ordinal form of 1?
   - a) One
   - b) First
   - c) Once

4. What number comes after 29?
   - a) 28
   - b) 30
   - c) 39

5. Which number is written correctly?
   - a) Fourty
   - b) Fourteen
   - c) Fourty-one
