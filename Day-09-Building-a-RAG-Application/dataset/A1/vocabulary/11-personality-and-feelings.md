# Chapter 11: Personality and Feelings

## 1. Learning Objectives

- Name common feelings and emotions.
- Describe people's personalities.
- Ask how someone feels.
- Use adjectives to describe emotions.
- Talk about feelings in simple sentences.

## 2. Common Feelings

| Word | Example |
|---|---|
| happy | I am happy today. |
| sad | She is sad. |
| angry | He is angry. |
| tired | I am tired after work. |
| hungry | We are hungry. |
| thirsty | I am thirsty. |
| excited | The children are excited. |
| scared | The little boy is scared. |

## 5. Asking About Feelings

| Question | Answer |
|---|---|
| How are you? | I am fine, thank you. |
| How do you feel? | I feel happy. |
| Are you tired? | Yes, I am. |
| Is she angry? | No, she isn't. |
| Why are you sad? | I miss my friend. |
| Are they excited? | Yes, they are. |

## 6. Using Feelings in Sentences

Use "be" before many feeling adjectives:

- I am happy.
- She is nervous.
- They are excited.

You can also use "feel":

- I feel tired.
- He feels sad.
- We feel relaxed.

## 7. Examples in Context

1. I am happy because today is my birthday.
2. She is nervous before her English test.
3. My brother is funny and friendly.
4. The children are excited about the trip.
5. He is tired after a long day.
6. My teacher is kind and patient.

## 8. Short Conversation

**Anna:** Hi, Tom. How are you?

**Tom:** I am a little tired.

**Anna:** Why?

**Tom:** I worked all day.

**Anna:** You should rest.

**Tom:** Yes. Thank you!

## 11. Role-Play

**Situation:** Two friends meet after school.

Student A asks how Student B feels.

Student B describes a feeling and explains why.

Use these expressions:

- How are you?
- I feel...
- Why?
- Because...
- I am happy for you!
- I hope you feel better.
