# Chapter 10: Personal Appearance

## 1. Learning Objectives

- Describe people's appearance.
- Name parts of the face and body.
- Describe hair and eye color.
- Use simple appearance adjectives.
- Ask questions about how someone looks.

## 2. Height and Body Description

| Word | Example |
|---|---|
| tall | My brother is tall. |
| short | She is short. |
| medium-height | He is medium-height. |
| slim | My friend is slim. |
| thin | The dog is thin. |
| strong | He is strong. |
| young | The boy is young. |
| old | My grandfather is old. |

Use respectful and neutral descriptions when talking about other people.

## 3. Hair

| Word | Example |
|---|---|
| hair | Her hair is long. |
| long hair | She has long hair. |
| short hair | He has short hair. |
| curly hair | My sister has curly hair. |
| straight hair | She has straight hair. |
| black hair | He has black hair. |
| brown hair | I have brown hair. |
| blonde hair | She has blonde hair. |
| gray hair | My grandfather has gray hair. |
| bald | My uncle is bald. |

## 4. Face and Eyes

| Word | Example |
|---|---|
| face | She has a round face. |
| eyes | His eyes are blue. |

| mouth | He has a big smile. |
| ears | My ears are small. |
| eyebrows | Her eyebrows are dark. |
| beard | My father has a beard. |
| mustache | He has a mustache. |
| glasses | She wears glasses. |
| smile | He has a friendly smile. |

## 5. Colors for Appearance

| Expression | Example |
|---|---|
| blue eyes | She has blue eyes. |
| green eyes | He has green eyes. |
| brown eyes | My mother has brown eyes. |
| black hair | She has black hair. |
| blonde hair | He has blonde hair. |
| gray hair | My grandfather has gray hair. |

## 6. Useful Questions

| Question | Answer |
|---|---|
| What does he look like? | He is tall and has brown hair. |
| What color are her eyes? | They are green. |
| Does she have long hair? | Yes, she does. |
| Does he wear glasses? | No, he doesn't. |
| Is he tall? | Yes, he is. |

## 7. Examples in Context

1. My sister has long black hair.
2. My father wears glasses.
3. My friend is tall and slim.
4. The girl has blue eyes.
5. My grandfather has gray hair.
6. The boy has short curly hair.

## 8. Short Conversation

**Emma:** What does your brother look like?

**Jack:** He is tall and slim.

**Emma:** What color is his hair?

**Jack:** It is brown.

**Emma:** Does he wear glasses?

**Jack:** Yes, he does.

## 9. Exercises

### Exercise A: Multiple Choice

1. Which word describes hair that is not straight?
   - a) Curly
   - b) Tall
   - c) Strong

2. What do we use to see?
   - a) Ears
   - b) Eyes
   - c) Nose

3. Which expression is correct?
   - a) She has blue eyes.
   - b) She is blue eyes.
   - c) She have blue eyes.

4. What does "bald" mean?
   - a) Having no hair on the head
   - b) Having long hair
   - c) Having curly hair

5. Which word describes something worn to help people see?
   - a) Beard
   - b) Glasses
   - c) Smile

### Exercise B: Complete the Sentences

Use: **glasses - curly - tall - beard - brown**

1. My father has a ______.
2. My sister has ______ hair.
3. My friend is very ______.
4. I wear ______ to read.
5. Her eyes are ______.

### Exercise C: True or False

1. We see with our eyes.
2. Straight hair is the same as curly hair.
3. A beard is hair on the face.
4. "Tall" describes height.
5. Glasses are worn on the feet.

### Exercise D: Short Answers

1. What color is your hair?
2. What color are your eyes?
3. Do you wear glasses?
4. How would you describe your best friend?
