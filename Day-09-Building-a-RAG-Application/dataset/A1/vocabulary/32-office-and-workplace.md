# Chapter 32: Office and Workplace

## Learning Objectives

By the end of this chapter, you can:
- Name common places and objects in an office.
- Talk about simple jobs and work activities.
- Ask and answer basic questions about work.

## 1. Places at Work

| Word | Meaning |
|---|---|
| office | A place where people work |
| company | A business organization |
| factory | A place where products are made |
| store | A place where people buy things |
| meeting room | A room for meetings |
| reception | The front area of an office |
| kitchen | A place to prepare food |
| break room | A place to rest at work |

## 2. Office Objects

| Word | Meaning |
|---|---|
| desk | A table for working |
| chair | Something you sit on |
| computer | An electronic machine |
| keyboard | Used to type |
| mouse | Used to control a computer |
| printer | Prints documents |
| phone | Used to call people |
| notebook | A book for writing notes |
| pen | Used for writing |
| paper | Material for writing or printing |
| folder | Used to keep papers |
| calendar | Shows days and dates |

## 3. People at Work

| Word | Meaning |
|---|---|
| boss | The person in charge |
| manager | A person who manages a team |
| coworker | A person who works with you |
| employee | A person who works for a company |
| customer | A person who buys something |
| receptionist | A person who welcomes visitors |

## 4. Common Work Verbs

| Verb | Example |
|---|---|
| work | I work in an office. |
| type | She types an email. |
| print | He prints a document. |
| call | I call my manager. |
| write | We write reports. |
| meet | They meet every Monday. |
| help | I help my coworkers. |
| start | Work starts at 9:00. |
| finish | I finish work at 5:00. |

## 5. Example Sentences

1. I work in a small office.
2. My desk is near the window.
3. My manager is very friendly.
4. I use a computer every day.
5. We have a meeting at 10:00.
6. She prints the documents.
7. My coworkers help me.

## 6. Short Dialogue

**Alex:** Where do you work?

**Sam:** I work in an office.

**Alex:** What do you do?

**Sam:** I answer emails and help customers.

**Alex:** Do you like your job?

**Sam:** Yes, I do. My coworkers are friendly.

## 7. Useful Expressions

- I work in an office.
- I work from home.
- I start work at 8:00.
- I finish work at 4:00.
- I have a meeting.
- I am busy today.
- Can you help me?
- I need a pen.

## 8. Exercises

### Exercise A: Choose the Correct Answer

1. You sit on a:
   - a) printer
   - b) chair
   - c) folder

2. You use a keyboard to:
   - a) type
   - b) sleep
   - c) cook

3. A person who works with you is your:
   - a) customer
   - b) coworker
   - c) visitor

### Exercise B: Fill in the Blanks

Use: `desk – printer – manager – meeting`

1. My computer is on my ______.
2. The ______ prints documents.
3. Our ______ is in charge of the team.
4. We have a ______ at 11:00.

### Exercise C: True or False

1. A printer is used to print documents.
2. A coworker is a person who works with you.
3. A desk is usually used for sleeping.

### Exercise D: Speaking Practice

Answer these questions:

1. Where do you work?
2. What objects do you use at work?
3. What time do you start work?
4. Do you like working with other people?

## 9. Role-Play

- The office

- The meeting room
- Working hours
