# Chapter 09: Common Adjectives

## 1. Learning Objectives

- Recognize common adjectives.
- Describe people, places, and objects.
- Understand common opposite adjectives.
- Use adjectives in simple sentences.
- Describe things using "be" and "have."

## 2. Size and Shape Adjectives

| Adjective | Opposite | Example |
|---|---|---|
| big | small | My house is big. |
| tall | short | My brother is tall. |
| long | short | Her hair is long. |
| wide | narrow | This street is wide. |
| thick | thin | This book is thick. |
| heavy | light | My bag is heavy. |

## 3. Appearance Adjectives

| Adjective | Opposite | Example |
|---|---|---|
| beautiful | ugly | The garden is beautiful. |
| clean | dirty | My room is clean. |
| new | old | I have a new phone. |
| young | old | The child is young. |
| strong | weak | He is strong. |
| pretty | unattractive | She has a pretty dress. |

## 4. Feelings and Personality

| Adjective | Opposite | Example |
|---|---|---|
| happy | sad | I am happy today. |
| kind | unkind | My teacher is kind. |
| friendly | unfriendly | Our neighbor is friendly. |
| funny | serious | He is very funny. |
| busy | free | I am busy today. |
| tired | energetic | She is tired. |
| angry | calm | The man is angry. |
| nervous | relaxed | I am nervous before a test. |

## 5. Quality and Description

| Adjective | Opposite | Example |
|---|---|---|
| good | bad | This is a good book. |
| easy | difficult | English is fun and easy. |
| fast | slow | This car is fast. |
| hot | cold | The coffee is hot. |
| warm | cool | The weather is warm. |
| expensive | cheap | This phone is expensive. |
| interesting | boring | The movie is interesting. |
| important | unimportant | English is important to me. |

## 6. Using Adjectives

Adjectives describe nouns.

### Before a Noun

- a big house
- a small dog
- a beautiful flower
- an interesting book

### After the Verb "Be"

- The house is big.
- The dog is small.
- The flower is beautiful.
- The book is interesting.

**Remember:** In English, adjectives usually do not change for singular or plural nouns.

Examples:

- a small car
- two small cars

## 7. Examples in Context

1. My new computer is fast.
2. This is an expensive restaurant.
3. My sister is very friendly.
4. The weather is cold today.
5. The English lesson is interesting.
6. Our classroom is clean and bright.

## 8. Short Conversation

**Tom:** How is your new apartment?

**Anna:** It is very nice.

**Tom:** Is it big?

**Anna:** No, it is small, but it is beautiful.

**Tom:** Is it expensive?

**Anna:** No, it is quite cheap.
