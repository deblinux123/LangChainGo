# Chapter 39: Common Phrases and Everyday Expressions

## Learning Objectives

By the end of this chapter, you can:
- Use common greetings and polite expressions.
- Ask for help and information.
- Communicate in simple everyday situations.

## 1. Greetings

| Expression | Use |
|---|---|
| Hello! | A common greeting |
| Hi! | An informal greeting |
| Good morning! | Greeting in the morning |
| Good afternoon! | Greeting in the afternoon |
| Good evening! | Greeting in the evening |
| How are you? | Asking about someone's health or feelings |
| Nice to meet you. | Meeting someone for the first time |
| How is it going? | Informal way to ask how someone is |

## 2. Saying Goodbye

| Expression | Use |
|---|---|
| Goodbye! | A general farewell |
| Bye! | An informal farewell |
| See you later! | Saying you will meet again |
| See you tomorrow! | Saying you will meet the next day |
| Have a nice day! | A friendly farewell |
| Take care! | A friendly way to say goodbye |
| Good night! | Said before going to sleep or leaving late at night |

## 3. Polite Expressions

| Expression | Use |
|---|---|
| Please. | Making a polite request |
| Thank you. | Showing thanks |
| You're welcome. | Responding to thanks |
| Excuse me. | Getting attention politely |
| I'm sorry. | Apologizing |
| No problem. | Saying something is okay |
| That's okay. | Accepting an apology |
| Can you help me? | Asking for help |

## 4. Everyday Responses

| Expression | Use |
|---|---|
| Yes, of course. | Agreeing |
| Sure. | Agreeing informally |
| I don't know. | Saying you do not have the answer |
| I understand. | Saying you understand |
| I don't understand. | Saying you do not understand |
| Maybe. | Showing uncertainty |
| That's great! | Showing happiness |
| That's interesting. | Showing interest |
| Really? | Showing surprise |
| Me too. | Saying you feel the same |

## 5. Asking for Information

- What is your name?
- Where are you from?
- Where is the bathroom?
- What time is it?
- How much is this?
- Can you say that again?
- Can you speak slowly?
- How do I get to the station?
- What does this word mean?

## 6. At Different Places

### At a Restaurant

- A table for two, please.
- Can I see the menu?
- I would like some water.
- Can I have the bill, please?

### At a Store

- How much is this?
- Do you have another size?
- Can I pay by card?
- Thank you for your help.

### At School

- Can you help me?
- I have a question.
- I don't understand.
- Can you repeat that, please?

## 7. Short Dialogues

### Dialogue A: Meeting Someone

**Emma:** Hi! My name is Emma.

**Daniel:** Hello! I'm Daniel.

**Emma:** Nice to meet you.

**Daniel:** Nice to meet you, too.

### Dialogue B: Asking for Help

**Visitor:** Excuse me. Where is the train station?

**Local:** It is near the park.

**Visitor:** Thank you very much.

**Local:** You're welcome.

### Dialogue C: In Class

**Student:** Excuse me, can you repeat that?

**Teacher:** Of course.

**Student:** Thank you.

**Teacher:** No problem.

## 8. Exercises

### Exercise A: Choose the Correct Expression

1. Someone says "Thank you." You say:
   - a) You're welcome.
   - b) Good night.
   - c) Excuse me.

2. You meet someone for the first time. You say:
   - a) I'm sorry.
   - b) Nice to meet you.
   - c) See you tomorrow.

3. You need help. You say:
   - a) Can you help me?
   - b) Good evening.
   - c) You're welcome.

### Exercise C: True or False

1. "Good night" is commonly said before going to sleep.
