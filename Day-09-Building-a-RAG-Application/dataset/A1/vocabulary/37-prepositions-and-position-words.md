# Chapter 37: Prepositions and Position Words

## Learning Objectives

By the end of this chapter, you can:
- Describe where objects and people are.
- Talk about simple time expressions.
- Use common prepositions in sentences.

## 1. Prepositions of Place

| Preposition | Meaning | Example |
|---|---|---|
| in | Inside something | The keys are in the bag. |
| on | On a surface | The book is on the table. |
| under | Below something | The cat is under the chair. |
| next to | Beside something | I sit next to my friend. |
| between | In the middle of two things | The bank is between the stores. |
| behind | At the back of something | The car is behind the house. |
| in front of | Before something | The bus is in front of the school. |
| near | Not far from something | My home is near the park. |
| above | Higher than something | The picture is above the sofa. |
| below | Lower than something | The shoes are below the shelf. |

## 2. Prepositions of Movement

| Preposition | Example |
|---|---|
| to | I go to school. |
| from | She comes from Canada. |
| into | He walks into the room. |
| out of | She goes out of the house. |
| across | We walk across the street. |
| through | The train goes through the tunnel. |

## 3. Prepositions of Time

| Preposition | Use | Example |
|---|---|---|
| at | Specific time | at 7:00 |
| on | Days and dates | on Monday |
| in | Months, years, and parts of the day | in June, in 2026, in the morning |

More examples:

- I wake up at 6:30.
- We have a meeting on Friday.
- My birthday is in May.
- I study in the evening.

## 4. Other Useful Prepositions

| Preposition | Example |
|---|---|
| with | I live with my family. |
| without | I drink coffee without sugar. |
| for | This gift is for you. |
| about | We talk about music. |
| of | A cup of tea |
| by | I go to work by bus. |

## 5. Common Position Phrases

- in the room
- on the table
- under the bed
- next to the door
- behind the car
- in front of the house
- near the school
- at home
- at work
- at school

## 6. Example Sentences

1. My phone is on the desk.
2. The shoes are under the bed.
3. The bank is next to the supermarket.
4. I go to work at 8:00.
5. We have English class on Tuesday.
6. My birthday is in October.
7. She walks into the kitchen.

## 7. Short Dialogue

**Tom:** Where is my backpack?

**Lisa:** It is under the table.

**Tom:** Where are my keys?

**Lisa:** They are in your backpack.

**Tom:** Thank you!

## 8. Exercises

### Exercise A: Choose the Correct Preposition

1. The book is ______ the table.
   - a) on
   - b) at
   - c) from

2. The cat is ______ the bed.
   - a) under
   - b) into
   - c) by

3. I have a class ______ Monday.
   - a) in
   - b) on
   - c) at

4. My birthday is ______ July.
   - a) at
   - b) on
   - c) in

### Exercise B: Fill in the Blanks

Use: `next to – at – in – under`

1. The shoes are ______ the bed.
2. I wake up ______ 7:00.
3. My school is ______ the park.
4. My books are ______ my bag.

### Exercise C: True or False

1. We usually use "at" with a specific clock time.
2. We use "on" with days of the week.
3. We use "in" with the month of June.

### Exercise D: Speaking Practice

Describe your room using five prepositions.

Example: "My computer is on the desk."
