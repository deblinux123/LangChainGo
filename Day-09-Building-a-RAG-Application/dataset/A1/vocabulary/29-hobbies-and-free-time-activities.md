# Chapter 29: Hobbies and Free-Time Activities

## Learning Objectives

By the end of this chapter, you can:
- Name common hobbies.
- Talk about your free time.
- Say what you like and dislike doing.

## 1. Indoor Hobbies

| Activity | Meaning |
|---|---|
| reading | Looking at and understanding books |
| drawing | Making pictures with a pen or pencil |
| painting | Making pictures with paint |
| cooking | Preparing food |
| baking | Making food in an oven |
| playing chess | Playing a board game |
| playing video games | Playing electronic games |
| watching movies | Viewing films |
| listening to music | Enjoying songs or other music |
| writing | Creating written texts |
| knitting | Making clothes with yarn |
| doing puzzles | Putting pieces together to make a picture |

## 2. Outdoor Activities

| Activity | Meaning |
|---|---|
| walking | Moving on foot |
| hiking | Walking in nature, often in the mountains |
| cycling | Riding a bicycle |
| camping | Staying outdoors in a tent |
| fishing | Trying to catch fish |
| gardening | Growing plants and flowers |
| taking photographs | Making pictures with a camera |
| bird-watching | Watching birds in nature |
| traveling | Visiting different places |

## 3. Common Hobby Verbs

| Verb | Example |
|---|---|
| read | I read books. |
| draw | She draws animals. |
| paint | He paints pictures. |
| cook | We cook together. |
| play | They play chess. |
| watch | I watch movies. |
| listen | She listens to music. |
| collect | He collects stamps. |
| take | I take photographs. |
| enjoy | We enjoy hiking. |

## 4. Talking About Preferences

| Expression | Example |
|---|---|
| I like... | I like reading. |
| I love... | I love traveling. |
| I enjoy... | I enjoy cooking. |
| I don't like... | I don't like fishing. |
| My favorite hobby is... | My favorite hobby is drawing. |
| In my free time... | In my free time, I play chess. |

## 5. Example Sentences

- My favorite hobby is reading.
- She enjoys painting pictures.
- We play video games on weekends.
- He likes taking photographs.
- I sometimes go hiking with my friends.
- They love listening to music.
- I don't like camping.

## 6. Short Dialogue

**Mia:** What do you do in your free time?

**Alex:** I like reading and playing video games.

**Mia:** Do you enjoy sports?

**Alex:** Yes, I do. I sometimes go cycling.

**Mia:** That's great!

## 7. Useful Questions

- What are your hobbies?
- What do you do in your free time?
- Do you like reading?
- What kind of music do you enjoy?
- Do you prefer indoor or outdoor activities?
- How often do you go hiking?

## 8. Exercises

### Exercise A: Choose the Correct Answer

1. You use a pencil when you ______.
   - a) draw
   - b) swim
   - c) cook

2. You need a tent for ______.
   - a) camping
   - b) reading
   - c) painting

3. You use a camera when you ______.
   - a) take photographs
   - b) play chess
   - c) bake

4. You use a chessboard when you ______.
   - a) go hiking
   - b) play chess
   - c) listen to music

### Exercise B: Complete the Sentences

Use: `reading`, `hiking`, `cooking`, `photographs`, `music`

1. I enjoy listening to ______.
2. My father likes ______ dinner.
3. She loves taking ______.
4. We go ______ in the mountains.
5. He enjoys ______ books.

### Exercise C: True or False

1. Camping is an outdoor activity.
2. Painting is usually an indoor hobby.
3. You need a camera to play chess.
4. Gardening involves growing plants.

### Exercise D: Speaking Practice

Talk about your hobbies:
1. What is your favorite hobby?
2. When do you do it?
3. Do you do it alone or with friends?
4. Why do you enjoy it?

## 9. Answer Key

**Exercise A:** 1. a, 2. a, 3. a, 4. b

**Exercise B:** 1. music, 2. cooking, 3. photographs, 4. hiking, 5. reading

**Exercise C:** 1. True, 2. True, 3. False, 4. True

## 10. Chapter Summary

You learned words for indoor hobbies and outdoor activities. You can now describe your interests and talk about what you enjoy doing in your free time.
