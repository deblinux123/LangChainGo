# Chapter 40: A1 Vocabulary Final Review

## Learning Objectives

By the end of this chapter, you can:
- Review important A1 vocabulary topics.
- Use common words in everyday sentences.
- Understand simple conversations.
- Describe your life using basic English vocabulary.

## 1. Review: People and Family

| Word | Meaning |
|---|---|
| mother | Female parent |
| father | Male parent |
| brother | Male sibling |
| sister | Female sibling |
| friend | A person you like and know |
| teacher | A person who teaches |
| student | A person who studies |
| neighbor | A person who lives near you |

Example sentences:

- My mother is a teacher.
- I have one brother and two sisters.
- My best friend lives near me.

## 2. Review: Home and Everyday Objects

| Word | Word | Word |
|---|---|---|

| living room | sofa | television |

Example sentences:

## 12. Writing Assessment

Write a short paragraph of 60–80 words about yourself.

Include:
- Your name and country
- Your family
- Your home
- Your work or studies
- Your daily routine
- Your hobbies

### Example

"My name is Alex. I am from Canada. I live in a small apartment with my family. I work in an office. I wake up at seven every morning and eat breakfast at home. I like reading books and listening to music. On weekends, I play soccer with my friends."
