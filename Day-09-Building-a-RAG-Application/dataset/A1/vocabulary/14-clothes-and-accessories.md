# Chapter 14: Clothes and Accessories

## Learning Objectives

By the end of this chapter, you can:
- Name common clothes and accessories.
- Describe what people are wearing.
- Talk about simple clothing preferences.

## 1. Everyday Clothes

| Word | Meaning |
|---|---|
| shirt | Clothing for the upper body |
| T-shirt | A casual shirt with short sleeves |
| pants | Clothing that covers both legs |
| jeans | Strong cotton pants, usually blue |
| shorts | Short pants |
| dress | One-piece clothing, often worn by women |
| skirt | Clothing that hangs from the waist |
| sweater | Warm clothing for the upper body |
| jacket | Short outer clothing |
| coat | Warm outer clothing |
| suit | Formal matching clothes |
| uniform | Special clothes worn for work or school |

## 2. Shoes and Other Clothing

| Word | Meaning |
|---|---|
| shoes | Things you wear on your feet |
| sneakers | Comfortable sports or casual shoes |
| boots | Strong shoes that cover the ankles |
| socks | Clothing worn on the feet |
| sandals | Open shoes |
| hat | Something worn on the head |
| cap | A soft hat with a front part |
| scarf | A piece of clothing worn around the neck |
| gloves | Clothing for your hands |
| belt | A strip worn around the waist |

## 3. Accessories

| Word | Meaning |
|---|---|
| watch | Something worn on the wrist to show time |
| glasses | Things worn in front of the eyes |
| sunglasses | Glasses that protect your eyes from sunlight |
| bag | Something used to carry personal items |
| backpack | A bag carried on your back |
| wallet | Something used to carry money and cards |
| necklace | Jewelry worn around the neck |
| ring | Jewelry worn on a finger |

## 4. Clothing Verbs and Adjectives

| Word | Example |
|---|---|
| wear | I wear a blue shirt. |
| put on | Put on your jacket. |
| take off | Take off your shoes. |
| clean | My shoes are clean. |
| dirty | These pants are dirty. |
| comfortable | This sweater is comfortable. |
| tight | These shoes are too tight. |
| loose | My shirt is loose. |

## 5. Example Sentences

- I wear jeans every day.
- She has a beautiful red dress.
- He is wearing black sneakers.
- My jacket is very warm.
- I need a new pair of shoes.
- These gloves are too small.
- He wears glasses.

## 6. Short Dialogue

**Customer:** Excuse me. How much is this jacket?

**Assistant:** It is forty dollars.

**Customer:** Do you have it in blue?

**Assistant:** Yes, we do.

**Customer:** Can I try it on?

**Assistant:** Of course. The fitting room is over there.

## 7. Exercises

### Exercise A: Choose the Correct Answer

1. You wear ______ on your feet.
   - a) gloves
   - b) shoes
   - c) hats

2. You wear a ______ around your neck.
   - a) scarf
   - b) belt
   - c) watch

3. You use a ______ to carry books on your back.
   - a) wallet
   - b) backpack
   - c) ring

4. You wear ______ to protect your eyes from sunlight.
   - a) sunglasses
   - b) socks
   - c) boots

### Exercise B: Fill in the Blanks

Use: `shirt`, `gloves`, `jeans`, `watch`, `coat`

1. It is cold. Put on your ______.
2. I wear a blue ______ with black pants.
3. My hands are cold. I need ______.
4. He wears a ______ on his wrist.
5. She likes wearing ______ with sneakers.

### Exercise C: True or False

1. A belt is worn around the waist.
2. A wallet is worn on your feet.
3. A sweater can keep you warm.
4. Sunglasses protect your eyes from sunlight.

### Exercise D: Speaking Practice

Describe what you are wearing today. Include:
- Your shirt or top
- Your pants or skirt
- Your shoes
- One accessory

## 8. Answer Key

**Exercise A:** 1. b, 2. a, 3. b, 4. a

**Exercise B:** 1. coat, 2. shirt, 3. gloves, 4. watch, 5. jeans

**Exercise C:** 1. True, 2. False, 3. True, 4. True

## 9. Chapter Summary

You learned the names of everyday clothes, shoes, and accessories. You can now describe what someone is wearing and ask simple questions in a clothing store.
