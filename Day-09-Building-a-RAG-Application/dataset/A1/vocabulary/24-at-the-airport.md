# Chapter 24: At the Airport

## Learning Objectives

By the end of this chapter, you can:
- Identify common places and objects at an airport.
- Understand basic airport instructions.
- Ask simple questions about flights.

## 1. Airport Vocabulary

| Word | Meaning |
|---|---|
| airport | A place where airplanes arrive and leave |
| airplane | A vehicle that flies |
| flight | A journey by airplane |
| airline | A company that operates airplanes |
| pilot | A person who flies an airplane |
| flight attendant | A person who helps passengers on an airplane |
| passenger | A person traveling on an airplane |
| passport | An official document for international travel |
| boarding pass | A document that allows a passenger to board a plane |
| ticket | A document showing permission to travel |
| luggage | Bags used for travel |
| suitcase | A large travel bag |
| security | The airport area where passengers and bags are checked |
| gate | The place where passengers board an airplane |
| terminal | A main airport building |
| baggage claim | The area where passengers collect checked bags |

## 2. Airport Actions

| Phrase | Meaning |
|---|---|
| check in | Register for a flight |
| board a plane | Get onto an airplane |
| take off | Leave the ground |
| land | Come down to the ground |
| go through security | Have your bags and belongings checked |
| collect luggage | Get your bags after a flight |
| show your passport | Present your travel document |
| wait for a flight | Stay until your airplane is ready |

## 3. Useful Airport Questions

| Question | Example Answer |
|---|---|
| Where is the check-in desk? | It is over there. |
| What is your flight number? | It is flight 205. |
| Where is Gate 12? | It is on the second floor. |
| When does the plane leave? | It leaves at 10:30. |
| Where is baggage claim? | It is downstairs. |
| Can I see your passport? | Yes, here it is. |

## 4. Example Sentences

- I am at the airport.
- My flight is at eleven o'clock.
- Please show your passport.
- We need to go through security.
- Our plane is ready to board.
- I collect my suitcase at baggage claim.
- The airplane takes off at noon.

## 5. Short Dialogue

**Airport Agent:** Good morning. May I see your passport and ticket?

**Passenger:** Yes, here they are.

**Airport Agent:** Are you checking any bags?

**Passenger:** Yes, one suitcase.

**Airport Agent:** Here is your boarding pass. Your gate is 15.

**Passenger:** Thank you. Where is Gate 15?

**Airport Agent:** It is straight ahead.

## 6. Useful Expressions

- Where is the check-in desk?
- Here is my passport.
- I have one suitcase.
- What time is my flight?
- Where is my boarding gate?
- Is the flight on time?
- Where can I collect my luggage?
