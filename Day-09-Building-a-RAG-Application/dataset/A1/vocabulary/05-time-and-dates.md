# Chapter 05: Time and Dates

## 1. Learning Objectives

- Ask and tell the time.
- Use common time expressions.
- Read simple dates.
- Talk about daily schedules.
- Use basic time-related prepositions.

## 2. Basic Time Vocabulary

| Word | Example |
|---|---|
| clock | The clock is on the wall. |
| watch | My watch is new. |
| hour | There are sixty minutes in an hour. |
| minute | Wait one minute, please. |
| second | One minute has sixty seconds. |
| morning | I drink coffee in the morning. |
| afternoon | I study in the afternoon. |
| evening | I watch TV in the evening. |
| night | I sleep at night. |
| midnight | I go to bed before midnight. |
| noon | We eat lunch at noon. |

## 3. Asking About the Time

Question:

**What time is it?**

Common answers:

| Time | Expression |
|---|---|
| 7:00 | It's seven o'clock. |
| 7:15 | It's seven fifteen. |
| 7:30 | It's seven thirty. |
| 7:45 | It's seven forty-five. |
| 8:05 | It's eight oh five. |
| 9:20 | It's nine twenty. |
| 12:00 | It's twelve o'clock. |

You can also use:

- 7:15 = a quarter past seven.
- 7:30 = half past seven.
- 7:45 = a quarter to eight.

## 4. A.M. and P.M.

- A.M. refers to the time from midnight to before noon.
- P.M. refers to the time from noon to before midnight.

Examples:

- 8:00 A.M. = eight in the morning.
- 2:00 P.M. = two in the afternoon.
- 7:00 P.M. = seven in the evening.
- 12:00 P.M. = noon.
- 12:00 A.M. = midnight.

## 5. Parts of the Day

| Expression | Example |
|---|---|
| early morning | I wake up in the early morning. |
| in the morning | I eat breakfast in the morning. |
| in the afternoon | I work in the afternoon. |
| in the evening | I read in the evening. |
| at night | I sleep at night. |

## 6. Dates

Dates can be written in different ways.

Examples:

- May 5, 2026
- June 12, 2026
- October 31, 2026

In American English, the month usually comes before the day.

Examples:

- My birthday is on May 5.
- The meeting is on June 12.
- Today is November 14.

## 7. Time Prepositions

| Preposition | Use | Example |
|---|---|---|
| at | clock time | at 8:00 |
| on | days and dates | on Monday |
| in | months and years | in July |
| in | parts of the day | in the morning |

More examples:

- I wake up at seven.
- My class is on Friday.
- My birthday is in April.
- I study in the evening.

## 8. Examples in Context

1. I wake up at 6:30 A.M.
2. Breakfast is at seven o'clock.
3. I go to work at eight.
4. I have lunch at noon.
5. I finish work at five P.M.
6. I go to bed at ten at night.

## 9. Short Conversation

**Mike:** Excuse me. What time is it?

**Sarah:** It is three fifteen.

**Mike:** Thank you.

**Sarah:** You're welcome.

**Mike:** What time is your English class?

**Sarah:** It starts at four o'clock.
