# Chapter 01: The Alphabet and Basic Words

## 1. Learning Objectives

By the end of this chapter, you will be able to:

- Recognize the 26 letters of the English alphabet.
- Identify uppercase and lowercase letters.
- Spell simple English words.
- Understand common classroom and everyday words.
- Use basic words in simple sentences.

## 2. The English Alphabet

The English alphabet has 26 letters.

| Uppercase | Lowercase | Letter Name |
|---|---|---|
| A | a | A |
| B | b | Bee |
| C | c | Cee |
| D | d | Dee |
| E | e | E |
| F | f | Eff |
| G | g | Gee |
| H | h | Aitch |
| I | i | I |
| J | j | Jay |
| K | k | Kay |
| L | l | Ell |
| M | m | Em |
| N | n | En |
| O | o | O |
| P | p | Pee |
| Q | q | Cue |
| R | r | Ar |
| S | s | Ess |
| T | t | Tee |
| U | u | U |
| V | v | Vee |
| W | w | Double-U |
| X | x | Ex |
| Y | y | Why |
| Z | z | Zee |

**Note:** In American English, the letter Z is usually called "Zee."

### Uppercase and Lowercase

- Uppercase letters: A, B, C, D
- Lowercase letters: a, b, c, d

Examples:

- My name starts with F.
- The word "book" starts with b.
- My name is Farid.

## 3. Vowels and Consonants

There are five main vowel letters in English:

**A, E, I, O, U**

The other 21 letters are consonants.

Examples:

| Vowels | Consonants |
|---|---|
| apple | book |
| egg | cat |
| ink | dog |
| orange | fish |
| umbrella | girl |

The letter Y can also represent a vowel sound in some words, such as "happy."

## 6. Spelling Simple Words

Spelling means saying or writing the letters of a word in the correct order.

| Word | Spelling |
|---|---|
| cat | C - A - T |
| dog | D - O - G |
| pen | P - E - N |
| book | B - O - O - K |
| fish | F - I - S - H |
| name | N - A - M - E |
| home | H - O - M - E |
| apple | A - P - P - L - E |

### Example Conversation

**A:** What is your name?

**B:** My name is Alex.

**A:** How do you spell Alex?

**B:** A - L - E - X.

**A:** Nice to meet you!

**B:** Nice to meet you, too!

## 12. Role-Play Activities

### Role-Play 1: Meeting a New Student

**Student A:** Say hello and introduce yourself.

**Student B:** Say hello and tell your name.

Continue the conversation by asking how to spell each other's names.

### Role-Play 2: In the Classroom

**Student A:** Ask for a pencil.

**Student B:** Give the pencil and respond politely.

Use these expressions:

- Can I have a pencil, please?
- Here you are.
- Thank you.
- You're welcome.
