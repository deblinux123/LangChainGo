# Chapter 15: Food and Drinks

## Learning Objectives

By the end of this chapter, you can:
- Name common foods and drinks.
- Talk about what you like and dislike.
- Ask for food and drinks.

## 1. Basic Food

| Word | Meaning |
|---|---|
| bread | Food made from flour and water |
| rice | Small grains used as food |
| pasta | Food made from flour and water, often in different shapes |
| cheese | Food made from milk |
| egg | Food produced by birds |
| meat | Food from animals |
| chicken | Meat from a chicken |
| fish | An animal that lives in water; also food |
| soup | A liquid food |
| salad | A mixture of vegetables or other ingredients |
| sandwich | Food between two pieces of bread |
| pizza | A flat baked dish with toppings |

## 2. Dairy and Other Foods

| Word | Meaning |
|---|---|
| milk | A white drink from animals such as cows |
| yogurt | A soft food made from milk |
| butter | A soft food made from milk |
| honey | A sweet food made by bees |
| sugar | A sweet substance |
| salt | A white substance used to add flavor |
| cereal | A common breakfast food |
| beans | Seeds used as food |

## 3. Drinks

| Word | Meaning |
|---|---|
| water | A clear liquid |
| coffee | A hot drink made from coffee beans |
| tea | A drink made by putting leaves in hot water |
| juice | A drink made from fruit or vegetables |
| soda | A sweet, fizzy drink |
| lemonade | A drink made with lemon |
| hot chocolate | A warm chocolate drink |

## 4. Food Adjectives

| Word | Meaning |
|---|---|
| sweet | Having a sugar-like taste |
| salty | Having a lot of salt |
| spicy | Having a strong, hot taste |
| sour | Having an acidic taste |
| bitter | Having a sharp, unpleasant taste |
| fresh | Recently made or picked |
| delicious | Very good to eat |
| hot | Having a high temperature |
| cold | Having a low temperature |
| hungry | Wanting food |
| thirsty | Wanting a drink |

## 5. Example Sentences

- I eat bread and eggs for breakfast.
- She drinks coffee every morning.
- We have rice and chicken for dinner.
- This soup is hot.
- The lemonade is cold.
- I like sweet food.
- He does not eat meat.

## 6. Useful Expressions

- I'm hungry.
- I'm thirsty.
- I like pizza.
- I don't like fish.
- Can I have some water?
- This food is delicious.
- Would you like some tea?
- No, thank you.

## 7. Short Dialogue

**Sam:** Are you hungry?

**Anna:** Yes, I am.

**Sam:** What would you like to eat?

**Anna:** I'd like a sandwich, please.

**Sam:** Would you like some juice?

**Anna:** Yes, please.

## 8. Exercises

### Exercise A: Choose the Correct Answer

1. Which one is a drink?
   - a) Bread
   - b) Coffee
   - c) Cheese

2. Which food is made from milk?
   - a) Cheese
   - b) Rice
   - c) Chicken

3. Which word means "wanting food"?
   - a) Thirsty
   - b) Hungry
   - c) Cold

4. Which taste is similar to lemon?
   - a) Sour
   - b) Sweet
   - c) Salty

### Exercise B: Fill in the Blanks

Use: `water`, `bread`, `hungry`, `cheese`, `juice`

1. I am ______. I want to eat something.
2. Please give me a glass of ______.
3. I like a sandwich with ______.
4. We eat ______ with soup.
5. She drinks orange ______.

### Exercise C: True or False

1. Coffee is a drink.
2. Rice is usually a drink.
3. Honey is sweet.
4. Fish can be food.

### Exercise D: Speaking Practice

Answer these questions:

1. What do you eat for breakfast?
2. What is your favorite drink?
3. Do you like spicy food?
4. What food do you dislike?
