# Chapter 07: People and Family

## 1. Learning Objectives

- Name family members.
- Describe relationships between people.
- Talk about family size.
- Use possessive adjectives.
- Introduce family members.

## 2. Immediate Family

| Word | Example |
|---|---|
| mother | My mother is kind. |
| father | My father is tall. |
| parents | My parents live here. |
| sister | I have one sister. |
| brother | My brother is a student. |
| daughter | Their daughter is five. |
| son | Their son is young. |
| wife | His wife is a teacher. |
| husband | Her husband is a doctor. |
| child | The child is happy. |
| children | They have two children. |

## 5. Possessive Adjectives

| Subject | Possessive Adjective | Example |
|---|---|---|
| I | my | My mother is kind. |
| you | your | Your brother is tall. |
| he | his | His father is a doctor. |
| she | her | Her sister is young. |
| it | its | The dog likes its food. |
| we | our | Our family is small. |
| they | their | Their children are happy. |

## 6. Family Questions

| Question | Answer |
|---|---|
| How many people are in your family? | There are four people. |
| Do you have brothers or sisters? | Yes, I have one brother. |
| What is your mother's name? | Her name is Sara. |
| Where do your parents live? | They live in New York. |
| Is he your brother? | Yes, he is. |
| Who is that woman? | She is my aunt. |

## 7. Examples in Context

1. My family has five people.
2. My mother is a teacher.
3. My father works in an office.
4. I have two brothers.
5. My grandmother lives with us.
6. My cousin is my best friend.

## 8. Short Conversation

**Anna:** Do you have a big family?

**Mark:** Yes, I do.

**Anna:** How many brothers do you have?

**Mark:** I have two brothers and one sister.

**Anna:** What does your mother do?

**Mark:** She is a teacher.

## 9. Exercises

### Exercise A: Multiple Choice

1. Your mother's mother is your:
   - a) Aunt
   - b) Grandmother
   - c) Sister

2. Your father's brother is your:
   - a) Uncle
   - b) Cousin
   - c) Nephew

3. What is the plural of "child"?
   - a) Childs
   - b) Childes
   - c) Children

4. Which word means a female sibling?
   - a) Brother
   - b) Sister
   - c) Uncle

5. Complete: "She loves ___ family."
   - a) his
   - b) her
   - c) their

### Exercise B: True or False

1. Your grandmother is a member of your family.
2. Your cousin is always your brother or sister.
3. Your parents are your mother and father.
4. "Children" is the plural of "child."
5. Your uncle is your mother's or father's brother.
