# Chapter 27: Weather and Climate

## Learning Objectives

By the end of this chapter, you can:
- Describe common weather conditions.
- Talk about the seasons.
- Ask and answer simple questions about the weather.

## 1. Weather Conditions

| Word | Meaning |
|---|---|
| sunny | With a lot of sunshine |
| cloudy | With many clouds |
| rainy | With a lot of rain |
| snowy | With a lot of snow |
| windy | With a lot of wind |
| foggy | With thick fog |
| stormy | With storms |
| hot | Having a high temperature |
| warm | A little hot |
| cool | A little cold |
| cold | Having a low temperature |
| dry | With little or no water |
| wet | Covered with water |

## 2. Weather Nouns

| Word | Meaning |
|---|---|
| weather | The condition of the air outside |
| temperature | How hot or cold something is |
| rain | Water that falls from clouds |
| snow | Soft white ice that falls from clouds |
| wind | Moving air |
| cloud | A white or gray mass in the sky |
| storm | Bad weather with strong wind, rain, or thunder |
| thunder | A loud sound during a storm |
| lightning | A bright flash during a storm |
| fog | A thick cloud near the ground |
| ice | Frozen water |
| sunshine | Light from the sun |

## 3. The Four Seasons

| Season | Common Weather |
|---|---|
| spring | Warm and sometimes rainy |
| summer | Usually hot and sunny |
| fall | Often cool and windy |
| winter | Usually cold and sometimes snowy |

*Fall* is the common American English word for the season also called *autumn*.

## 4. Useful Weather Questions

| Question | Example Answer |
|---|---|
| How is the weather? | It is sunny. |
| What's the weather like today? | It is cloudy and cool. |
| Is it raining? | Yes, it is. |
| Is it cold outside? | No, it is warm. |
| What is your favorite season? | My favorite season is spring. |

## 5. Example Sentences

- It is sunny today.
- The weather is cold in winter.
- I like warm and sunny days.
- It is raining outside.
- The sky is cloudy.
- We have snow in winter.
- It is very windy today.

## 6. Short Dialogue

**Mia:** What's the weather like today?

**Tom:** It is sunny and warm.

**Mia:** Great! Let's go to the park.

**Tom:** Good idea. It is a beautiful day.

## 7. Useful Expressions

- It's a beautiful day.
- It is very cold outside.
- Take an umbrella.
- It's raining.
- The weather is getting warmer.
- I love sunny weather.
- It is too hot today.
