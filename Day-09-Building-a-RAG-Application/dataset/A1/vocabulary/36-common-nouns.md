# Chapter 36: Common Nouns

## Learning Objectives

By the end of this chapter, you can:
- Recognize common nouns.
- Identify people, places, things, and ideas.
- Use nouns in simple sentences.

## 1. People

| Noun | Example |
|---|---|

## 6. Singular and Plural Nouns

Many nouns form the plural by adding **-s**:

| Singular | Plural |
|---|---|
| book | books |
| car | cars |
| apple | apples |
| friend | friends |

Some nouns add **-es**:

| Singular | Plural |
|---|---|
| bus | buses |
| box | boxes |
| watch | watches |

Some common nouns have irregular plurals:

| Singular | Plural |
|---|---|
| man | men |
| woman | women |
| child | children |
| person | people |

## 7. Example Sentences

2. The books are on the table.
3. We live in a big city.
4. There are flowers in the garden.
5. I have a question.
6. The students are in the classroom.

## 8. Short Dialogue

**Anna:** What is in your bag?

**Ben:** I have a book, a phone, and two pens.

**Anna:** Do you have your keys?

**Ben:** Yes, they are in my bag.

## 9. Exercises

### Exercise A: Identify the Noun

Choose the noun in each sentence.

1. The dog is small.
   - a) the
   - b) dog
   - c) is

2. I read a book.
   - a) read
   - b) a
   - c) book

3. She is happy.
   - a) she
   - b) is
   - c) happy
