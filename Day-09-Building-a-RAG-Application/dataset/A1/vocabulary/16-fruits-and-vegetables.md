# Chapter 16: Fruits and Vegetables

## Learning Objectives

By the end of this chapter, you can:
- Name common fruits and vegetables.
- Describe their colors and tastes.
- Talk about buying fruit and vegetables.

## 1. Fruits

| Word | Meaning |
|---|---|
| apple | A round fruit that can be red, green, or yellow |
| banana | A long, yellow fruit |
| orange | A round orange-colored fruit |
| lemon | A small yellow sour fruit |
| grape | A small round fruit that grows in groups |
| strawberry | A small red fruit |
| watermelon | A large fruit with green skin and red inside |
| pear | A sweet fruit with a narrow top |
| peach | A soft fruit with fuzzy skin |
| mango | A sweet tropical fruit |
| pineapple | A large tropical fruit with rough skin |
| cherry | A small round red or dark fruit |

## 2. Vegetables

| Word | Meaning |
|---|---|
| potato | A common vegetable that grows underground |
| tomato | A soft red fruit used as a vegetable in cooking |
| carrot | A long orange vegetable |
| onion | A vegetable with a strong smell and taste |
| cucumber | A long green vegetable |
| lettuce | A leafy vegetable used in salads |
| cabbage | A round vegetable with many leaves |
| broccoli | A green vegetable with small flower-like parts |
| spinach | A green leafy vegetable |
| pepper | A vegetable that can be green, red, or yellow |
| corn | A yellow grain that grows on a tall plant |
| mushroom | A soft food that grows from fungi |

## 3. Describing Fruits and Vegetables

| Word | Example |
|---|---|
| fresh | These apples are fresh. |
| ripe | The bananas are ripe. |
| unripe | This mango is unripe. |
| sweet | The grapes are sweet. |
| sour | The lemon is sour. |
| juicy | The watermelon is juicy. |
| crunchy | The carrot is crunchy. |
| soft | The peach is soft. |

## 4. Useful Expressions

- I like apples.
- I don't like onions.
- These bananas are ripe.
- How much are the tomatoes?
- I'd like a kilo of potatoes.
- The strawberries look fresh.
- Can I have three oranges, please?

## 5. Example Sentences

- I eat an apple every day.
- She buys fresh vegetables.
- The lemon is yellow and sour.
- We need two potatoes for dinner.
- He likes carrots and broccoli.
- This watermelon is very sweet.

## 6. Short Dialogue

**Customer:** Excuse me. How much are these apples?

**Seller:** They are two dollars a pound.

**Customer:** I'd like two pounds, please.

**Seller:** Here you are.

**Customer:** Thank you.
