# Chapter 22: Buildings and Public Places

## Learning Objectives

By the end of this chapter, you can:
- Name common public buildings.
- Describe what people do in different places.
- Ask simple questions about public places.

## 1. Public Buildings

| Word | Meaning |
|---|---|
| city hall | A building where city government works |
| courthouse | A building where legal cases are heard |
| police station | A building where police officers work |
| fire station | A building where firefighters work |
| hospital | A building where sick people receive medical care |
| school | A building where students learn |
| university | A place for higher education |
| library | A place where people read or borrow books |
| museum | A place where people see historical or artistic objects |
| theater | A place where people watch plays |
| cinema | A place where people watch movies |
| stadium | A large place for sports events |
| church | A building used for Christian religious services |
| community center | A place for local activities and meetings |

## 2. Shops and Services

| Word | Meaning |
|---|---|
| bakery | A shop that sells bread and cakes |
| bookstore | A shop that sells books |
| barbershop | A place where people get haircuts |
| hair salon | A place where people have their hair cut or styled |
| post office | A place for sending letters and packages |
| bank | A place for financial services |
| pharmacy | A shop that sells medicine |
| gas station | A place where vehicles get fuel |
| laundry | A place or service for washing clothes |
| grocery store | A shop that sells food |

## 3. People Who Work There

| Person | Workplace |
|---|---|

| librarian | Library |
| baker | Bakery |
| barber | Barbershop |

## 4. Example Sentences

- The firefighter works at a fire station.
- I buy bread at the bakery.
- My sister studies at a university.
- We watch movies at the cinema.
- The librarian works in the library.
- My father goes to the barbershop.
- The city hall is near the museum.

## 5. Useful Questions

| Question | Answer |
|---|---|
| Where can I buy bread? | At the bakery. |
| Where can I watch a movie? | At the cinema. |
| Where can I borrow a book? | At the library. |
| Where can I see a doctor? | At the hospital or clinic. |
| Where can I send a package? | At the post office. |

## 6. Short Dialogue

**Tourist:** Excuse me. Is there a museum near here?

**Local:** Yes. It is next to the city hall.

**Tourist:** Is it open today?

**Local:** Yes, it is.

**Tourist:** Thank you!

**Local:** You're welcome.
