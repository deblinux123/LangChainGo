# Chapter 06: Countries and Nationalities

## 1. Learning Objectives

- Recognize common country names.
- Identify nationalities.
- Ask where someone is from.
- Talk about languages and countries.
- Use country and nationality words in simple sentences.

## 4. Useful Questions

| Question | Answer |
|---|---|
| Where are you from? | I am from Canada. |
| What is your nationality? | I am Canadian. |
| Where is she from? | She is from Japan. |
| What language do you speak? | I speak English. |
| Is he American? | Yes, he is. |
| Do you speak Spanish? | No, I don't. |

## 5. Important Vocabulary

| Word | Meaning in Context |
|---|---|
| country | A place such as Canada or Japan |
| nationality | The national identity associated with a country |
| language | A system of communication |
| flag | A symbol of a country |
| capital | The main city of a country |
| city | A large town |
| map | A picture of places and countries |
| foreign | From another country |
| international | Involving different countries |
| citizen | A person who legally belongs to a country |

## 6. Examples in Context

1. I am from the United States.
2. My friend is German.
3. She is from Brazil.
4. He speaks Japanese.
5. We are Canadian.
6. My teacher is from Italy.

## 7. Short Conversation

**Alex:** Where are you from?

**Maria:** I am from Spain.

**Alex:** Are you Spanish?

**Maria:** Yes, I am.

**Alex:** What language do you speak?

**Maria:** I speak Spanish and English.

## 9. Speaking Practice

Introduce yourself using this pattern:

"My name is ____. I am from ____. I am ____. I speak ____."

Then introduce a friend using:

"He/She is from ____. He/She speaks ____."
