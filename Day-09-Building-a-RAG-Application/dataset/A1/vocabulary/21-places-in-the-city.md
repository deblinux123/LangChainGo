# Chapter 21: Places in the City

## Learning Objectives

By the end of this chapter, you can:
- Name common places in a city.
- Ask where places are.
- Give simple directions.

## 1. Important Places

| Word | Meaning |
|---|---|
| city | A large town |
| town | A place where people live, usually smaller than a city |
| street | A road in a town or city |
| road | A way for vehicles to travel |
| park | A public green area |
| school | A place where children or students learn |
| hospital | A place where sick people receive medical care |
| bank | A place where people keep and manage money |
| supermarket | A large shop that sells food and household products |
| grocery store | A store that sells food |
| pharmacy | A place where people buy medicine |
| library | A place where people can read or borrow books |
| museum | A place where people see historical or artistic objects |
| restaurant | A place where people buy and eat meals |
| café | A place where people buy coffee and light food |
| hotel | A place where travelers can stay |
| post office | A place where people send letters and packages |
| bus stop | A place where people wait for buses |
| train station | A place where trains arrive and leave |
| police station | A place where police officers work |

## 2. Places and Activities

| Place | Activity |
|---|---|
| Park | Walk or relax |
| Library | Read or borrow books |
| Bank | Get money or manage an account |
| Supermarket | Buy food |
| Pharmacy | Buy medicine |
| Museum | Look at historical objects |
| Restaurant | Eat a meal |
| Hotel | Stay overnight |
| Post office | Send a package |
| Train station | Take a train |

## 3. Direction Words

| Word or Phrase | Meaning |
|---|---|
| left | The opposite direction of right |
| right | The opposite direction of left |
| straight | In the same direction |
| near | Not far away |
| far | A long distance away |
| next to | Beside something |
| opposite | Across from something |
| between | In the middle of two things |
| behind | At the back of something |
| in front of | Before or at the front of something |

## 4. Asking for Directions

| Question | Example Answer |
|---|---|
| Where is the bank? | It is next to the supermarket. |
| Is there a pharmacy near here? | Yes, there is. |
| How can I get to the park? | Go straight and turn left. |
| Is the station far from here? | No, it is near. |

## 5. Example Sentences

- The bank is next to the supermarket.
- There is a park near my house.
- The library is opposite the school.
- Turn right at the traffic light.
- Go straight for two blocks.
- The bus stop is in front of the hotel.
- I go to the grocery store every Friday.

## 6. Short Dialogue

**Tourist:** Excuse me. Where is the train station?

**Local:** Go straight and turn right at the bank.

**Tourist:** Is it far from here?

**Local:** No. It is about five minutes away.

**Tourist:** Thank you for your help.

**Local:** You're welcome.

## 7. Useful Expressions

- Excuse me, where is the bank?
- How can I get to the library?
- Is there a supermarket near here?
- Go straight ahead.
- Turn left at the next street.
- Turn right at the traffic light.
- It is next to the hotel.
- It is opposite the park.
