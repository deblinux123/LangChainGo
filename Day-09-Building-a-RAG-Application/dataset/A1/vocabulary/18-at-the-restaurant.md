# Chapter 18: At the Restaurant

## Learning Objectives

By the end of this chapter, you can:
- Understand common restaurant vocabulary.
- Order food and drinks politely.
- Ask for the bill and simple information.

## 1. Restaurant Vocabulary

| Word | Meaning |
|---|---|
| restaurant | A place where people buy and eat meals |
| menu | A list of food and drinks |
| waiter | A person who serves customers |
| waitress | A woman who serves customers |
| server | A person who serves food and drinks |
| customer | A person who buys something |
| table | Furniture where people eat |
| reservation | An arrangement to keep a table for someone |
| order | Food or drinks requested by a customer |
| bill | A document showing how much you must pay |
| tip | Extra money given for good service |
| dessert | Sweet food after a meal |
| appetizer | A small dish before the main meal |
| main course | The main part of a meal |

## 2. Food and Drink Orders

| Expression | Example |
|---|---|
| I'd like... | I'd like a chicken sandwich. |
| Can I have...? | Can I have some water? |
| I'll have... | I'll have the vegetable soup. |
| Anything else? | No, thank you. |
| For here or to go? | For here, please. |
| The bill, please. | Could we have the bill, please? |

## 3. Restaurant Adjectives

| Word | Meaning |
|---|---|
| delicious | Very good in taste |
| fresh | Recently prepared |
| spicy | Having a hot taste |
| sweet | Having a sugary taste |
| expensive | Costing a lot of money |
| cheap | Not costing much money |
| busy | Having many customers |
| quiet | Not noisy |

## 4. Example Sentences

- Can I see the menu, please?
- I'd like a glass of water.
- The chicken is delicious.
- We have a reservation for two.
- The restaurant is very busy.
- Could we have the bill, please?

## 5. Short Dialogue

**Server:** Good evening. Welcome to our restaurant.

**Customer:** Thank you. Can I see the menu, please?

**Server:** Of course. Here you are.

**Customer:** I'd like a chicken sandwich and some water.

**Server:** Anything else?

**Customer:** No, thank you.

**Server:** Enjoy your meal!

## 6. Useful Expressions

- A table for two, please.
- Do you have a table available?
- What do you recommend?
- I'd like to order.
- Can I have the menu?
- No onions, please.
- Could I have some more water?
- Can we have the bill, please?

   - c) doctor

   - b) reservation
   - c) bill

   - b) dessert
   - c) drink
