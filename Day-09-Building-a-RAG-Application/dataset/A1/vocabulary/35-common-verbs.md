# Chapter 35: Common Verbs

## Learning Objectives

By the end of this chapter, you can:
- Understand common everyday verbs.
- Use verbs in simple sentences.
- Describe basic daily activities.

## 5. Important Verb Patterns

### Verb + Noun

- eat breakfast
- drink water
- read a book
- write a message
- clean the room

### Verb + to + Place

- go to school
- go to work
- come to my house

### Verb + -ing

Some common verbs can be followed by an -ing form:

- like reading
- love swimming
- enjoy playing

Examples:

- I like reading books.
- She enjoys cooking.

## 6. Example Sentences

1. I wake up early.
2. My brother goes to school.
3. We eat dinner together.
4. She speaks English and Spanish.
5. They play soccer on Sundays.
6. I need a new notebook.
7. He watches TV at night.

## 7. Short Dialogue

**Emma:** What do you do every morning?

**Jack:** I wake up, take a shower, and eat breakfast.

**Emma:** Do you go to work by bus?

**Jack:** No, I walk to work.

**Emma:** What do you do after work?

**Jack:** I study English and read a book.
