# Chapter 33: Technology and Communication

## Learning Objectives

By the end of this chapter, you can:
- Name common technology devices.
- Talk about basic internet activities.
- Use simple words for communication.

## 1. Technology Devices

| Word | Meaning |
|---|---|
| computer | An electronic machine |
| laptop | A portable computer |
| tablet | A small touchscreen computer |
| smartphone | A mobile phone with many features |
| phone | A device for calling people |
| camera | A device for taking pictures |
| television | A device for watching programs |
| headphones | Things you wear to listen |
| speaker | A device that plays sound |
| charger | A device that gives power to a battery |

## 2. Internet Vocabulary

| Word | Meaning |
|---|---|
| internet | A worldwide computer network |
| website | A place on the internet |
| email | An electronic message |
| message | Information sent to someone |
| password | A secret word or code |
| account | A personal online profile |
| app | A program on a phone or computer |
| file | A saved digital document |
| photo | A picture |
| video | A moving picture with sound |

## 3. Communication Verbs

| Verb | Example |
|---|---|
| call | I call my friend. |
| text | She texts her brother. |
| send | I send an email. |
| receive | He receives a message. |
| write | We write emails. |
| read | I read the news online. |
| download | I download an app. |
| upload | She uploads a photo. |
| search | They search the internet. |
| share | I share a video. |

## 4. Useful Technology Phrases

- Turn on the computer.
- Turn off the television.
- Charge your phone.
- Open the app.
- Close the website.
- Send me a message.
- Check your email.
- Take a photo.
- Make a phone call.
- Connect to the internet.

## 5. Example Sentences

1. I have a new laptop.
2. My phone needs a charger.
3. She sends emails every morning.
4. We watch videos online.
5. I use an app to learn English.
6. He takes photos with his camera.
7. Please send me a message.

## 6. Short Dialogue

**Mia:** Do you have a smartphone?

**Leo:** Yes, I do.

**Mia:** Do you use it for work?

**Leo:** Yes. I read emails and send messages.

**Mia:** Do you watch videos on it?

**Leo:** Sometimes.

## 7. Exercises

### Exercise A: Match the Words

1. Charger
2. Camera
3. Password
4. Headphones

a. Used to take pictures

b. A secret code

c. Used to charge a phone

d. Used to listen to sound

### Exercise B: Choose the Correct Answer

1. You use a ______ to take photos.
   - a) camera
   - b) charger
   - c) keyboard

2. You send an ______ to someone.
   - a) chair
   - b) email
   - c) window

3. You need a ______ to access your protected account.
   - a) password
   - b) speaker
   - c) photo

### Exercise C: Fill in the Blanks

Use: `download – call – message – internet`

1. I use the ______ to visit websites.
2. Please send me a ______.
3. I want to ______ a new app.
4. I ______ my mother every evening.

### Exercise D: Speaking Practice

1. Do you have a laptop?
2. What apps do you use?
3. Do you send emails every day?
4. What do you use your phone for?
