# Chapter 19: Future with Going To

## 1. Learning Objectives

By the end of this chapter, you will be able to:

- Talk about future plans and intentions.
- Use am, is, and are with going to.
- Make positive and negative sentences.
- Ask yes/no and Wh-questions.
- Describe predictions based on present evidence.

## 2. What Is Going To?

We use **be going to** to talk about future plans, intentions, and predictions based on present evidence.

Examples:

- I am going to study English tonight.
- She is going to visit her parents.
- They are going to travel next month.
- Look at those clouds! It is going to rain.

The action or event is in the future.

## 3. Sentence Structure

Use the correct form of the verb **be** before going to.

| Subject | Be | Structure |
|---|---|---|
| I | am | I am going to... |
| He | is | He is going to... |
| She | is | She is going to... |
| It | is | It is going to... |
| You | are | You are going to... |
| We | are | We are going to... |
| They | are | They are going to... |

### Main Structure

**Subject + am/is/are + going to + base verb**

Examples:

- I am going to work tomorrow.
- He is going to buy a new phone.
- We are going to watch a movie.
- They are going to learn English.

Important: Always use the base form of the main verb after going to.

Correct:
- She is going to study.
- They are going to play.

Incorrect:
- She is going to studies.
- They are going to played.

## 4. Positive Sentences

Use am, is, or are according to the subject.

Examples:

- I am going to cook dinner.
- You are going to meet your friend.
- She is going to start a new job.
- He is going to visit New York.
- We are going to study tonight.
- They are going to move next year.

### Contractions

| Full Form | Contraction |
|---|---|
| I am | I'm |
| You are | You're |
| He is | He's |
| She is | She's |
| It is | It's |
| We are | We're |
| They are | They're |

Examples:

- I'm going to call my friend.
- She's going to travel tomorrow.
- We're going to have dinner together.

## 5. Negative Sentences

Add **not** after am, is, or are.

### Structure

**Subject + am/is/are + not + going to + base verb**

Examples:

- I am not going to work tomorrow.
- She is not going to buy a car.
- They are not going to travel this weekend.
- We are not going to watch TV tonight.

### Negative Contractions

| Full Form | Contraction |
|---|---|
| is not | isn't |
| are not | aren't |

Examples:

- He isn't going to come.
- They aren't going to stay.
- She isn't going to cook tonight.

With "I am not," the common contraction is **I'm not**.

## 6. Yes/No Questions

Put am, is, or are before the subject.

### Structure

**Am/Is/Are + subject + going to + base verb?**

Examples:

- Are you going to study tonight?
- Is she going to visit her parents?
- Is he going to buy a computer?
- Are they going to travel next month?
- Am I going to need a ticket?

### Short Answers

| Question | Positive Answer | Negative Answer |
|---|---|---|
| Are you going to study? | Yes, I am. | No, I'm not. |
| Is she going to work? | Yes, she is. | No, she isn't. |
| Is he going to come? | Yes, he is. | No, he isn't. |
| Are they going to travel? | Yes, they are. | No, they aren't. |

## 7. Wh- Questions

Put a question word before am, is, or are.

### Structure

**Question word + am/is/are + subject + going to + base verb?**

Examples:

| Question Word | Example |
|---|---|
| What | What are you going to do? |
| Where | Where is she going to stay? |
| When | When are they going to arrive? |
| Why | Why is he going to leave? |
| Who | Who are you going to meet? |
| How | How are we going to get there? |

More examples:

- What are you going to study?
- Where are they going to live?
- When is she going to call?
- Who is he going to invite?

## 8. Uses of Going To

### Use 1: Future Plans

Use going to for plans you have already decided.

Examples:

- I'm going to study computer science.
- We're going to visit our grandparents.
- She's going to start a new course.
- They are going to move to California.

### Use 2: Future Intentions

Use going to to describe something you intend or want to do.

Examples:

- I'm going to learn English.
- He's going to exercise more.
- We're going to save money.
- She is going to find a new job.

### Use 3: Predictions Based on Present Evidence

Use going to when something in the present gives evidence about the future.

Examples:

- Look at the dark clouds! It's going to rain.
- Be careful! You're going to fall.
- The baby is going to cry.
- That car is going to hit the wall!

## 9. Future Time Expressions

Common expressions used with going to:

| Expression | Example |
|---|---|
| tomorrow | I'm going to work tomorrow. |
| tonight | We're going to cook tonight. |
| this weekend | She's going to travel this weekend. |
| next week | They are going to visit us next week. |
| next month | He is going to move next month. |
| next year | I'm going to start university next year. |
| soon | It is going to happen soon. |
| in two days | We're going to leave in two days. |

## 10. Going To vs. Present Continuous

Both forms can describe future arrangements, but they emphasize different things.

| Going To | Present Continuous |
|---|---|
| I'm going to visit my friend. | I'm visiting my friend tomorrow. |
| We're going to travel next week. | We're traveling next week. |

**Going to** often emphasizes a plan or intention.

**Present continuous** often emphasizes a future arrangement that has been organized.

At A1 level, both forms can be useful for talking about future plans.

## 11. Common Mistakes

| Incorrect | Correct |
|---|---|
| I going to study. | I'm going to study. |
| She are going to work. | She is going to work. |
| They is going to travel. | They are going to travel. |
| He is going to buys a car. | He is going to buy a car. |
| Are she going to come? | Is she going to come? |
| You aren't going study. | You aren't going to study. |
| Is they going to play? | Are they going to play? |

## 12. American English Notes

These examples use standard American English.

- "Going to" is often pronounced informally as "gonna" in everyday speech.
- "Gonna" is informal and should generally not be used in formal writing.
- Use "vacation" for a trip away from home.
- "This weekend" is a common expression for the upcoming weekend.

Examples:

- I'm going to get some coffee.
- We're going to watch a movie.
- She's going to take a vacation next month.

## 15. Chapter Summary

In this chapter, you learned:

- Use am/is/are + going to + base verb.
- Use going to for future plans and intentions.
- Use going to for predictions based on present evidence.
- Add not after am/is/are to make negative sentences.
- Put am/is/are before the subject to make questions.
- Use question words before am/is/are for Wh-questions.

### Key Structures

| Sentence Type | Structure |
|---|---|
| Positive | Subject + am/is/are + going to + base verb |
| Negative | Subject + am/is/are + not + going to + base verb |

| Wh-Question | Question word + am/is/are + subject + going to + base verb? |
