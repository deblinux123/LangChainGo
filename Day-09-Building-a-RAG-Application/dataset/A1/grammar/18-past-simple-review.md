# Chapter 18: Past Simple — Review and Mixed Practice

## 1. Learning Objectives

By the end of this chapter, you will be able to:

- Review the past forms of the verb be.
- Use regular and irregular past-tense verbs.
- Make positive, negative, and question forms.
- Choose between was/were and did.
- Use past-time expressions correctly.
- Write and talk about completed past events.

## 2. Review: Past Simple

The past simple describes actions and situations that started and finished in the past.

Examples:

- I watched a movie yesterday.
- She went to school last Monday.
- They were at home last night.
- We visited our friends last weekend.

## 3. Review: Was and Were

Use **was** and **were** as the past forms of the verb be.

| Subject | Past Form |
|---|---|
| I | was |
| He | was |
| She | was |
| It | was |
| You | were |
| We | were |
| They | were |

Examples:

- I was tired yesterday.
- She was at work.
- You were late.
- They were happy.

### Negative Form

Use wasn't or weren't.

- I wasn't busy.
- He wasn't at home.
- We weren't ready.
- They weren't tired.

### Question Form

Put was or were before the subject.

- Was she happy?
- Were they at school?
- Was he busy?
- Were you tired?

Remember: Do not use did with was or were.

## 4. Review: Regular Verbs

Regular verbs usually form the past simple by adding **-ed**.

| Base Form | Past Form |
|---|---|
| work | worked |
| play | played |
| watch | watched |
| clean | cleaned |
| visit | visited |
| study | studied |
| stop | stopped |
| live | lived |

Examples:

- I worked yesterday.
- She studied English last night.
- They visited their grandparents.

### Negative Form

Use **didn't + base verb**.

- I didn't work yesterday.
- She didn't study English.
- They didn't visit us.

### Question Form

Use **Did + subject + base verb?**

- Did you work yesterday?
- Did she study English?
- Did they visit their friends?

## 5. Review: Irregular Verbs

Irregular verbs have special past forms.

| Base Form | Past Form |
|---|---|
| go | went |
| eat | ate |
| see | saw |
| buy | bought |
| come | came |
| have | had |
| make | made |
| take | took |
| write | wrote |
| get | got |
| find | found |
| give | gave |
| speak | spoke |
| drink | drank |
| sleep | slept |

Examples:

- I went to the store.
- She ate breakfast.
- We saw a movie.
- He bought a new phone.

### Negative Form

Use the base form after didn't.

- I didn't go.
- She didn't eat.
- We didn't see the movie.
- He didn't buy anything.

### Question Form

Use the base form after did.

- Did you go?
- Did she eat?
- Did they see the movie?
- Did he buy a phone?

## 6. Choosing Between Was/Were and Did

This is one of the most important differences in the past simple.

Use **was/were** when the main verb is be.

Examples:

- I was tired.
- She wasn't at home.
- Were they happy?

Use **did/didn't** with other verbs.

Examples:

- I worked yesterday.
- She didn't call me.
- Did they visit you?

| Meaning | Correct Question |
|---|---|
| You were tired. | Were you tired? |
| You worked yesterday. | Did you work yesterday? |
| She was happy. | Was she happy? |
| She visited her friend. | Did she visit her friend? |

Common mistakes:

- Incorrect: Did you were tired?
- Correct: Were you tired?
- Incorrect: Did she visited him?
- Correct: Did she visit him?

## 7. Past-Time Expressions

| Expression | Example |
|---|---|
| yesterday | I worked yesterday. |
| last night | She watched TV last night. |
| last week | We visited them last week. |
| last month | He bought a car last month. |
| last year | They moved last year. |
| two days ago | I saw him two days ago. |
| a week ago | She called me a week ago. |
| in 2021 | We lived there in 2021. |

## 8. Common Sentence Patterns

| Type | Structure | Example |
|---|---|---|
| Positive with be | Subject + was/were | She was happy. |
| Negative with be | Subject + wasn't/weren't | She wasn't happy. |
| Question with be | Was/Were + subject? | Was she happy? |
| Positive with other verbs | Subject + past verb | She worked. |
| Negative with other verbs | Subject + didn't + base verb | She didn't work. |
| Question with other verbs | Did + subject + base verb? | Did she work? |

### Exercise F: Complete the Conversation

Complete the conversation using the past simple.

**Alex:** Hi, Sam! How _____ (be) your weekend?

**Sam:** It _____ (be) great! I _____ (go) to the beach with my family.

**Alex:** That sounds fun. _____ you _____ (swim)?

**Sam:** Yes, I _____. We also _____ (eat) lunch at a restaurant.

**Alex:** _____ the weather good?

**Sam:** Yes, it _____. But we _____ (not/stay) until evening because we _____ (be) tired.

**Alex:** Nice! I _____ (stay) home and _____ (watch) a movie.

### Exercise G: Writing Practice

Write a paragraph of 8–10 sentences about what you did last weekend.

Include:

- At least three regular verbs.
- At least three irregular verbs.
- At least two sentences with was or were.
- Two negative sentences.
- One yes/no question or Wh-question.
- At least three past-time expressions.

Example:

"Last Saturday, I went to the city with my friends. We visited a new restaurant and ate lunch there. The food was delicious. After lunch, we walked around the city. I bought a book, but my friend didn't buy anything. We were tired in the evening. Did we enjoy the day? Yes, we did!"

## 11. Chapter Summary

In this chapter, you reviewed:

- Was and were for the past form of be.
- Regular verbs with -ed.
- Common irregular past forms.
- Negative sentences with wasn't, weren't, and didn't.
- Questions with was, were, and did.

- The difference between be and other verbs in past simple sentences.

### Final Reminder

- Use **was/were** with the past form of be.
- Use **did/didn't + base verb** with other verbs.
- Never use a past-tense verb after did or didn't.
