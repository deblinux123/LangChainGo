# Chapter 15: Past Simple — Was and Were

## 1. Learning Objectives

By the end of this chapter, you will be able to:

- Use **was** and **were** to talk about the past.
- Make positive and negative sentences.
- Ask and answer yes/no questions.
- Ask questions with where, when, and how.
- Use common past-time expressions.

## 2. What Are Was and Were?

**Was** and **were** are the past forms of the verb **be**.

We use them to describe people, places, feelings, situations, and conditions in the past.

Examples:

- I was tired yesterday.
- She was at home last night.
- They were happy.
- We were in New York last year.

## 3. Was or Were?

Use the following table:

| Subject | Past Form |
|---|---|
| I | was |
| He | was |
| She | was |
| It | was |
| You | were |
| We | were |
| They | were |

Examples:

- I was busy.
- He was a student.
- You were late.
- They were at school.

Remember: **You always takes were**, even when you are talking to one person.

## 4. Positive Sentences

### Structure

**Subject + was/were + complement**

Examples:

- I was at work yesterday.
- My brother was sick.
- The movie was interesting.
- We were very busy.
- You were right.
- The children were in the park.

### Contractions

In informal English, we often use contractions:

| Full Form | Contraction |
|---|---|
| I was | I was |
| He was | He was |
| She was | She was |
| It was | It was |
| You were | You were |
| We were | We were |
| They were | They were |

Unlike present forms such as "I'm" and "you're," **was** and **were** are not normally contracted in standard written English.

## 5. Negative Sentences

Add **not** after was or were.

### Structure

**Subject + was/were + not + complement**

Examples:

- I was not tired.
- She was not at home.
- We were not ready.
- They were not happy.

### Negative Contractions

| Full Form | Contraction |
|---|---|
| was not | wasn't |
| were not | weren't |

Examples:

- I wasn't hungry.
- He wasn't at school.
- We weren't late.
- They weren't ready.

## 6. Yes/No Questions

To make a question, put **was** or **were** before the subject.

### Structure

**Was/Were + subject + complement?**

Examples:

- Was he tired?
- Was she at work?
- Were you busy?
- Were they at home?

### Short Answers

| Question | Positive Answer | Negative Answer |
|---|---|---|
| Was he tired? | Yes, he was. | No, he wasn't. |
| Was she happy? | Yes, she was. | No, she wasn't. |
| Were you busy? | Yes, I was. | No, I wasn't. |
| Were they ready? | Yes, they were. | No, they weren't. |

Notice that the answer to "Were you...?" often uses **I** when you are talking about yourself.

## 7. Wh- Questions

We can use question words before was or were.

| Question Word | Example |
|---|---|
| Where | Where were you yesterday? |
| When | When was the meeting? |
| Why | Why was he angry? |
| How | How was your weekend? |
| Who | Who was at the door? |

Examples:

- Where was your brother?
- Why were they late?
- How was the movie?
- When were you in Chicago?

### Structure

**Question word + was/were + subject + complement?**

## 8. Past-Time Expressions

We often use these expressions with was and were:

| Expression | Example |
|---|---|
| yesterday | I was busy yesterday. |
| yesterday morning | She was at home yesterday morning. |
| last night | We were tired last night. |
| last week | They were in Boston last week. |
| last month | He was sick last month. |
| last year | I was a student last year. |
| two days ago | She was here two days ago. |
| in 2020 | We were in California in 2020. |

Remember:

- **Last** + time period: last week, last year, last night.
- **Ago** comes after a period of time: two days ago, three years ago.

## 9. Was/Were vs. Am/Is/Are

Use am, is, and are for the present. Use was and were for the past.

| Present | Past |
|---|---|
| I am tired. | I was tired yesterday. |
| She is happy. | She was happy yesterday. |
| They are at home. | They were at home last night. |
| We are busy. | We were busy last week. |

Examples:

- Today, he is at work.
- Yesterday, he was at home.
- They are happy now.
- They were sad yesterday.

## 10. Important: Do Not Use "Did" With Was/Were

For questions and negatives with the past form of **be**, use was or were directly.

Correct:

- Was she tired?
- Were they at school?
- He wasn't happy.
- We weren't ready.

Incorrect:

- Did she was tired?
- Did they were at school?
- He didn't was happy.

Compare this with ordinary past-tense verbs:

- Did you work yesterday?
- Did she visit her friend?

The verb **be** works differently from ordinary verbs in the simple past.

## 11. Common Mistakes

| Incorrect | Correct |
|---|---|
| I were tired. | I was tired. |
| They was happy. | They were happy. |
| You was late. | You were late. |
| Was they at home? | Were they at home? |
| Did you were busy? | Were you busy? |
| She weren't ready. | She wasn't ready. |
| He were a teacher. | He was a teacher. |

## 12. American English Notes

These examples use standard American English.

- "At work" is a common expression for being at your workplace.
- "On the weekend" is common in American English.
- "How was your weekend?" is a natural everyday question.
- "Last night" refers to the previous evening or night.

Examples:

- I was at the office yesterday.
- We were on vacation last month.
- How was your weekend?
- The restaurant was great.

## 15. Chapter Summary

In this chapter, you learned:

- **Was** is used with I, he, she, and it.
- **Were** is used with you, we, and they.
- Add **not** after was or were to make negative sentences.
- Put was or were before the subject to make yes/no questions.
- Put question words before was or were to make Wh-questions.
- Do not use **did** with was or were in simple past questions and negatives.

### Key Structures

| Sentence Type | Structure |
|---|---|
| Positive | Subject + was/were + complement |
| Negative | Subject + wasn't/weren't + complement |
| Yes/No Question | Was/Were + subject + complement? |
| Wh-Question | Question word + was/were + subject + complement? |
