# Chapter 17: Past Simple — Irregular Verbs

## 1. Learning Objectives

By the end of this chapter, you will be able to:

- Understand what irregular verbs are.
- Recognize common irregular past-tense forms.
- Use irregular verbs in positive past simple sentences.
- Make negative sentences and questions with irregular verbs.
- Use common past-time expressions.

## 2. What Are Irregular Verbs?

Most regular verbs form the past simple by adding **-ed**.

Examples:

- work → worked
- play → played
- watch → watched

However, some verbs do not follow this rule. They are called **irregular verbs**.

Examples:

- go → went
- eat → ate
- see → saw
- have → had
- buy → bought

You need to learn and remember their past forms.

## 4. Positive Sentences

Use the irregular past form directly after the subject.

### Structure

**Subject + irregular past verb + complement**

Examples:

- I went to school yesterday.
- She ate breakfast at 8:00.
- We saw a movie last night.
- He bought a new phone.
- They came home late.
- My friend wrote an email.

The past form is the same for all subjects.

Correct:

- I went.
- She went.
- They went.

Do not add **-ed** to irregular verbs.

Incorrect:

- goed
- eated
- buyed
- comed

## 5. Negative Sentences

Use **did not** or **didn't** followed by the base form of the verb.

### Structure

**Subject + didn't + base verb + complement**

Examples:

- I didn't go to work.
- She didn't eat breakfast.
- They didn't see the movie.
- He didn't buy a car.
- We didn't come home late.

Important: After **didn't**, always use the base form, even when the original verb is irregular.

| Incorrect | Correct |
|---|---|
| I didn't went. | I didn't go. |
| She didn't ate. | She didn't eat. |
| They didn't saw it. | They didn't see it. |
| He didn't bought it. | He didn't buy it. |

## 6. Yes/No Questions

Use **did** before the subject and the base form of the main verb.

### Structure

**Did + subject + base verb + complement?**

Examples:

- Did you go to school?
- Did she eat lunch?
- Did they see the movie?
- Did he buy a new phone?
- Did your friends come yesterday?

### Short Answers

| Question | Positive Answer | Negative Answer |
|---|---|---|
| Did you go? | Yes, I did. | No, I didn't. |
| Did she eat? | Yes, she did. | No, she didn't. |
| Did they come? | Yes, they did. | No, they didn't. |
| Did he buy it? | Yes, he did. | No, he didn't. |

## 7. Wh- Questions

Use a question word before **did**.

### Structure

**Question word + did + subject + base verb?**

Examples:

- Where did you go?
- What did she eat?
- When did they come?
- Why did he leave?
- Who did you meet?
- What did you buy?

Remember: The main verb remains in its base form after **did**.

Correct:
- Where did you go?

Incorrect:
- Where did you went?

## 8. The Special Case: Was and Were

The verb **be** is different from other irregular verbs.

Use:

- **was** with I, he, she, and it.
- **were** with you, we, and they.

Examples:

- I was tired.
- She was at home.
- They were happy.

For questions and negatives, do not use **did** with was/were.

Correct:
- Was he at home?
- They weren't ready.

Incorrect:
- Did he be at home?
- They didn't were ready.

## 10. Past-Time Expressions

Irregular verbs often appear with past-time expressions.

| Expression | Example |
|---|---|
| yesterday | I went shopping yesterday. |
| last night | She slept early last night. |
| last week | We saw him last week. |
| last month | They bought a car last month. |
| two days ago | He came home two days ago. |
| in 2022 | I moved here in 2022. |

## 11. Common Mistakes

| Incorrect | Correct |
|---|---|
| I goed home. | I went home. |
| She eated lunch. | She ate lunch. |
| He buyed a phone. | He bought a phone. |
| They comed late. | They came late. |
| I didn't went. | I didn't go. |
| Did you saw him? | Did you see him? |
| Where did she went? | Where did she go? |
| He didn't took it. | He didn't take it. |

## 12. American English Notes

These examples use standard American English.

- "Got" is a common American past form of "get."
- "Takeout" is a common American term for restaurant food that you take away.
- "Vacation" is common in American English.

Examples:

- I got a new laptop last week.
- We took some food home.
- She went on vacation last month.
- He bought takeout for dinner.

## 15. Chapter Summary

In this chapter, you learned:

- Irregular verbs have special past forms.
- Learn the base form and past form together.
- Use the past form in positive sentences.
- Use **didn't + base verb** in negative sentences.
- Use **Did + subject + base verb** in questions.
- Do not use the past form after did or didn't.
- The verb **be** uses was and were instead of did.

### Key Structures

| Sentence Type | Structure |
|---|---|
| Positive | Subject + irregular past verb |
| Negative | Subject + didn't + base verb |
| Yes/No Question | Did + subject + base verb? |
| Wh-Question | Question word + did + subject + base verb? |
