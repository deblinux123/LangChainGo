# Chapter 1: Present Simple

## 1. Learning Objectives

By the end of this chapter, students will be able to:

- Understand the present simple tense.
- Describe daily routines and habits.
- Talk about general facts.
- Make positive and negative sentences.
- Ask questions using do and does.
- Use common frequency expressions.

## 2. What Is the Present Simple?

The present simple is one of the most common verb tenses in English.

We use it to describe:

### A. Daily Routines

Things that we do regularly.

Examples:
- I wake up at 7:00 every morning.
- She goes to work by bus.
- We eat dinner at 6:30.

### B. Habits

Things that we do repeatedly.

Examples:
- He drinks coffee every day.
- They play basketball on weekends.
- I usually read before bed.

### C. General Facts

Things that are generally or scientifically true.

Examples:
- Water boils at 100 degrees Celsius.
- The sun rises in the east.
- Birds have wings.

### D. Permanent or Long-Term Situations

Examples:
- She lives in New York.
- My father works at a hospital.
- They study English.

## 3. Positive Sentences

### Structure

Subject + Base Verb + Complement

Examples:

- I speak English.
- You work every day.
- We live in the United States.
- They study together.

### Third-Person Singular: He, She, It

For he, she, and it, we usually add -s or -es to the verb.

Examples:

| Base Verb | Third-Person Form |
|---|---|
| work | works |
| speak | speaks |
| eat | eats |
| watch | watches |
| go | goes |
| study | studies |
| have | has |

### Spelling Rules

1. Most verbs: add -s.
   - work → works
   - read → reads

2. Verbs ending in -ch, -sh, -ss, -x, or -o: usually add -es.
   - watch → watches
   - wash → washes
   - pass → passes
   - fix → fixes
   - go → goes

3. Verbs ending in consonant + y: change y to ies.
   - study → studies
   - try → tries

4. Verbs ending in vowel + y: add -s.
   - play → plays
   - enjoy → enjoys

## 4. Negative Sentences

Use do not (don't) or does not (doesn't).

### Structure

Subject + do/does + not + Base Verb

Examples:

- I don't like cold weather.
- You don't work on Sundays.
- They don't speak Spanish.
- He doesn't play tennis.
- She doesn't drink coffee.

### Important Rule

After doesn't, always use the base form of the verb.

Correct:
- She doesn't work here.

Incorrect:
- She doesn't works here.

## 5. Questions

Use Do or Does at the beginning of a question.

### Structure

Do/Does + Subject + Base Verb + Complement?

Examples:

- Do you speak English?
- Do they live in Chicago?
- Does she work here?
- Does he play soccer?

### Short Answers

| Question | Positive | Negative |
|---|---|---|
| Do you work? | Yes, I do. | No, I don't. |
| Does he work? | Yes, he does. | No, he doesn't. |
| Do they study? | Yes, they do. | No, they don't. |

## 6. Frequency Adverbs

Frequency adverbs describe how often something happens.

| Adverb | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Always | 100% of the time |
| Usually | Most of the time |
| Often | Many times |
| Sometimes | Occasionally |
| Rarely | Not often |
| Never | 0% of the time |

### Position in Sentences

Frequency adverbs usually come before the main verb.

Examples:
- I always drink water.
- She usually walks to work.
- They sometimes watch movies.
- He never eats breakfast.

With the verb to be, they usually come after the verb.

Examples:
- She is always happy.
- They are usually busy.

## 7. Time Expressions

Common time expressions:

- Every day
- Every morning
- Every week
- On Mondays
- Once a week
- Twice a month
- In the morning
- At night

Examples:
- I exercise every morning.
- She visits her parents twice a month.
- We go shopping on Saturdays.

## 8. Common Mistakes

Incorrect: He work every day.

Correct: He works every day.

Incorrect: She don't like pizza.

Correct: She doesn't like pizza.

Incorrect: Does he works here?

Correct: Does he work here?

Incorrect: I am go to school every day.

Correct: I go to school every day.

## 9. American English Notes

This chapter uses standard American English.

Common American vocabulary examples:

| American English | British English |
|---|---|
| Apartment | Flat |
| Elevator | Lift |
| Vacation | Holiday |
| Soccer | Football |
| Trash | Rubbish |

These vocabulary differences do not change the present simple grammar rules.

## 12. Chapter Summary

The present simple is used for habits, routines, facts, and long-term situations.

Remember:

- Use the base verb with I, you, we, and they.
- Add -s or -es with he, she, and it.
- Use don't for negative sentences with I, you, we, and they.
- Use doesn't for negative sentences with he, she, and it.
- Use do and does to form questions.
