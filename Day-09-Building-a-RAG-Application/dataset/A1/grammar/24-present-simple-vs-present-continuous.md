# Chapter 24 — Present Simple vs. Present Continuous

## 1. Learning Objectives

By the end of this chapter, you will be able to:

- Understand the difference between the present simple and present continuous.
- Talk about habits and routines.
- Describe actions happening now.
- Choose the correct tense in everyday situations.
- Use common time expressions with each tense.
- Ask and answer questions using both tenses.

## 2. Present Simple: Habits and Facts

We use the **present simple** to talk about:

- Daily routines and habits.
- Things that happen regularly.
- Facts and general truths.
- Likes and dislikes.

### Examples

- I work every day.
- She drinks coffee every morning.
- They play soccer on weekends.
- Water boils at 100°C.
- He likes chocolate.
- We live in the United States.

### Structure

**Positive:**

Subject + base verb (+ s/es for he, she, it) + ...

| Subject | Example |
|---|---|
| I | I work every day. |
| You | You study English. |
| He | He works every day. |
| She | She studies English. |
| We | We play soccer. |
| They | They live here. |

### Negative

Subject + don't / doesn't + base verb + ...

- I don't work on Sundays.
- She doesn't drink coffee.
- They don't play tennis.
- He doesn't watch TV.

### Questions

Do / Does + subject + base verb + ...?

- Do you work every day?
- Does she speak English?
- Do they live here?
- Does he play soccer?

## 3. Present Continuous: Actions Happening Now

We use the **present continuous** to talk about:

- Actions happening at the moment of speaking.
- Temporary situations.
- Activities happening around the present time.

### Examples

- I am studying English now.
- She is drinking coffee.
- They are playing soccer.
- He is talking on the phone.
- We are learning grammar.

### Structure

Subject + am / is / are + verb-ing + ...

| Subject | Form | Example |
|---|---|---|
| I | am + verb-ing | I am working. |
| You | are + verb-ing | You are studying. |
| He | is + verb-ing | He is sleeping. |
| She | is + verb-ing | She is cooking. |
| We | are + verb-ing | We are learning. |
| They | are + verb-ing | They are playing. |

### Negative

Subject + am not / isn't / aren't + verb-ing + ...

- I am not sleeping.
- She isn't working.
- They aren't studying.
- We aren't watching TV.

### Questions

Am / Is / Are + subject + verb-ing + ...?

- Are you studying?
- Is she cooking?
- Are they playing?
- Am I speaking too fast?

## 4. The Main Difference

The most important difference is:

- **Present simple:** Something happens regularly or is generally true.
- **Present continuous:** Something is happening now or is temporary.

<text> </text>

| Present Simple | Present Continuous |
|---|---|
| I work every day. | I am working now. |
| She drinks coffee every morning. | She is drinking coffee now. |
| They play soccer on Sundays. | They are playing soccer now. |
| He reads books every night. | He is reading a book right now. |
| We study English every day. | We are studying English at the moment. |

<text> </text>

### Compare These Situations

**Situation 1: Work**

- I work in a bank. (My regular job.)
- I am working from home today. (A temporary situation.)

**Situation 2: Coffee**

- She drinks coffee every morning. (A habit.)
- She is drinking coffee now. (An action happening now.)

**Situation 3: English**

- They study English every Monday. (A regular activity.)
- They are studying English right now. (An action happening now.)

## 5. Time Expressions

Some time expressions help us identify the correct tense.

### Present Simple

Common expressions:

- Every day
- Every morning
- Every week
- On Mondays
- Usually
- Always
- Often
- Sometimes
- Never

Examples:

- I exercise every morning.
- She usually takes the bus.
- We visit our grandparents on Sundays.
- He never drinks soda.

### Present Continuous

Common expressions:

- Now
- Right now
- At the moment
- Today
- This week
- These days

Examples:

- I am studying now.
- She is working at the moment.
- They are staying with their friends this week.
- We are learning English these days.

Important: Time expressions are clues, not absolute rules. Always think about the meaning of the sentence.

## 6. Verb Forms: Adding -ing

To make the present continuous, add **-ing** to the base form of the verb.

### Rule 1: Most Verbs

Add -ing.

| Base Verb | -ing Form |
|---|---|
| work | working |
| read | reading |
| play | playing |
| eat | eating |
| watch | watching |

### Rule 2: Verbs Ending in -e

Remove the final -e and add -ing.

| Base Verb | -ing Form |
|---|---|
| make | making |
| write | writing |
| take | taking |
| dance | dancing |
| come | coming |

### Rule 3: Short Verbs with a Consonant-Vowel-Consonant Pattern

For many short verbs, double the final consonant and add -ing.

| Base Verb | -ing Form |
|---|---|
| run | running |
| sit | sitting |
| swim | swimming |
| stop | stopping |

There are spelling exceptions, but these are useful basic patterns.

## 7. Stative Verbs: Verbs Not Usually Used in Continuous Forms

Some verbs describe feelings, thoughts, possession, or preferences rather than physical actions.

At this level, learn these common examples:

| Verb | Example |
|---|---|
| like | I like music. |
| love | She loves chocolate. |
| want | I want some water. |
| need | We need help. |
| know | He knows the answer. |
| understand | I understand you. |
| believe | They believe him. |

These verbs are not usually used in the present continuous with their ordinary meanings.

Incorrect: I am knowing the answer.

Correct: I know the answer.

Incorrect: She is wanting coffee.

Correct: She wants coffee.

## 8. Verbs That Can Have Different Meanings

Some verbs can be used in both tenses, but their meanings may change.

### Think

- I think this movie is good. (Opinion.)
- I am thinking about my vacation. (An activity happening now.)

### Have

- I have a car. (Possession.)
- I am having lunch. (An activity.)

At A1 level, focus on the common meanings and examples.

## 9. Common Mistakes

### Mistake 1: Forgetting am, is, or are

Incorrect: She cooking now.

Correct: She is cooking now.

### Mistake 2: Using -ing without a form of be

Incorrect: I studying English.

Correct: I am studying English.

### Mistake 3: Adding -s in the present continuous

Incorrect: He is plays soccer.

Correct: He is playing soccer.

### Mistake 4: Using the base verb after doesn't

Incorrect: She doesn't works here.

Correct: She doesn't work here.

### Mistake 5: Using present simple for an action happening now

Incorrect: I eat dinner right now.

Correct: I am eating dinner right now.

### Mistake 6: Using present continuous for a regular habit

Incorrect: I am going to work every day.

Correct: I go to work every day.

### Mistake 7: Using continuous forms with common stative verbs

Incorrect: I am liking this song.

Correct: I like this song.

## 10. American English Notes

Both tenses are commonly used in everyday American English.

In American English:

- "Right now" is very common when talking about actions happening at this moment.
- "These days" often describes temporary situations around the present time.
- Present simple is commonly used for routines, schedules, and facts.
- Present continuous is commonly used for temporary activities and current situations.

Examples:

- I usually drive to work, but today I am taking the bus.
- She works at a hospital, but this week she is working from home.
- We normally eat at home, but tonight we are eating out.

## 13. Chapter Summary

In this chapter, you learned:

- The present simple describes habits, routines, and facts.
- The present continuous describes actions happening now and temporary situations.
- Present simple uses the base verb, with -s or -es for he, she, and it.
- Present continuous uses am, is, or are + verb-ing.
- Frequency expressions such as "every day" often appear with the present simple.
- Expressions such as "right now" and "at the moment" often appear with the present continuous.
- Some stative verbs, such as know, like, and want, are not usually used in continuous forms.
