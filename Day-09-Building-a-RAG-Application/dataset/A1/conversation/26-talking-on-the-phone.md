# Chapter 26: Talking About Health

## Learning Objectives

By the end of this chapter, you will be able to:

- Talk about simple health problems.
- Ask someone how they feel.
- Describe common symptoms.
- Give simple suggestions.

## 1. Key Expressions

| Expression | Example |
|---|---|
| How do you feel? | How do you feel today? |
| I feel sick. | I feel sick today. |
| I have a headache. | I have a headache. |
| I have a cold. | I have a cold. |
| My stomach hurts. | My stomach hurts. |
| Are you okay? | Are you okay? |
| Get some rest. | You should get some rest. |
| Drink some water. | Please drink some water. |

## 2. Example Conversations

### Conversation 1: Feeling Sick

**Anna:** Hi, Ben. Are you okay?

**Ben:** Not really. I feel sick.

**Anna:** What's wrong?

**Ben:** I have a headache.

**Anna:** I'm sorry. Get some rest.

### Conversation 2: A Stomachache

**Mike:** You look tired. Are you okay?

**Sara:** My stomach hurts.

**Mike:** Oh, I'm sorry.

**Sara:** Thank you.

### Conversation 3: Giving Simple Advice

**John:** I have a cold.

**Emma:** Oh no! Drink some water and get some rest.

**John:** Thank you for your advice.

**Emma:** You're welcome.

## 3. Useful Vocabulary

| Word | Meaning |
|---|---|
| health | The condition of your body |
| sick | Not feeling well |
| healthy | In good health |
| headache | Pain in your head |
| stomachache | Pain in your stomach |
| cold | A common illness |
| cough | A sound made when your throat is irritated |
| fever | A high body temperature |
| tired | Needing rest |
| pain | An unpleasant feeling in your body |
| rest | Time to relax |
| symptom | A sign of an illness |
