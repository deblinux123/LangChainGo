# Chapter 15: Shopping at a Store

## Learning Objectives

- Ask for products in a store.
- Ask about prices.
- Understand simple shopping questions.
- Pay for products politely.

## Key Expressions

| Expression | Example |
|---|---|
| Can I help you? | Can I help you find something? |
| I'm just looking. | Thanks, I'm just looking. |
| I'm looking for... | I'm looking for a notebook. |
| How much is this? | How much is this bag? |
| Do you have...? | Do you have this in blue? |
| Can I pay by card? | Can I pay by card? |
| I'll take it. | I'll take this one. |
| That's too expensive. | That's too expensive for me. |

## Conversation 1: At a Bookstore

**Assistant:** Hello! Can I help you?

**Customer:** Yes, I'm looking for an English book.

**Assistant:** What level do you need?

**Customer:** Beginner level, please.

**Assistant:** This book is good for beginners.

**Customer:** Great! How much is it?

**Assistant:** It's twelve dollars.

**Customer:** I'll take it.

## Conversation 2: At a Gift Shop

**Shop Assistant:** Hi! Are you looking for anything?

**Mia:** Yes, I need a gift for my sister.

**Shop Assistant:** How about this small bag?

**Mia:** That's nice. How much is it?

**Shop Assistant:** It's twenty dollars.

**Mia:** Do you have it in black?

**Shop Assistant:** Yes, we do.

**Mia:** Great. I'll take the black one.

## Conversation 3: Paying at the Store

**Cashier:** Your total is thirty-five dollars.

**Customer:** Can I pay by card?

**Cashier:** Yes, of course.

**Customer:** Here you are.

**Cashier:** Thank you. Have a nice day!

**Customer:** You, too!

## Useful Vocabulary

| Word | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Store | A place where people buy things |
| Customer | A person who buys something |
| Cashier | A person who receives payment |
| Price | The amount of money something costs |
| Product | Something that is sold |
| Cheap | Not expensive |
| Expensive | Costing a lot of money |
| Receipt | A paper showing a purchase |
| Cash | Money in notes or coins |
| Card | A payment card |
| Total | The complete amount |
