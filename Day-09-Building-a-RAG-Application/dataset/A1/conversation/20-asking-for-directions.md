# Chapter 20: Asking for and Giving Directions

## Learning Objectives

- Ask where a place is.
- Understand simple directions.
- Use basic direction expressions.
- Give directions to common places.

## Key Expressions

| Expression | Example |
|---|---|
| Excuse me, where is the bank? | Where is the bank? |
| How can I get to the station? | How can I get to the station? |
| Go straight. | Go straight for two blocks. |
| Turn left. | Turn left at the corner. |
| Turn right. | Turn right at the traffic light. |
| It's next to... | It's next to the pharmacy. |
| It's across from... | It's across from the park. |
| It's on your left. | The bank is on your left. |
| It's on your right. | The school is on your right. |
| Is it far? | No, it's very close. |

## Conversation 1: Finding the Bank

**Tourist:** Excuse me. Where is the bank?

**Local:** Go straight for two blocks.

**Tourist:** Okay. Then what?

**Local:** Turn left at the traffic light.

**Tourist:** Is it far from there?

**Local:** No. It's on your right.

**Tourist:** Thank you very much!

**Local:** You're welcome!

## Conversation 2: Finding the Train Station

**Emma:** Excuse me. How can I get to the train station?

**Man:** Walk straight and turn right at the next street.

**Emma:** Is the station near the supermarket?

**Man:** Yes, it's across from the supermarket.

**Emma:** Thank you for your help.

**Man:** No problem!

## Conversation 3: Asking About a Pharmacy

**Jack:** Is there a pharmacy near here?

**Woman:** Yes. It's next to the bookstore.

**Jack:** How do I get there?

**Woman:** Go straight and turn left at the corner.

**Jack:** Great. Thank you!

**Woman:** You're welcome.

## Useful Vocabulary

| Word | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Direction | Information about how to get somewhere |
| Straight | Without turning |
| Left | The opposite direction of right |
| Right | The opposite direction of left |
| Corner | The place where two streets meet |
| Traffic light | A signal that controls road traffic |
| Block | A section of a city street |
| Station | A place where trains or buses stop |
| Across from | On the opposite side |
| Next to | Beside something |
| Pharmacy | A place where medicine is sold |

## Understanding the Conversations

### A. Multiple Choice

1. Where does the tourist want to go?
   - a) The bank
   - b) The school
   - c) The supermarket

2. What should the tourist do at the traffic light?
   - a) Turn right
   - b) Turn left
   - c) Stop and go home

3. Where is the train station?
   - a) Next to the bookstore
   - b) Across from the supermarket
   - c) Behind the bank

4. Where is the pharmacy?
   - a) Next to the bookstore
   - b) Inside the station
   - c) Across from the bank

### B. True or False

1. The bank is on the tourist's left.
2. The train station is near the supermarket.
3. Jack is looking for a pharmacy.
4. The pharmacy is next to the bookstore.

### C. Short Answers

1. How many blocks should the tourist go straight?
2. What should the tourist do at the traffic light?
3. Where should Emma turn?
4. What should Jack do at the corner?
