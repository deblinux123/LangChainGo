# Chapter 06: Talking About Friends

## Learning Objectives

By the end of this chapter, you can:
- Introduce your friends.
- Describe a friend's appearance and personality.
- Talk about activities with friends.
- Ask questions about someone's friends.

## Key Expressions

| Expression | Example |
|---|---|
| This is my best friend. | This is my best friend, Alex. |
| We met at school. | We met at school last year. |
| He is very friendly. | He is very friendly. |
| She has long hair. | She has long brown hair. |
| We like playing soccer. | We like playing soccer together. |
| We spend time together. | We spend time together on weekends. |
| How often do you meet? | We meet every week. |
| What does your friend look like? | He is tall and has short hair. |

## Conversation 1: Best Friends

**Olivia:** Who is your best friend, Ethan?

**Ethan:** My best friend is Lucas.

**Olivia:** How old is he?

**Ethan:** He is twenty years old.

**Olivia:** What does he look like?

**Ethan:** He is tall and has short brown hair.

**Olivia:** What do you do together?

**Ethan:** We play soccer and watch movies.

## Conversation 2: Meeting a Friend

**Mason:** Hi, Ava! Are you here with a friend?

**Ava:** Yes. This is my friend, Sophia.

**Mason:** Nice to meet you, Sophia.

**Sophia:** Nice to meet you, too.

**Mason:** How long have you been friends?

**Ava:** We are old friends. We met at school.

## Conversation 3: Weekend Plans

**Noah:** Do you want to go to the park on Saturday?

**Liam:** Sure! Can I invite my friend, Jack?

**Noah:** Of course!

**Liam:** Great. We can play basketball.

**Noah:** Sounds good!

## Useful Vocabulary

| Word | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Best friend | Your closest friend |
| Friendly | Kind and pleasant |
| Helpful | Ready to help |
| Funny | Making people laugh |
| Tall | Having greater height |
| Short | Not tall |
| Appearance | How someone looks |
| Personality | A person's character |
| Together | With another person |
| Invite | To ask someone to join you |
