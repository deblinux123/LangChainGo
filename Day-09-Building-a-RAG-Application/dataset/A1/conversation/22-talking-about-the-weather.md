# Chapter 22: Talking About Time and Dates

## Learning Objectives

By the end of this chapter, you will be able to:

- Ask and tell the time.
- Talk about days, months, and dates.
- Ask when an event happens.
- Talk about birthdays and simple schedules.

## 1. Key Expressions

| Expression | Example |
|---|---|
| What time is it? | What time is it now? |
| It's ... o'clock. | It's three o'clock. |
| What day is it? | What day is it today? |
| It's Monday. | Today is Monday. |
| What's the date today? | It's May 10th. |
| When is your birthday? | My birthday is in June. |
| At seven o'clock | The class starts at seven o'clock. |
| On Monday | I work on Monday. |

## 2. Example Conversations

### Conversation 1: Asking the Time

**Anna:** Excuse me. What time is it?

**Tom:** It's nine o'clock.

**Anna:** Thank you.

**Tom:** You're welcome.

### Conversation 2: Talking About the Date

**Lisa:** What's the date today?

**Mark:** It's October 12th.

**Lisa:** What day is it?

**Mark:** It's Monday.

### Conversation 3: Talking About Birthdays

**Emma:** When is your birthday?

**John:** It's on April 5th.

**Emma:** That's nice! How old are you?

**John:** I'm twenty years old.

## 3. Useful Vocabulary

| Word | Meaning |
|---|---|
| time | The hour and minute |
| clock | Something that shows time |
| date | A day, month, and year |
| day | A period of 24 hours |
| week | Seven days |
| month | One of the twelve parts of a year |
| year | Twelve months |
| birthday | The day you were born |
| morning | The early part of the day |
| afternoon | The time after 12 p.m. |
| evening | The later part of the day |
| midnight | 12:00 at night |
| noon | 12:00 in the afternoon |
