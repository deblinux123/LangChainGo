# Chapter 07: Talking About Your Daily Routine

## Learning Objectives

By the end of this chapter, you can:
- Describe your daily activities.
- Ask about another person's routine.
- Talk about times and daily habits.
- Use common routine expressions.

## Key Expressions

| Expression | Example |
|---|---|
| I wake up at... | I wake up at seven. |
| I get dressed. | I get dressed after breakfast. |
| I have breakfast. | I have breakfast at eight. |
| I go to work. | I go to work by bus. |
| I come home. | I come home at six. |
| I have dinner. | I have dinner with my family. |
| I go to bed. | I go to bed at eleven. |
| What time do you...? | What time do you wake up? |
| How often do you...? | How often do you exercise? |

## Conversation 1: Morning Routine

**Lily:** What time do you wake up, Daniel?

**Daniel:** I wake up at seven o'clock.

**Lily:** What do you do next?

**Daniel:** I take a shower and get dressed.

**Lily:** Do you have breakfast at home?

**Daniel:** Yes, I do. I usually eat eggs and bread.

**Lily:** What time do you leave home?

**Daniel:** At eight o'clock.

## Conversation 2: After Work

**Chris:** What do you do after work, Emma?

**Emma:** I usually come home at five thirty.

**Chris:** Do you cook dinner?

**Emma:** Yes. I cook dinner and eat with my family.

**Chris:** What do you do in the evening?

**Emma:** I read a book or watch TV.

**Chris:** What time do you go to bed?

**Emma:** Around ten thirty.

## Conversation 3: Weekend Routine

**Sam:** Is your weekend different from your weekdays?

**Mia:** Yes! I wake up later on weekends.

**Sam:** What do you usually do?

**Mia:** I meet my friends, go shopping, or watch movies.

**Sam:** That sounds relaxing!

## Useful Vocabulary

| Word | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Wake up | Stop sleeping |
| Get dressed | Put on clothes |
| Take a shower | Wash your body |
| Leave | Go away from a place |
| Return | Come back |
| Weekday | Monday to Friday |
| Weekend | Saturday and Sunday |
| Usually | Most of the time |
| Sometimes | On some occasions |
| Rarely | Not often |

### B. True or False

1. Daniel leaves home at eight.
2. Emma never cooks dinner.
3. Emma reads books in the evening.
4. Mia wakes up later on weekends.

1. What does Daniel do after waking up?
2. Who does Emma have dinner with?
