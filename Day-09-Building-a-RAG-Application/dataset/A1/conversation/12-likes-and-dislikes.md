# Chapter 12: Talking About Likes and Dislikes

## Learning Objectives

- Express things you like and dislike.
- Ask other people about their preferences.
- Agree and disagree politely.
- Talk about favorite activities and things.

## Key Expressions

| Expression | Example |
|---|---|
| I like... | I like coffee. |
| I love... | I love music. |
| I really enjoy... | I really enjoy swimming. |
| I don't like... | I don't like cold weather. |
| I hate... | I hate waiting. |
| My favorite... is... | My favorite sport is soccer. |
| Do you like...? | Do you like chocolate? |
| Me too! | I like pizza. — Me too! |
| Not really. | Do you like horror movies? — Not really. |

## Conversation 1: Talking About Food

**Anna:** Do you like pizza, Ben?

**Ben:** Yes, I love pizza!

**Anna:** What's your favorite kind?

**Ben:** I really like cheese pizza. What about you?

**Anna:** I like vegetable pizza.

**Ben:** I don't like mushrooms.

**Anna:** That's okay. I like them!

## Conversation 2: Talking About Music

**Lucas:** What kind of music do you like?

**Sophie:** I love pop music.

**Lucas:** Do you like rock music?

**Sophie:** Not really. I prefer pop music.

**Lucas:** I like both pop and rock.

**Sophie:** That's interesting!

## Conversation 3: Free-Time Preferences

**David:** Do you like watching movies?

**Mia:** Yes, I do. I enjoy comedy movies.

**David:** I like action movies.

**Mia:** Do you like reading books?

**David:** Yes, but I don't read very often.

## Useful Vocabulary

| Word | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Like | To enjoy something |
| Love | To like something very much |
| Dislike | To not like something |
| Prefer | To like one thing more than another |
| Favorite | The thing you like most |
| Comedy | A funny movie or show |
| Action | A movie type with exciting events |
| Pop | A popular style of music |
| Rock | A style of music |
| Both | Two things together |

## Understanding the Conversations

### A. Multiple Choice

1. What kind of pizza does Ben like?
   - a) Vegetable pizza
   - b) Cheese pizza
   - c) Mushroom pizza

2. What kind of music does Sophie love?
   - a) Rock
   - b) Classical
   - c) Pop

3. What kind of movies does Mia enjoy?
   - a) Comedy
   - b) Horror
   - c) Action

4. Does David read books very often?
   - a) Yes, every day.
   - b) No, he doesn't.
   - c) He doesn't like books.

### B. True or False

1. Ben loves pizza.
2. Anna dislikes mushrooms.
3. Sophie prefers pop music.
4. David likes action movies.

### C. Short Answers

1. What is Ben's favorite kind of pizza?
2. What music does Lucas like?
3. What kind of movies does David like?
4. Does Mia like watching movies?
