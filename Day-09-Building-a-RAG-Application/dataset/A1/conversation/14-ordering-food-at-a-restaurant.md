# Chapter 14: Ordering Food at a Restaurant

## Learning Objectives

- Ask for a table.
- Read a simple menu.
- Order food and drinks politely.
- Ask for the check.

## Key Expressions

| Expression | Use |
|---|---|
| A table for two, please. | Asking for a table |
| Can I see the menu? | Asking for the menu |
| I'd like... | Ordering something |
| I'll have... | Choosing food |
| What do you recommend? | Asking for advice |
| Can I have some water? | Requesting a drink |
| That's all, thank you. | Finishing an order |
| Can we have the check, please? | Asking to pay |
| Is service included? | Asking about the service charge |

## Conversation 1: Getting a Table

**Host:** Good evening! Welcome to Green Cafe.

**Customer:** Good evening. A table for two, please.

**Host:** Of course. Please follow me.

**Customer:** Thank you.

**Host:** Here is your table.

## Conversation 2: Ordering Dinner

**Server:** Are you ready to order?

**Emma:** Yes. I'd like the chicken salad, please.

**Server:** Certainly. What would you like to drink?

**Emma:** I'll have orange juice.

**Server:** And for you?

**Jack:** I'll have a cheeseburger and water, please.

**Server:** Great. I'll be right back.

## Conversation 3: Asking for the Check

**Server:** Was everything okay?

**Mia:** Yes, everything was delicious.

**Server:** I'm glad to hear that.

**Mia:** Can we have the check, please?

**Server:** Of course. Here you are.

**Mia:** Thank you.

## Useful Vocabulary

| Word | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Restaurant | A place where people buy meals |
| Menu | A list of food and drinks |
| Host | A person who welcomes customers |
| Server | A person who serves food |
| Order | To ask for food or drinks |
| Main course | The main part of a meal |
| Dessert | Sweet food after a meal |
| Check | A paper showing how much you must pay |
| Table | Furniture where people eat |
| Recommend | To suggest something |
