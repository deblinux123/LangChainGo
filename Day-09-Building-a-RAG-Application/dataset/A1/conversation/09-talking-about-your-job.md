# Chapter 09: Talking About Your Job

## Learning Objectives

By the end of this chapter, you can:
- Talk about your job or occupation.
- Ask someone what they do.
- Describe a simple workplace.
- Talk about working hours.

## Key Expressions

| Expression | Example |
|---|---|
| What do you do? | I'm a teacher. |
| Where do you work? | I work at a hospital. |
| I work as a... | I work as a designer. |
| I work in... | I work in an office. |
| I start work at... | I start work at nine. |
| I finish work at... | I finish work at five. |
| Do you like your job? | Yes, I do. |
| I'm looking for a job. | I'm looking for a job. |
| I'm retired. | I'm retired now. |

## Conversation 1: Meeting Someone at Work

**David:** Hi! My name is David. What do you do?

**Sarah:** I'm a teacher. What about you?

**David:** I'm an engineer.

**Sarah:** That's interesting. Where do you work?

**David:** I work for a small company in Chicago.

**Sarah:** Do you like your job?

**David:** Yes, I do. It's interesting.

## Conversation 2: Working Hours

**Anna:** What time do you start work, Mike?

**Mike:** I start at eight in the morning.

**Anna:** What time do you finish?

**Mike:** I finish at four thirty.

**Anna:** Do you work on weekends?

**Mike:** No, I don't. I have weekends off.

**Anna:** That's great!

## Conversation 3: Looking for a Job

**Tom:** What do you do, Lisa?

**Lisa:** I'm a student, but I'm looking for a part-time job.

**Tom:** What kind of job do you want?

**Lisa:** I'd like to work in a bookstore.

**Tom:** Good luck!

**Lisa:** Thank you!

## Useful Vocabulary

| Word | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Job | Work that someone does |
| Occupation | A person's profession |
| Engineer | Someone who designs or builds things |
| Teacher | Someone who teaches |
| Nurse | Someone who cares for sick people |
| Designer | Someone who creates designs |
| Office | A place where people work |
| Company | A business |
| Part-time | Working fewer hours than a full-time job |
| Full-time | Working normal full working hours |
| Retired | No longer working because of age or choice |

1. David is an engineer.
2. Sarah works at a hospital.
3. Mike works on weekends.
