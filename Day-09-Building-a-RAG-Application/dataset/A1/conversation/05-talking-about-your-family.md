# Chapter 05: Talking About Your Family

## Learning Objectives

By the end of this chapter, you can:
- Talk about family members.
- Ask questions about someone's family.
- Describe family members using simple adjectives.
- Explain who lives in your home.

## Key Expressions

## Conversation 1: Talking About Family

**Emma:** Do you have a big family, Daniel?

**Daniel:** Yes, I do. There are five people in my family.

**Emma:** Who are they?

**Daniel:** My parents, my two sisters, and me.

**Emma:** Are your sisters older or younger?

**Daniel:** One is older, and one is younger.

**Emma:** That's nice!

## Conversation 2: Looking at Family Photos

**Lily:** Who is this in the photo?

**Jack:** That's my father.

**Lily:** What is his name?

**Jack:** His name is Robert. He is very friendly.

**Lily:** And who is this?

**Jack:** That's my mother. Her name is Susan.

**Lily:** Your family looks lovely!

## Conversation 3: Family Activities

**Noah:** What do you do with your family on weekends?

**Mia:** We usually have lunch together.

**Noah:** Do you go out?

**Mia:** Sometimes. We visit our grandparents or go to the park.

**Noah:** That sounds fun!

## Understanding the Conversations

### A. Multiple Choice

1. How many people are in Daniel's family?
   - a) Three
   - b) Four
   - c) Five

2. How many sisters does Daniel have?
   - a) One
   - b) Two
   - c) Three

3. What is Jack's father's name?
   - a) Robert
   - b) Peter
   - c) David

4. What does Mia do with her family on weekends?
   - a) They go to work.
   - b) They have lunch together.
   - c) They study together.

### B. True or False

1. Daniel has two sisters.
2. Both of Daniel's sisters are younger.
3. Jack's mother is Susan.
4. Mia sometimes visits her grandparents.

### C. Short Answers

1. Who is in Daniel's family?
2. How does Jack describe his father?
3. Where does Mia sometimes go with her family?
