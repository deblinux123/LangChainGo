# Chapter 24: Inviting Someone

## Learning Objectives

By the end of this chapter, you will be able to:

- Invite someone to an activity.
- Ask someone to come to your home.
- Suggest a place or event.
- Use polite invitation expressions.

## 1. Key Expressions

| Expression | Example |
|---|---|
| Would you like to ...? | Would you like to have dinner? |
| Do you want to ...? | Do you want to watch a movie? |
| Come to my house. | Come to my house on Sunday. |
| Can you come? | Can you come tonight? |
| Let's go ... | Let's go to the park. |
| Join us! | Join us for lunch! |
| I'd love to invite you. | I'd love to invite you to my party. |
| Are you available? | Are you available on Saturday? |

## 2. Example Conversations

### Conversation 1: Inviting a Friend

**Anna:** Would you like to watch a movie tonight?

**David:** That sounds great!

**Anna:** The movie starts at seven.

**David:** What time should we meet?

**Anna:** Let's meet at six thirty.

### Conversation 2: A Birthday Party

**Emma:** I'm having a birthday party on Saturday.

**Jack:** That's wonderful!

**Emma:** Would you like to come?

**Jack:** Yes! Thank you for inviting me.

**Emma:** Great! See you there.

### Conversation 3: Inviting Someone for Dinner

**Peter:** Do you want to have dinner with us?

**Sara:** Yes, I'd love to.

**Peter:** Can you come at eight?

**Sara:** Sure. Thank you!

## 3. Useful Vocabulary

| Word | Meaning |
|---|---|
| invite | Ask someone to come |
| invitation | A request to come to an event |
| party | A social event |
| guest | A person invited to an event |
| dinner | An evening meal |
| movie | A film |
| event | Something special that happens |
| tonight | This evening |
| available | Free to do something |
| join | Come and do something with others |
| celebrate | Do something special for an event |
| host | A person who invites guests |
