# Chapter 21: Talking About Transportation

## Learning Objectives

- Name common forms of transportation.
- Talk about how you travel.
- Ask about travel time and cost.
- Discuss simple transportation preferences.

## Key Expressions

| Expression | Example |
|---|---|
| How do you get to work? | I go by bus. |
| I take the bus. | I take the bus every morning. |
| I drive to work. | I drive to work every day. |
| I walk to school. | I walk to school. |
| How long does it take? | It takes twenty minutes. |
| How much is the ticket? | It's three dollars. |
| Where is the bus stop? | It's across from the park. |
| Is this seat free? | Yes, it is. |
| I'd rather take a taxi. | I'd rather take a taxi. |

## Conversation 1: Going to Work

**Emma:** How do you get to work, David?

**David:** I usually take the bus.

**Emma:** How long does it take?

**David:** About thirty minutes.

**Emma:** Is the bus crowded?

**David:** Sometimes, especially in the morning.

**Emma:** I usually walk to work. It takes fifteen minutes.

## Conversation 2: At the Bus Stop

**Jack:** Excuse me. Does this bus go to the city center?

**Woman:** Yes, it does.

**Jack:** How much is a ticket?

**Woman:** It's two dollars.

**Jack:** Thank you. How long does the trip take?

**Woman:** About twenty minutes.

## Conversation 3: Choosing Transportation

**Mia:** How do you usually travel around the city?

**Noah:** I usually ride my bike.

**Mia:** That's good exercise!

**Noah:** Yes. But when it rains, I take the subway.

**Mia:** I prefer taking the bus because it's easy.

**Noah:** That's a good choice, too.

## Useful Vocabulary

| Word | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Transportation | Ways of traveling |
| Bus | A large vehicle for passengers |
| Train | A vehicle that travels on railway tracks |
| Subway | An underground city train |
| Taxi | A car that takes passengers for money |
| Bicycle | A vehicle with two wheels |
| Ticket | A document that allows travel |
| Passenger | A person traveling in a vehicle |
| Crowded | Full of people |
| Trip | A journey from one place to another |
| Traffic | Vehicles moving on roads |

## Speaking Practice

2. Do you use public transportation?
3. How long is your daily trip?

5. Why do you like that type of transportation?
