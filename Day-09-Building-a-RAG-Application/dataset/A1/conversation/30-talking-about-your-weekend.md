# Chapter 30: Talking About Holidays

## Learning Objectives

By the end of this chapter, you will be able to:

- Talk about holidays and special days.
- Ask people about their favorite holidays.
- Describe simple holiday activities.
- Talk about plans for a holiday.

## 1. Key Expressions

| Expression | Example |
|---|---|
| What's your favorite holiday? | What's your favorite holiday? |
| I like Christmas. | I like Christmas. |
| Happy New Year! | Happy New Year! |
| Happy birthday! | Happy birthday! |
| We celebrate ... | We celebrate New Year's Day. |
| I visit my family. | I visit my family on holidays. |
| I stay at home. | I stay at home during the holiday. |
| Have a great holiday! | Have a great holiday! |

## 2. Example Conversations

### Conversation 1: Favorite Holiday

**Alex:** What's your favorite holiday?

**Mia:** My favorite holiday is Christmas.

**Alex:** Why do you like it?

**Mia:** I like the lights and family dinner.

### Conversation 2: New Year's Day

**Sam:** What do you do on New Year's Day?

**Lucy:** I visit my family.

**Sam:** Do you eat special food?

**Lucy:** Yes, we do.

### Conversation 3: Holiday Plans

**Ben:** What are you doing during the holiday?

**Kate:** I'm going to visit my grandparents.

**Ben:** That sounds nice!

**Kate:** What about you?

**Ben:** I'm going to stay at home.

## 3. Useful Vocabulary

| Word | Meaning |
|---|---|
| holiday | A special day or time away from work or school |
| celebration | An event for a special occasion |
| festival | A special public celebration |
| Christmas | A holiday celebrated on December 25th |
| New Year's Day | The first day of the year |
| birthday | The day someone was born |
| family | People related to you |
| gift | Something you give to someone |
| decoration | Something used to make a place look nice |
| fireworks | Bright lights in the sky |
| tradition | Something people do regularly |
| vacation | Time away from work or school |
