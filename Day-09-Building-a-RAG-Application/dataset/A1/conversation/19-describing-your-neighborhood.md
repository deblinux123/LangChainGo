# Chapter 19: Describing Your Neighborhood

## Learning Objectives

- Describe your neighborhood.
- Name places near your home.
- Talk about what you like and dislike about an area.
- Ask people about their neighborhood.

## Key Expressions

| Expression | Example |
|---|---|
| Where do you live? | I live in a quiet neighborhood. |
| Is there a park nearby? | Yes, there is. |
| There is a supermarket near my home. | There is a supermarket near my home. |
| It's close to... | My house is close to the school. |
| It's far from... | The station is far from here. |
| It's a quiet area. | It's a quiet area. |
| There are many shops. | There are many shops on my street. |
| I like my neighborhood because... | I like it because it's safe. |

## Conversation 1: Talking About the Area

**Emma:** Do you like your neighborhood, Daniel?

**Daniel:** Yes, I do. It's quiet and clean.

**Emma:** Are there any parks nearby?

**Daniel:** Yes. There is a small park near my apartment.

**Emma:** Are there any stores?

**Daniel:** Yes, there is a supermarket and a bakery.

**Emma:** That sounds convenient!

## Conversation 2: A New Neighbor

**Mia:** Hi! I'm Mia. I just moved into this building.

**Jack:** Nice to meet you, Mia. I'm Jack.

**Mia:** Is there a bus stop near here?

**Jack:** Yes, it's across from the supermarket.

**Mia:** Great! Is there a coffee shop nearby?

**Jack:** Yes, there's one on the next street.

**Mia:** Thank you for your help!

## Conversation 3: Comparing Neighborhoods

**Noah:** Is your neighborhood busy?

**Lily:** Yes, it is. There are many stores and restaurants.

**Noah:** Do you like living there?

**Lily:** Yes, but sometimes it's noisy.

**Noah:** My neighborhood is quieter, but there aren't many stores.

**Lily:** Every place has good and bad points!

## Useful Vocabulary

| Word | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Neighborhood | An area where people live |
| Nearby | Not far away |
| Quiet | Making little noise |
| Noisy | Making a lot of noise |
| Clean | Not dirty |
| Safe | Protected from danger |
| Convenient | Easy to use or reach |
| Bakery | A shop that sells bread and cakes |
| Bus stop | A place where buses stop |
| Across from | On the opposite side |
| Street | A road in a town or city |

## Understanding the Conversations

### A. Multiple Choice

1. How does Daniel describe his neighborhood?
   - a) Busy and noisy
   - b) Quiet and clean
   - c) Dirty and dangerous

2. What is near Daniel's apartment?
   - a) A park
   - b) An airport
   - c) A hospital

3. Where is the bus stop?
   - a) Behind the building
   - b) Across from the supermarket
   - c) Inside the coffee shop

4. Why does Lily sometimes dislike her neighborhood?
   - a) It is too quiet.
   - b) It is noisy.
   - c) There are no stores.

### B. True or False

1. Daniel has a supermarket nearby.
2. Mia lives in the building before the conversation.
3. There is a coffee shop on the next street.
4. Noah's neighborhood has many stores.

### C. Short Answers

1. What places are near Daniel's apartment?
2. Why does Mia ask Jack questions?
3. What is one disadvantage of Lily's neighborhood?
4. What is one disadvantage of Noah's neighborhood?
