# Chapter 33: At the Hotel

## Learning Objectives

By the end of this chapter, you will be able to:

- Check in at a hotel.
- Ask about hotel rooms and services.
- Give basic personal information.
- Ask simple questions about your room.

## 1. Key Expressions

| Expression | Example |
|---|---|
| I have a reservation. | I have a reservation for two nights. |
| I'd like a room. | I'd like a room for one person. |
| Do you have a reservation? | Do you have a reservation? |
| Can I see your ID? | Can I see your ID, please? |
| What time is check-out? | What time is check-out? |
| Is breakfast included? | Is breakfast included? |
| Where is my room? | Where is my room? |
| Thank you for your help. | Thank you for your help. |

## 2. Example Conversations

### Conversation 1: Checking In

**Receptionist:** Good afternoon. Welcome to our hotel.

**Guest:** Hello. I have a reservation.

**Receptionist:** What's your name?

**Guest:** My name is David Brown.

**Receptionist:** Can I see your ID, please?

**Guest:** Yes. Here you are.

**Receptionist:** Thank you. Your room is number 205.

### Conversation 2: Asking About Breakfast

**Guest:** Excuse me. Is breakfast included?

**Receptionist:** Yes, it is.

**Guest:** What time is breakfast?

**Receptionist:** From seven to ten in the morning.

**Guest:** Thank you!

### Conversation 3: Checking Out

**Receptionist:** Good morning. Are you checking out?

**Guest:** Yes, I am.

**Receptionist:** How was your stay?

**Guest:** It was very nice. Thank you.

**Receptionist:** Have a safe trip!

**Guest:** Thank you. Goodbye!

## 3. Useful Vocabulary

| Word | Meaning |
|---|---|
| hotel | A place where people pay to stay |
| guest | A person staying at a hotel |
| receptionist | A person who works at the front desk |
| reservation | An arrangement to use a room |
| room | A space in a building |
| key | Something used to open a door |
| elevator | A machine that moves people between floors |
| breakfast | The first meal of the day |
| check-in | The process of arriving at a hotel |
| check-out | The process of leaving a hotel |
| ID | A document that identifies a person |
| stay | Time spent at a place |
