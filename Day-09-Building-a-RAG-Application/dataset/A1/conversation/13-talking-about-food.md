# Chapter 13: Talking About Food

## Learning Objectives

- Name common foods and drinks.
- Talk about favorite meals.
- Describe food using simple adjectives.
- Ask and answer questions about eating habits.

## Key Expressions

| Expression | Example |
|---|---|
| What's your favorite food? | My favorite food is pasta. |
| What do you usually eat? | I usually eat rice and chicken. |
| I'm hungry. | Let's get something to eat. |
| I'm thirsty. | I'd like some water. |
| It tastes delicious. | This soup tastes delicious. |
| It's spicy. | This food is very spicy. |
| Would you like some...? | Would you like some tea? |
| I'm full. | Thank you, I'm full. |

## Conversation 1: Lunch at School

**Emma:** What do you usually eat for lunch?

**Jack:** I usually eat a sandwich and an apple.

**Emma:** Do you bring lunch from home?

**Jack:** Yes, I do. What about you?

**Emma:** I sometimes buy lunch at school.

**Jack:** What's your favorite food?

**Emma:** I love pasta.

## Conversation 2: Cooking at Home

**Mia:** What are you cooking, Daniel?

**Daniel:** I'm making chicken soup.

**Mia:** That smells delicious!

**Daniel:** Thank you. Would you like some?

**Mia:** Yes, please. I'm hungry.

**Daniel:** Here you are.

## Conversation 3: Favorite Drinks

**Noah:** What do you usually drink in the morning?

**Lily:** I usually drink coffee.

**Noah:** Do you like tea?

**Lily:** Yes, but I prefer coffee.

**Noah:** I prefer orange juice.

## Useful Vocabulary

| Word | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Meal | Food eaten at a particular time |
| Breakfast | The first meal of the day |
| Lunch | A meal in the middle of the day |
| Dinner | The main evening meal |
| Delicious | Very good to eat |
| Spicy | Having a strong hot taste |
| Sweet | Having a sugar taste |
| Sour | Having an acidic taste |
| Hungry | Needing food |
| Thirsty | Needing a drink |
| Full | Having eaten enough |

## Understanding the Conversations

### A. Multiple Choice

1. What does Jack usually eat for lunch?
   - a) Soup
   - b) A sandwich and an apple
   - c) Pasta

2. What is Emma's favorite food?
   - a) Pasta
   - b) Rice
   - c) Chicken

3. What is Daniel making?
   - a) Pizza
   - b) Salad
   - c) Chicken soup

4. What does Lily prefer?
   - a) Tea
   - b) Coffee
   - c) Juice

### B. True or False

1. Jack brings lunch from home.
2. Emma always buys lunch at school.
3. Mia thinks the soup smells delicious.
4. Noah prefers orange juice.

### C. Short Answers

1. What does Jack eat for lunch?
2. What does Daniel offer Mia?
3. What does Lily usually drink in the morning?
4. What is Noah's favorite drink?
