# Chapter 18: Talking About Your Home

## Learning Objectives

- Describe your home.
- Name common rooms and furniture.
- Ask where someone lives.
- Talk about your favorite room.

## Key Expressions

| Expression | Example |
|---|---|
| Where do you live? | I live in an apartment. |
| I live in a house. | I live in a small house. |
| How many rooms are there? | There are four rooms. |
| My favorite room is... | My favorite room is the kitchen. |
| There is a... | There is a sofa in the living room. |
| There are... | There are two bedrooms. |
| It's next to... | The kitchen is next to the dining room. |
| It's comfortable. | My bedroom is comfortable. |

## Conversation 1: Talking About an Apartment

**Anna:** Where do you live, Peter?

**Peter:** I live in an apartment near the city center.

**Anna:** How many bedrooms are there?

**Peter:** There are two bedrooms.

**Anna:** Do you have a balcony?

**Peter:** Yes, I do. I like sitting there in the morning.

**Anna:** That sounds nice!

## Conversation 2: Favorite Room

**Mia:** What's your favorite room in your house, Jack?

**Jack:** My favorite room is the living room.

**Mia:** Why do you like it?

**Jack:** Because it's comfortable. There is a big sofa and a television.

**Mia:** Do you watch TV there?

**Jack:** Yes, I do. I also read books there.

## Conversation 3: Visiting a Friend

**Emma:** Your house is beautiful!

**Liam:** Thank you. Would you like to see the kitchen?

**Emma:** Sure!

**Liam:** It's next to the dining room.

**Emma:** I like the big table.

**Liam:** We usually eat dinner there.

## Useful Vocabulary

| Word | Meaning |
|---|---|
| House | A building where people live |
| Apartment | A home inside a larger building |
| Bedroom | A room for sleeping |
| Living room | A room for relaxing |
| Kitchen | A room for cooking |
| Bathroom | A room with a shower or toilet |
| Dining room | A room for eating |
| Balcony | A platform outside an apartment |
| Sofa | A comfortable seat |
| Television | A device for watching programs |
| Comfortable | Making you feel relaxed |

## Understanding the Conversations

### A. Multiple Choice

1. Where does Peter live?
   - a) In a house
   - b) In an apartment
   - c) In a hotel

2. How many bedrooms does Peter have?
   - a) One
   - b) Two
   - c) Three

3. What is Jack's favorite room?
   - a) The bedroom
   - b) The kitchen
   - c) The living room

4. Where is the kitchen in Liam's house?
   - a) Next to the dining room
   - b) Next to the bathroom
   - c) Upstairs

### B. True or False

1. Peter lives near the city center.
2. Peter doesn't have a balcony.
3. Jack's living room has a sofa and television.
4. Liam and his family eat dinner in the dining room.

### C. Short Answers

1. What does Peter like doing on his balcony?
2. Why does Jack like his living room?
3. What does Emma like in the dining room?
