# Chapter 39: Everyday Problems and Solutions

## Learning Objectives

By the end of this chapter, you will be able to:

- Describe common everyday problems.
- Ask someone for help.
- Suggest simple solutions.
- Explain what you need.

## 1. Key Expressions

| Expression | Example |
|---|---|
| What's wrong? | What's wrong with your phone? |
| I have a problem. | I have a problem with my computer. |
| It doesn't work. | My printer doesn't work. |
| I lost my keys. | I lost my keys yesterday. |
| I can't find it. | I can't find my wallet. |
| What should I do? | What should I do now? |
| Try this. | Try this solution. |
| Let me check. | Let me check your computer. |

## 2. Example Conversations

### Conversation 1: A Broken Phone

**Alex:** What's wrong?

**Mia:** My phone doesn't work.

**Alex:** Is the battery empty?

**Mia:** Yes, I think so.

**Alex:** Try charging it.

**Mia:** Good idea. Thank you!

### Conversation 2: Lost Keys

**Sam:** I can't find my keys.

**Lucy:** Where did you last see them?

**Sam:** I saw them in the kitchen.

**Lucy:** Let's look there.

**Sam:** Here they are! Thank you!

### Conversation 3: A Computer Problem

**Ben:** My computer is very slow.

**Kate:** Did you restart it?

**Ben:** No, I didn't.

**Kate:** Try restarting it.

**Ben:** Okay. I'll try that.

## 3. Useful Vocabulary

| Word | Meaning |
|---|---|
| problem | Something that is difficult or wrong |
| solution | An answer to a problem |
| broken | Not working correctly |
| slow | Not fast |
| lost | Unable to find something |
| key | Something used to open a lock |
| wallet | Something used to carry money and cards |
| battery | A device that stores energy |
| charger | A device used to charge a battery |
| restart | Turn something off and on again |
| repair | Fix something |
| check | Look at something to understand it |
