# Chapter 28: Talking About Sports

## Learning Objectives

By the end of this chapter, you will be able to:

- Talk about sports you like.
- Ask someone about their favorite sport.
- Describe simple sports activities.
- Talk about how often you exercise.

## 1. Key Expressions

| Expression | Example |
|---|---|
| Do you like sports? | Do you like sports? |
| What's your favorite sport? | What's your favorite sport? |
| I like playing ... | I like playing soccer. |
| I play ... | I play tennis. |
| I go swimming. | I go swimming on Sundays. |
| How often do you ...? | How often do you exercise? |
| Every day | I walk every day. |
| I'm not very good at ... | I'm not very good at basketball. |

## 2. Example Conversations

### Conversation 1: Favorite Sport

**Alex:** Do you like sports?

**Mia:** Yes, I do.

**Alex:** What's your favorite sport?

**Mia:** My favorite sport is soccer.

**Alex:** I like basketball.

### Conversation 2: Playing Tennis

**Sam:** Do you play tennis?

**Lucy:** Yes, I do.

**Sam:** How often do you play?

**Lucy:** Twice a week.

**Sam:** That's great!

### Conversation 3: Exercise

**Ben:** Do you exercise every day?

**Kate:** Yes. I walk every morning.

**Ben:** That's good. I go swimming on weekends.

**Kate:** I like swimming, too!

## 3. Useful Vocabulary

| Word | Meaning |
|---|---|
| sport | An activity or game involving physical exercise |
| soccer | A game played with a ball and two teams |
| basketball | A game in which players throw a ball into a basket |
| tennis | A game played with rackets |
| swimming | Moving through water |
| running | Moving quickly on foot |
| cycling | Riding a bicycle |
| exercise | Physical activity |
| team | A group of players |
| player | A person who plays a sport |
| game | An activity with rules |
| practice | Training to improve a skill |

## 4. Understanding the Conversations

### Exercise A: Multiple Choice

1. What is Mia's favorite sport?

   a) Basketball  
   b) Soccer  
   c) Tennis

2. How often does Lucy play tennis?

   a) Every day  
   b) Once a week  
   c) Twice a week

3. When does Ben go swimming?

   a) On weekends  
   b) Every morning  
   c) On Mondays

### Exercise B: True or False

1. Mia likes sports.
2. Alex's favorite sport is soccer.
3. Lucy plays tennis twice a week.
4. Kate walks every morning.

### Exercise C: Short Answers

1. What's your favorite sport?
2. How often do you exercise?
3. Do you prefer team sports or individual sports?
