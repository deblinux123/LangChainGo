# Chapter 32: At the Airport

## Learning Objectives

By the end of this chapter, you will be able to:

- Ask simple questions at an airport.
- Check in for a flight.
- Ask about gates and boarding times.
- Understand basic airport vocabulary.

## 1. Key Expressions

| Expression | Example |
|---|---|
| Where is the check-in desk? | Where is the check-in desk? |
| I have a ticket. | I have a ticket to London. |
| Here is my passport. | Here is my passport. |
| Where is Gate 10? | Where is Gate 10? |
| What time is boarding? | What time is boarding? |
| Is the flight on time? | Is the flight on time? |
| I have one suitcase. | I have one suitcase. |
| Thank you for your help. | Thank you for your help. |

## 2. Example Conversations

### Conversation 1: Checking In

**Passenger:** Hello. I'd like to check in.

**Agent:** Sure. May I see your passport and ticket?

**Passenger:** Yes. Here you are.

**Agent:** Thank you. Do you have any bags?

**Passenger:** Yes, I have one suitcase.

**Agent:** Okay. Here is your boarding pass.

### Conversation 2: Finding the Gate

**Passenger:** Excuse me. Where is Gate 12?

**Airport Worker:** It's on the second floor.

**Passenger:** Thank you. Is it far?

**Airport Worker:** No, it's near the stairs.

**Passenger:** Thank you!

### Conversation 3: Asking About a Flight

**Tom:** Excuse me. What time is boarding?

**Airport Worker:** Boarding starts at ten thirty.

**Tom:** Is the flight on time?

**Airport Worker:** Yes, it is.

**Tom:** Thank you.

## 3. Useful Vocabulary

| Word | Meaning |
|---|---|
| airport | A place where planes arrive and leave |
| passenger | A person traveling in a vehicle |
| flight | A journey by plane |
| check-in | The process of registering for a flight |
| check-in desk | A place to register for a flight |
| passport | An official travel document |
| ticket | A document for a journey |
| boarding pass | A document that allows you to board a plane |
| gate | The place where passengers enter a plane |
| suitcase | A bag for travel |
| luggage | Bags and personal items for a trip |
| boarding | The process of getting on a plane |
| departure | The act of leaving |
| arrival | The act of reaching a place |
