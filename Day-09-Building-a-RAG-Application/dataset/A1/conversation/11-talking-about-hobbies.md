# Chapter 11: Talking About Hobbies

## Learning Objectives

- Talk about hobbies and free-time activities.
- Ask people about their interests.
- Say how often you do an activity.
- Express simple preferences.

## Key Expressions

| Expression | Example |
|---|---|
| What are your hobbies? | My hobbies are reading and swimming. |
| What do you do in your free time? | I listen to music. |
| I enjoy... | I enjoy playing soccer. |
| I'm interested in... | I'm interested in photography. |
| How often do you...? | I swim twice a week. |
| I do it every day. | I read every day. |
| I'm not interested in... | I'm not interested in painting. |
| That's fun! | That's fun! |

## Conversation 1: Meeting a New Classmate

**Emma:** What do you do in your free time, Jack?

**Jack:** I like playing basketball and reading books.

**Emma:** That's nice! How often do you play basketball?

**Jack:** I play twice a week.

**Emma:** What kind of books do you read?

**Jack:** I usually read adventure stories. What about you?

**Emma:** I enjoy drawing and listening to music.

## Conversation 2: Weekend Activities

**Mia:** Do you have any hobbies, Daniel?

**Daniel:** Yes. I love cooking.

**Mia:** What do you like cooking?

**Daniel:** I like making pasta and pizza.

**Mia:** Do you cook every day?

**Daniel:** No, I usually cook on weekends.

## Conversation 3: A Shared Interest

**Noah:** Are you interested in photography?

**Lily:** Yes, I am! I take pictures of nature.

**Noah:** Me too. We can take pictures together sometime.

**Lily:** Great idea!

## Useful Vocabulary

| Word | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Hobby | An activity you enjoy |
| Free time | Time when you are not working |
| Photography | Taking pictures |
| Drawing | Making pictures with a pencil |
| Cooking | Preparing food |
| Swimming | Moving through water |
| Reading | Looking at and understanding written words |
| Adventure | An exciting experience |
| Interested | Wanting to know or do something |
| Twice | Two times |

## Understanding the Conversations

### A. Multiple Choice

1. What does Jack like doing?
   - a) Cooking and dancing
   - b) Basketball and reading
   - c) Swimming and running

2. How often does Jack play basketball?
   - a) Every day
   - b) Once a week
   - c) Twice a week

3. What does Daniel enjoy?
   - a) Cooking
   - b) Painting
   - c) Singing

4. What does Lily take pictures of?
   - a) Cars
   - b) Nature
   - c) Food

### B. True or False

1. Jack reads adventure stories.
2. Emma enjoys drawing.
3. Daniel cooks every day.
4. Noah is interested in photography.

### C. Short Answers

1. What are Jack's hobbies?
2. What does Daniel like cooking?
3. When does Daniel usually cook?
4. What do Noah and Lily want to do together?
