# Chapter 03: Introducing Yourself

## Learning Objectives

By the end of this chapter, you can:
- Give basic information about yourself.
- Say your name, age, and nationality.
- Talk about your city and occupation.
- Ask another person basic questions.

## Key Expressions

| Expression | Example |
|---|---|
| My name is... | My name is David. |
| I'm... | I'm Sarah. |
| I'm ... years old. | I'm twenty years old. |
| I come from... | I come from Canada. |
| I live in... | I live in Chicago. |
| I'm a student. | I'm a student at a college. |
| I work as a... | I work as a teacher. |
| My favorite hobby is... | My favorite hobby is reading. |
| Nice to meet you. | Nice to meet you, too. |

## Conversation 1: At an English Class

**Teacher:** Good morning, everyone. Let's introduce ourselves. What's your name?

**Emily:** My name is Emily.

**Teacher:** Nice to meet you, Emily. How old are you?

**Emily:** I'm nineteen years old.

**Teacher:** Where are you from?

**Emily:** I'm from the United States.

**Teacher:** Great! What do you like doing?

**Emily:** I like reading and listening to music.

## Conversation 2: Meeting at a Party

**Ryan:** Hi! I'm Ryan. What's your name?

**Olivia:** Hello! I'm Olivia.

**Ryan:** Where do you live, Olivia?

**Olivia:** I live in Boston. How about you?

**Ryan:** I live in New York. What do you do?

**Olivia:** I'm a university student.

**Ryan:** That's nice!

## Conversation 3: Introducing a Friend

**Chris:** Anna, this is my friend, Peter.

**Anna:** Hi, Peter! Nice to meet you.

**Peter:** Nice to meet you, too.

**Chris:** Peter is from Texas. He is a teacher.

**Anna:** That's great!

## Useful Vocabulary

| Word | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Age | How old someone is |
| Nationality | The country someone belongs to |
| Occupation | A person's job |
| Student | Someone who studies |
| Teacher | Someone who teaches |
| City | A large town |
| Hobby | An activity you enjoy |
| Favorite | The one you like most |
