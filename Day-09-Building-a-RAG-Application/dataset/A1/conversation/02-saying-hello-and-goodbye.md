# Chapter 02: Saying Hello and Goodbye

## Learning Objectives

By the end of this chapter, you can:
- Choose appropriate greetings for different times of day.
- Use formal and informal expressions.
- End conversations politely.
- Respond naturally to common greetings.

## Key Expressions

| Expression | Meaning / Use |
|---|---|
| Hey! | Very informal hello |
| Hello there! | Friendly greeting |
| What's up? | Informal way to ask how someone is |
| How's it going? | Asking how things are |
| I'm doing well. | Positive response |
| Not bad. | A neutral or positive response |
| See you soon. | Goodbye for now |
| Take care. | Friendly goodbye |
| Talk to you later. | Goodbye, often on the phone |
| Have a good night. | Evening or nighttime goodbye |

## Conversation 1: At School

**Mike:** Hey, Jessica! What's up?

**Jessica:** Hi, Mike! Not much. How about you?

**Mike:** I'm doing well. Are you going to class?

**Jessica:** Yes, I am. See you later!

**Mike:** Sure. Have a good day!

## Conversation 2: Meeting a Neighbor

**Mr. Brown:** Good afternoon, Mrs. Green.

**Mrs. Green:** Good afternoon, Mr. Brown. How are you?

**Mr. Brown:** I'm doing well, thank you.

**Mrs. Green:** That's good to hear.

**Mr. Brown:** Have a nice afternoon.

**Mrs. Green:** Thank you. You, too.

## Conversation 3: Ending a Phone Call

**Alex:** I have to go now.

**Mia:** Okay. Talk to you later.

**Alex:** Sure. Take care!

**Mia:** You, too. Bye!

## Useful Vocabulary

| Word | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Neighbor | Someone who lives near you |
| Afternoon | The time after noon |
| Evening | The later part of the day |
| Night | The dark part of the day |
| Later | After now |
| Soon | In a short time |
| Conversation | A talk between people |
| Respond | To answer someone |
