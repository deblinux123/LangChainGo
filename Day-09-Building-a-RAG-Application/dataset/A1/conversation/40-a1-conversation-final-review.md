## 2. Grammar and Conversation Review

### A. Present Simple

Use the present simple to talk about routines and facts.

**Examples:**

- I work every day.
- She studies English.
- They live in Berlin.

### B. Present Continuous

Use the present continuous for actions happening now.

**Examples:**

- I am studying now.
- She is cooking dinner.
- They are playing soccer.

### C. Past Simple

Use the past simple to talk about finished past events.

**Examples:**

- I watched a movie yesterday.
- She visited her friend.
- We went to the park.

### D. Future Plans with Going To

Use "going to" to talk about plans.

**Examples:**

- I'm going to study tomorrow.
- She is going to travel next month.
- We are going to meet on Friday.

## 3. Conversation Practice

### Conversation 1: Meeting Someone

**Anna:** Hello! My name is Anna. What's your name?

**David:** Hi, I'm David. Nice to meet you.

**Anna:** Nice to meet you, too. Where are you from?

**David:** I'm from Canada. What about you?

**Anna:** I'm from the United States.

### Conversation 2: Daily Life

**Lisa:** What time do you get up?

**Mark:** I get up at seven.

**Lisa:** What do you do in the morning?

**Mark:** I have breakfast and go to work.

**Lisa:** Do you like your job?

**Mark:** Yes, I do.

### Conversation 3: Making Plans

**Emma:** Are you free this weekend?

**John:** Yes, I am.

**Emma:** Let's go to the park.

**John:** Good idea! What time?

**Emma:** At ten in the morning.

**John:** Perfect. See you there!

### Conversation 4: At a Restaurant

**Waiter:** Hello! Are you ready to order?

**Customer:** Yes. I'd like a chicken sandwich, please.

**Waiter:** Would you like something to drink?

**Customer:** Yes, a glass of water, please.

**Waiter:** Certainly.

### Conversation 5: Talking About the Past

**Tom:** What did you do yesterday?

**Sara:** I visited my friend.

**Tom:** What did you do together?

**Sara:** We watched a movie and had dinner.

**Tom:** That sounds fun!
