# Chapter 29: Talking About Movies and TV

## Learning Objectives

By the end of this chapter, you will be able to:

- Talk about movies and TV shows.
- Ask someone about their favorite movie.
- Describe simple types of movies.
- Invite someone to watch a movie.

## 1. Key Expressions

| Expression | Example |
|---|---|
| Do you like movies? | Do you like movies? |
| What's your favorite movie? | What's your favorite movie? |
| I like comedies. | I like comedies. |
| I watch TV. | I watch TV every evening. |
| It's funny. | This movie is funny. |
| It's interesting. | The show is interesting. |
| Who is your favorite actor? | Who is your favorite actor? |
| Let's watch a movie. | Let's watch a movie tonight. |

## 2. Example Conversations

### Conversation 1: Favorite Movies

**Anna:** Do you like movies?

**David:** Yes, I do.

**Anna:** What's your favorite movie?

**David:** I like action movies.

**Anna:** I like comedies.

### Conversation 2: Watching TV

**Lisa:** Do you watch TV every day?

**Mark:** Yes, I do.

**Lisa:** What's your favorite TV show?

**Mark:** I like nature shows.

**Lisa:** That's interesting!

### Conversation 3: Going to the Movies

**Emma:** Would you like to watch a movie tonight?

**John:** Sure!

**Emma:** Let's meet at the movie theater at seven.

**John:** Great! See you there.

## 3. Useful Vocabulary

| Word | Meaning |
|---|---|
| movie | A film |
| TV show | A program on television |
| actor | A person who acts in movies |
| actress | A woman who acts in movies |
| comedy | A funny movie or show |
| action movie | A movie with exciting events |
| cartoon | An animated movie or show |
| documentary | A program about real people or events |
| funny | Making you laugh |
| interesting | Making you want to know more |
| theater | A place where people watch movies |
| episode | One part of a TV series |
