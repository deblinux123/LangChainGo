# Chapter 23: Making Plans

## Learning Objectives

By the end of this chapter, you will be able to:

- Talk about simple future plans.
- Ask someone about their plans.
- Suggest an activity.
- Talk about when and where to meet.

## 1. Key Expressions

| Expression | Example |
|---|---|
| What are you doing tomorrow? | What are you doing tomorrow? |
| I'm going to ... | I'm going to study. |
| Let's ... | Let's go shopping. |
| Good idea! | Good idea! |
| What about ...? | What about Saturday? |
| Are you free? | Are you free tomorrow? |
| See you then. | See you at six. |
| Where shall we meet? | Where shall we meet? |

## 2. Example Conversations

### Conversation 1: Weekend Plans

**Alex:** What are you doing this weekend?

**Mia:** I'm going to visit my parents.

**Alex:** That sounds nice.

**Mia:** What about you?

**Alex:** I'm going to watch a movie.

### Conversation 2: Making a Plan

**Sam:** Are you free tomorrow?

**Lucy:** Yes, I am.

**Sam:** Let's have lunch together.

**Lucy:** Good idea! What time?

**Sam:** At one o'clock.

**Lucy:** Great!

### Conversation 3: Choosing a Day

**Ben:** Let's play tennis on Friday.

**Kate:** I'm busy on Friday.

**Ben:** What about Saturday?

**Kate:** Saturday is perfect!

**Ben:** See you at ten.

**Kate:** See you!

## 3. Useful Vocabulary

| Word | Meaning |
|---|---|
| plan | Something you want to do |
| tomorrow | The day after today |
| weekend | Saturday and Sunday |
| free | Not busy |
| busy | Having many things to do |
| together | With another person |
| visit | Go to see someone |
| movie | A film |
| lunch | A meal in the middle of the day |
| perfect | Very good |
| meet | See someone at a place |
| later | After some time |
