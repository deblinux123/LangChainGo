# Chapter 10: Talking About School and Studies

## Learning Objectives

By the end of this chapter, you can:
- Talk about your school or college.
- Name common school subjects.
- Ask about classes and schedules.
- Talk about your favorite subject.

## Key Expressions

| Expression | Example |
|---|---|
| Where do you study? | I study at a university. |
| What do you study? | I study English. |
| What is your favorite subject? | My favorite subject is science. |
| I have a class. | I have an English class today. |
| When does class start? | It starts at nine. |
| When does class finish? | It finishes at ten. |
| I have homework. | I have English homework. |
| I need to study. | I need to study for a test. |
| I'm in class. | I'm in class now. |

## Conversation 1: At School

**Emma:** Hi, Jack! What subject do you have today?

**Jack:** I have math and science.

**Emma:** What's your favorite subject?

**Jack:** My favorite subject is science. How about you?

**Emma:** I like English.

**Jack:** Is English difficult?

**Emma:** Sometimes, but it's interesting.

## Conversation 2: Talking About College

**Mia:** Where do you study, Alex?

**Alex:** I study at a community college.

**Mia:** What do you study?

**Alex:** I study computer science.

**Mia:** Do you like it?

**Alex:** Yes! I enjoy learning about computers.

**Mia:** That's great!

## Conversation 3: Class Schedule

**Noah:** What time does your English class start?

**Sophie:** It starts at ten o'clock.

**Noah:** When does it finish?

**Sophie:** It finishes at eleven thirty.

**Noah:** Do you have homework today?

**Sophie:** Yes, I do. I need to read a short story.

## Useful Vocabulary

| Word | Meaning |
|---|---|
| School | A place where students learn |
| College | An educational institution |
| University | An institution for higher education |
| Subject | An area of study |
| Math | The study of numbers |
| Science | The study of the natural world |
| History | The study of the past |
| Homework | Schoolwork done at home |
| Class | A lesson or group of students |
| Schedule | A plan of times and activities |
| Test | An examination |

## Understanding the Conversations

### A. Multiple Choice

1. What subjects does Jack have?
   - a) English and history
   - b) Math and science
   - c) Art and music

2. What is Emma's favorite subject?
   - a) English
   - b) Science
   - c) Math

3. What does Alex study?
   - a) Medicine
   - b) History
   - c) Computer science

4. What time does Sophie's class start?
   - a) Nine
   - b) Ten
   - c) Eleven

### B. True or False

1. Jack likes science.
2. Emma thinks English is always easy.
3. Alex studies at a community college.
4. Sophie's class finishes at eleven thirty.

### C. Short Answers

1. Why does Jack like science?
2. What does Mia ask Alex?
3. What homework does Sophie have?
4. How long is Sophie's English class?
