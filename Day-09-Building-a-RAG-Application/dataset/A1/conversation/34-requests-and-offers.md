# Chapter 34: Talking About Past Events

## Learning Objectives

By the end of this chapter, you will be able to:

- Talk about simple past activities.
- Ask what someone did yesterday.
- Describe a past weekend.
- Use common past-tense verbs in conversation.

## 1. Key Expressions

| Expression | Example |
|---|---|
| What did you do yesterday? | What did you do yesterday? |
| I watched a movie. | I watched a movie last night. |
| I visited my friend. | I visited my friend on Sunday. |
| I went shopping. | I went shopping yesterday. |
| It was fun. | The party was fun. |
| Where did you go? | Where did you go last weekend? |
| Who did you meet? | Who did you meet yesterday? |
| I stayed at home. | I stayed at home last night. |

## 2. Example Conversations

### Conversation 1: Yesterday

**Anna:** What did you do yesterday?

**Tom:** I watched a movie.

**Anna:** Was it good?

**Tom:** Yes, it was very interesting.

### Conversation 2: Last Weekend

**Lisa:** Where did you go last weekend?

**Mark:** I went to the park.

**Lisa:** Who did you go with?

**Mark:** I went with my brother.

**Lisa:** Did you have fun?

**Mark:** Yes, we did!

### Conversation 3: A Birthday Party

**Emma:** Did you go to the party on Saturday?

**John:** Yes, I did.

**Emma:** How was it?

**John:** It was great! I met some new people.

**Emma:** That's wonderful!

## 3. Useful Vocabulary

| Word | Meaning |
|---|---|
| yesterday | The day before today |
| last night | The night before today |
| last week | The previous week |
| last weekend | The weekend before this one |
| visited | Went to see someone or somewhere |
| watched | Looked at something |
| played | Took part in a game |
| went | Past form of go |
| saw | Past form of see |
| met | Past form of meet |
| stayed | Remained in a place |
| enjoyed | Had a good time |

## 10. Chapter Summary

You learned how to talk about yesterday, last weekend, and other past events.

**Remember:** Use the past form of the verb when talking about finished past activities. After "did," use the base form: "What did you do?"
