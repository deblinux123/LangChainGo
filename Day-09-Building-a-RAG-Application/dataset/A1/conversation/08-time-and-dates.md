# Chapter 08: Talking About Time and Dates

## Learning Objectives

By the end of this chapter, you can:
- Ask and tell the time.
- Talk about days and dates.
- Ask when an event happens.
- Make simple plans using time expressions.

## Key Expressions

| Expression | Example |
|---|---|
| What time is it? | It's three o'clock. |
| It's half past four. | It's 4:30. |
| It's quarter past five. | It's 5:15. |
| It's quarter to six. | It's 5:45. |
| What day is it? | It's Monday. |
| What's the date today? | It's June 10. |
| When is your birthday? | It's on August 5. |
| At seven o'clock | The movie starts at seven. |
| On Monday | I work on Monday. |
| In July | My vacation is in July. |

## Conversation 1: Asking the Time

**Ben:** Excuse me, what time is it?

**Lucy:** It's half past two.

**Ben:** Thank you. Is the library open now?

**Lucy:** Yes, it is. It closes at five.

**Ben:** Great. Thanks!

**Lucy:** You're welcome.

## Conversation 2: Planning a Meeting

**Alex:** Are you free tomorrow?

**Mia:** Yes, I am. Why?

**Alex:** Do you want to have lunch together?

**Mia:** Sure! What time?

**Alex:** At twelve thirty.

**Mia:** That sounds good. Where should we meet?

**Alex:** Let's meet at the cafe near the park.

## Conversation 3: Birthday Date

**Emma:** When is your birthday, Jack?

**Jack:** It's on October 12.

**Emma:** What day is it this year?

**Jack:** It's on Monday.

**Emma:** Are you having a party?

**Jack:** Yes! The party starts at six in the evening.

## Useful Vocabulary

| Word | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Clock | Something that shows the time |
| Minute | A unit of time equal to 60 seconds |
| Hour | A unit of time equal to 60 minutes |
| Date | A day, month, and sometimes year |
| Calendar | A chart showing days and months |
| Birthday | The anniversary of your birth |
| Appointment | An arranged meeting |
| Tomorrow | The day after today |
| Yesterday | The day before today |
| Free | Available, not busy |
