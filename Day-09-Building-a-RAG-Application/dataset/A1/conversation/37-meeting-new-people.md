# Chapter 37: Making Appointments

## Learning Objectives

By the end of this chapter, you will be able to:

- Make a simple appointment.
- Ask about available times.
- Confirm an appointment.
- Change or cancel an appointment.

## 1. Key Expressions

| Expression | Example |
|---|---|
| I'd like to make an appointment. | I'd like to make an appointment. |
| Are you available on Monday? | Are you available on Monday? |
| What time is available? | What time is available? |
| Does ten o'clock work? | Does ten o'clock work for you? |
| That works for me. | That works for me. |
| I'd like to change my appointment. | I'd like to change my appointment. |
| I need to cancel. | I need to cancel my appointment. |
| See you then. | See you on Monday. |

## 2. Example Conversations

### Conversation 1: Making an Appointment

**Receptionist:** Good morning. How can I help you?

**Customer:** I'd like to make an appointment.

**Receptionist:** What day would you like?

**Customer:** Monday, please.

**Receptionist:** We have ten o'clock available.

**Customer:** That works for me.

### Conversation 2: Changing an Appointment

**Anna:** Hello. I'd like to change my appointment.

**Receptionist:** Certainly. What day would you prefer?

**Anna:** Is Wednesday available?

**Receptionist:** Yes. We have two o'clock.

**Anna:** Perfect. Thank you.

### Conversation 3: Confirming an Appointment

**Mark:** I have an appointment tomorrow at eleven.

**Receptionist:** Yes, that's correct.

**Mark:** Great. Thank you.

**Receptionist:** You're welcome. See you tomorrow.
