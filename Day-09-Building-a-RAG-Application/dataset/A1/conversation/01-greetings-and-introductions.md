# Chapter 01: Greetings and Introductions

## Learning Objectives

By the end of this chapter, you can:
- Greet people in formal and informal situations.
- Ask someone how they are.
- Introduce yourself and other people.
- Say goodbye politely.

## Key Expressions

| Expression | Use |
|---|---|
| Hello! | A common greeting |
| Hi! | An informal greeting |
| Good morning! | A greeting before noon |
| Good afternoon! | A greeting after noon |
| Good evening! | A greeting in the evening |
| How are you? | Asking about someone's condition |
| I'm fine, thanks. | Giving a positive answer |
| Nice to meet you. | Meeting someone for the first time |
| See you later. | Saying goodbye |
| Have a nice day! | A friendly goodbye |

## Conversation 1: Meeting a New Person

**Emma:** Hi! My name is Emma. What's your name?

**Jack:** Hello! I'm Jack. Nice to meet you.

**Emma:** Nice to meet you, too. How are you?

**Jack:** I'm good, thanks. How about you?

**Emma:** I'm great!

**Jack:** Are you a student here?

**Emma:** Yes, I am. I'm a new student.

**Jack:** Welcome!

## Conversation 2: Morning Greeting

**Sarah:** Good morning, David!

**David:** Good morning, Sarah! How are you today?

**Sarah:** I'm fine, thank you. And you?

**David:** I'm very well, thanks.

**Sarah:** Have a nice day!

**David:** You, too!

## Conversation 3: Saying Goodbye

**Tom:** It's time to go home.

**Anna:** Okay. See you tomorrow!

**Tom:** See you! Have a good evening.

**Anna:** Thanks. You, too!

## Useful Vocabulary

| Word | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Greeting | Words used when meeting someone |
| Introduce | To tell someone your name |
| Welcome | A friendly word for someone arriving |
| Polite | Showing good manners |
| Informal | Relaxed and friendly |
| Formal | Respectful and professional |
| Tomorrow | The day after today |
| Today | This day |
