# Chapter 38: Phone Conversations

## Learning Objectives

By the end of this chapter, you will be able to:

- Start a simple phone conversation.
- Introduce yourself on the phone.
- Ask to speak to someone.
- End a phone call politely.

## 1. Key Expressions

| Expression | Example |
|---|---|
| Hello? | Hello? |
| This is Anna. | Hello, this is Anna. |
| May I speak to John? | May I speak to John, please? |
| Who's calling? | Who's calling, please? |
| Please hold. | Please hold for a moment. |
| He's not available. | He's not available right now. |
| Can I take a message? | Can I take a message? |
| Talk to you later. | Talk to you later! |

## 2. Example Conversations

### Conversation 1: Calling a Friend

**Anna:** Hello?

**Tom:** Hi, Anna. This is Tom.

**Anna:** Hi, Tom! How are you?

**Tom:** I'm fine, thanks. Are you free today?

**Anna:** Yes, I am.

**Tom:** Great. Let's meet at six.

### Conversation 2: Asking for Someone

**Receptionist:** Good morning. Green Hotel.

**Caller:** Hello. May I speak to Mr. Brown, please?

**Receptionist:** Certainly. Please hold.

**Caller:** Thank you.

### Conversation 3: Leaving a Message

**Lisa:** Hello. May I speak to Mark?

**Emma:** I'm sorry. He's not available right now.

**Lisa:** Can I leave a message?

**Emma:** Of course.

**Lisa:** Please tell him to call me.

**Emma:** No problem.

## 3. Useful Vocabulary

| Word | Meaning |
|---|---|
| phone | A device used to call people |
| call | Speak to someone by phone |
| caller | A person who makes a phone call |
| message | Information sent to someone |
| number | A series of digits |
| available | Free to talk or meet |
| hold | Wait on the phone |
| answer | Respond to a call or question |
| voicemail | A recorded phone message |
| conversation | A talk between people |
| later | After some time |
| goodbye | A word used when leaving |
