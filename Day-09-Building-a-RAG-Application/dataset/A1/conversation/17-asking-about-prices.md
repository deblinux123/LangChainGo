# Chapter 17: Asking About Prices

## Learning Objectives

- Ask how much products cost.
- Understand simple prices.
- Compare two prices.
- Ask about discounts and payment.

## Key Expressions

| Expression | Example |
|---|---|
| How much is this? | How much is this watch? |
| How much are these? | How much are these shoes? |
| It's twenty dollars. | The bag is twenty dollars. |
| They're fifteen dollars. | The shoes are fifteen dollars. |
| That's expensive. | That's expensive for me. |
| That's a good price. | That's a good price! |
| Is it on sale? | Is this jacket on sale? |
| Do you have a discount? | Do you have a student discount? |
| Can I pay in cash? | Can I pay in cash? |

## Conversation 1: At a Shoe Store

**Customer:** Excuse me. How much are these shoes?

**Assistant:** They're sixty dollars.

**Customer:** That's a little expensive.

**Assistant:** We have another pair for forty dollars.

**Customer:** Can I see them?

**Assistant:** Of course. Here you are.

**Customer:** I like these. I'll take them.

## Conversation 2: At a Market

**Customer:** How much are these apples?

**Seller:** They're three dollars a bag.

**Customer:** And how much are the oranges?

**Seller:** They're four dollars a bag.

**Customer:** Okay. I'll take one bag of apples and one bag of oranges.

## Conversation 3: Asking About a Discount

**Customer:** Is this jacket on sale?

**Assistant:** Yes. It's twenty-five percent off.

**Customer:** Great! What's the new price?

**Assistant:** It's forty-five dollars.

**Customer:** I'll buy it. Can I pay by card?

**Assistant:** Yes, you can.

## Useful Vocabulary

| Word | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Price | The amount something costs |
| Dollar | A unit of money |
| Sale | A time when products cost less |
| Discount | A reduction in price |
| Cheap | Not expensive |
| Expensive | Costing a lot |
| Cost | To have a particular price |
| Change | Money returned after payment |
| Cash | Money in notes or coins |
| Payment | Money given for something |

## Understanding the Conversations

### A. Multiple Choice

1. How much are the first pair of shoes?
   - a) Forty dollars
   - b) Fifty dollars
   - c) Sixty dollars

2. How much is the second pair?
   - a) Forty dollars
   - b) Thirty dollars
   - c) Twenty dollars

3. How much are the apples?
   - a) Two dollars a bag
   - b) Three dollars a bag
   - c) Four dollars a bag

4. What is the new price of the jacket?
   - a) Thirty-five dollars
   - b) Forty dollars
   - c) Forty-five dollars

### B. True or False

1. The customer buys the first pair of shoes.
2. Oranges cost more than apples.
3. The jacket has a discount.
4. The customer asks to pay by card.

### C. Short Answers

1. Why doesn't the customer choose the first pair of shoes?
2. What does the customer buy at the market?
3. How much is the jacket after the discount?
4. What payment method does the customer ask about?
