# Chapter 35: Talking About Future Plans

## Learning Objectives

By the end of this chapter, you will be able to:

- Talk about future plans.
- Ask people about their plans.
- Describe simple goals.
- Use "will" and "going to" in basic conversations.

## 1. Key Expressions

| Expression | Example |
|---|---|
| What are you going to do? | What are you going to do tomorrow? |
| I'm going to study. | I'm going to study English. |
| I will call you. | I will call you tonight. |
| I hope to ... | I hope to travel next year. |
| Next week | I'm going to visit my friend next week. |
| Next year | I want to learn Spanish next year. |
| I plan to ... | I plan to buy a new computer. |
| Maybe ... | Maybe I will go shopping. |

## 2. Example Conversations

### Conversation 1: Tomorrow's Plans

**Alex:** What are you going to do tomorrow?

**Mia:** I'm going to study English.

**Alex:** Are you going to study at home?

**Mia:** Yes, I am.

### Conversation 2: Future Goals

**Sam:** What do you want to do next year?

**Lucy:** I want to learn Spanish.

**Sam:** That's great!

**Lucy:** What about you?

**Sam:** I want to travel to Canada.

### Conversation 3: Making a Promise

**Ben:** Can you help me tomorrow?

**Kate:** Yes. I will help you.

**Ben:** Thank you!

**Kate:** You're welcome.

## 3. Useful Vocabulary

| Word | Meaning |
|---|---|
| future | Time after now |
| plan | Something you intend to do |
| goal | Something you want to achieve |
| tomorrow | The day after today |
| next week | The week after this week |
| next month | The month after this month |
| next year | The year after this year |
| hope | Want something good to happen |
| learn | Get knowledge or a skill |
| improve | Become better |
| promise | Say that you will do something |
| decide | Choose something |

## 10. Chapter Summary

You learned how to talk about future plans, personal goals, and promises.

**Remember:** Use "going to" for plans and "will" for simple predictions, promises, and decisions made at the moment of speaking.
