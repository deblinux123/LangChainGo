# Chapter 04: Asking and Answering Personal Questions

## Learning Objectives

By the end of this chapter, you can:
- Ask basic personal questions.
- Answer questions about your life.
- Ask about age, hometown, job, and family.
- Keep a simple conversation going.

## Key Expressions

| Question | Example Answer |
|---|---|
| What's your name? | My name is Lucy. |
| How old are you? | I'm twenty years old. |
| Where are you from? | I'm from Canada. |
| Where do you live? | I live in Denver. |
| What do you do? | I'm a student. |
| Are you married? | No, I'm not. |
| Do you have any brothers or sisters? | Yes, I have one brother. |
| What languages do you speak? | I speak English and Spanish. |
| What is your phone number? | It's 555-1234. |

## Conversation 1: At a Language School

**Teacher:** Hello! Can I ask you a few questions?

**Mark:** Sure!

**Teacher:** What's your full name?

**Mark:** My name is Mark Wilson.

**Teacher:** Where are you from?

**Mark:** I'm from the United States.

**Teacher:** Where do you live now?

**Mark:** I live in Denver.

**Teacher:** What languages do you speak?

**Mark:** I speak English and a little Spanish.

**Teacher:** Thank you, Mark!

## Conversation 2: Two New Friends

**Nina:** Hi! Do you have any brothers or sisters?

**Leo:** Yes. I have one brother and two sisters.

**Nina:** That's nice! Are they older or younger?

**Leo:** My brother is older, and my sisters are younger.

**Nina:** Do they live with you?

**Leo:** Yes, we all live together.

## Conversation 3: Asking About Work

**Maya:** What do you do, Ben?

**Ben:** I'm a waiter. What about you?

**Maya:** I'm a nurse.

**Ben:** Do you work in a hospital?

**Maya:** Yes, I do.

**Ben:** That's interesting!

## Useful Vocabulary

| Word | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Full name | First name and last name |
| Hometown | The place where you are from |
| Language | A system of communication |
| Married | Having a husband or wife |
| Older | Having more age |
| Younger | Having less age |
| Together | With other people |
| Question | Something you ask |
| Answer | Something you say in response |
