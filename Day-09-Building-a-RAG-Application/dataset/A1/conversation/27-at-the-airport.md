# Chapter 27: At the Doctor's Office

## Learning Objectives

By the end of this chapter, you will be able to:

- Talk to a doctor about simple symptoms.
- Answer basic questions about your health.
- Ask simple questions at a doctor's office.
- Understand basic medical instructions.

## 1. Key Expressions

| Expression | Example |
|---|---|
| I need to see a doctor. | I need to see a doctor. |
| What's the problem? | What's the problem today? |
| I have a fever. | I have a fever. |
| How long? | How long have you felt sick? |
| Since yesterday. | I've felt sick since yesterday. |
| Open your mouth. | Please open your mouth. |
| Take this medicine. | Take this medicine after lunch. |
| Thank you, Doctor. | Thank you, Doctor. |

## 2. Example Conversations

### Conversation 1: At the Reception Desk

**Receptionist:** Hello. How can I help you?

**Patient:** I'd like to see a doctor.

**Receptionist:** Do you have an appointment?

**Patient:** No, I don't.

**Receptionist:** Please wait here.

### Conversation 2: Talking to the Doctor

**Doctor:** Hello. What's the problem?

**Patient:** I have a cough and a fever.

**Doctor:** How long have you felt this way?

**Patient:** Since yesterday.

**Doctor:** Okay. Please sit down.

### Conversation 3: Getting Instructions

**Doctor:** You need some rest.

**Patient:** Do I need medicine?

**Doctor:** Yes. Take this medicine after lunch.

**Patient:** Thank you, Doctor.

**Doctor:** You're welcome.

## 3. Useful Vocabulary

| Word | Meaning |
|---|---|
| doctor | A person who treats sick people |
| patient | A person receiving medical care |
| receptionist | A person who welcomes visitors |
| appointment | A planned meeting |
| clinic | A place where people receive medical care |
| medicine | Something used to treat an illness |
| fever | A high body temperature |
| cough | A sudden sound from your throat |
| examination | A medical check |
| problem | Something that is wrong |
| rest | Time to relax |
| prescription | Instructions for medicine from a doctor |
