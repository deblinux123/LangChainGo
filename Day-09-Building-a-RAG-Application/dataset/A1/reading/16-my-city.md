# Chapter 16 — My City

## 1. Learning Objectives

- Understand a simple description of a city.
- Identify common city places.
- Describe what a city has.
- Recognize basic city vocabulary.
- Write about your city.

## 2. Reading Passage

### My Beautiful City

I live in a beautiful city called Lakeview. It is not very big, but it has many interesting places.

There is a large park in the center of the city. Families go there on weekends. Children play on the grass, and people walk their dogs.

My city has a public library, a hospital, and several schools. There is also a shopping mall with many stores and restaurants.

The main street is busy during the day. Many people go to work, shop, and visit friends. There are buses and taxis on the roads.

I like the city museum because I enjoy learning about history. My favorite place is the park because it is quiet and green.

The people in Lakeview are friendly. I feel happy living here.

## 3. Key Vocabulary

| Word | Meaning |
|---|---|
| City | A large town |
| Center | The middle part of a place |
| Park | A public outdoor area |
| Library | A place where people read or borrow books |
| Hospital | A place where sick people receive care |
| Museum | A place where people see historical or interesting objects |
| Street | A public road in a city |
| Shopping mall | A building with many stores |
| Store | A place where people buy things |
| Restaurant | A place where people eat meals |
| Busy | Full of activity |
| Quiet | Making little noise |
| Public | For everyone to use |
| History | Events from the past |
| Friendly | Kind and pleasant |
