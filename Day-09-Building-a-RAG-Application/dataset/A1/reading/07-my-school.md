## 2. Reading Passage

### A Day at My School

My name is Ethan, and I am a student at Lincoln Middle School. I am 13 years old, and I live in San Diego, California.

My school is large and modern. It has three floors, a library, a computer room, a gym, a cafeteria, and a big playground.

My classes start at 8:00 in the morning. My favorite subject is science because I like learning about animals, plants, and the human body. I also enjoy English and history.

My English teacher is very friendly. Her name is Mrs. Wilson. She always helps students with their homework.

I have lunch at 12:00 in the cafeteria with my friends. I usually eat a sandwich and drink some water.

After lunch, I have math and art classes. I like art because I enjoy drawing pictures.

School finishes at 3:00 in the afternoon. Sometimes, I play basketball with my friends in the gym before going home.

I like my school because the teachers are helpful, and I have many good friends there.
