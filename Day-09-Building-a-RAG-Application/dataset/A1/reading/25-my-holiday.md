# Chapter 25 — My Holiday

## 1. Learning Objectives

- Understand a simple holiday story.
- Recognize travel and holiday vocabulary.
- Identify places and activities during a trip.
- Describe a past holiday.
- Write about a vacation.

## 2. Reading Passage

### My Summer Vacation

Last summer, my family went on vacation to a small town near the mountains. We stayed there for five days.

We traveled by car. The journey took about four hours. When we arrived, we checked into a small hotel near a lake.

Every morning, we had breakfast at the hotel. Then we went outside to explore the town.

On the first day, we walked around the lake and took pictures. On the second day, we visited a local museum. My brother loved the old cars there.

On the third day, we went hiking in the mountains. The views were beautiful, and the air was fresh.

In the evenings, we ate dinner at different restaurants. I tried some new food and enjoyed it.

On the last day, we packed our bags and drove home. I loved my vacation because I spent time with my family and discovered new places.

## 3. Key Vocabulary

| Word | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Holiday | A period of time away from work or school |
| Vacation | A period of rest or travel |
| Journey | Travel from one place to another |
| Mountain | A very high natural area of land |
| Lake | A large area of water surrounded by land |
| Hotel | A place where travelers pay to stay |
| Explore | Travel around to learn about a place |
| Local | Related to a particular area |
| Museum | A place with historical or interesting objects |
| Hiking | Walking in natural areas |
| View | What you can see from a place |
| Discover | Find something new |
| Pack | Put things into bags |
| Stay | Live somewhere temporarily |
| Vacation | Time away from normal work or study |
