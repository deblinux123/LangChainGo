# Chapter 15 — At the Supermarket

## 1. Learning Objectives

- Recognize common grocery items.
- Understand a supermarket shopping trip.
- Identify quantities and food categories.
- Understand a simple shopping list.
- Write about grocery shopping.

## 2. Reading Passage

### Shopping with Mom

Every Saturday morning, I go to the supermarket with my mother. We usually make a shopping list before we leave home.

This week, we need milk, eggs, bread, cheese, and rice. We also need apples, bananas, tomatoes, and potatoes.

First, we visit the fruit and vegetable section. My mother chooses fresh apples and red tomatoes. I put some bananas in our shopping cart.

Next, we go to the dairy section. We buy two bottles of milk, a package of cheese, and a dozen eggs.

We also buy a loaf of bread and a bag of rice. The supermarket is busy, but the workers are friendly.

At the checkout counter, my mother pays for everything. We put the groceries in our bags and take them home.

I like going to the supermarket because I enjoy helping my mother.

## 3. Key Vocabulary

| Word | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Supermarket | A large store that sells food and other products |
| Grocery | Food and household items bought at a store |
| Shopping list | A list of things to buy |
| Cart | A container with wheels used for shopping |
| Dairy | Food products made from milk |
| Section | One part of a larger place |
| Checkout counter | A place where customers pay |
| Loaf | A shaped amount of bread |
| Dozen | A group of twelve |
| Bottle | A container for liquids |
| Package | Something wrapped or packed |
| Bag | A container used to carry things |
| Fresh | Recently picked or prepared |
| Choose | To select something |
| Pay | To give money for something |
