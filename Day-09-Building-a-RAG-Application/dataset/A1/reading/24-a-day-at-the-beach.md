# Chapter 24 — A Day at the Beach

## 1. Learning Objectives

- Recognize beach-related vocabulary.
- Understand a story about a day at the beach.
- Identify activities and objects.
- Describe a simple day trip.
- Write about a visit to the beach.

## 2. Reading Passage

### A Sunny Day by the Sea

Last Sunday, my family and I went to the beach. The weather was sunny and warm. We left home early in the morning and arrived at the beach at ten o'clock.

My brother and I ran on the sand and collected small shells. Then we played with a ball near the water.

My father swam in the sea, but my mother sat under a large umbrella and read a book. After that, we ate sandwiches, fruit, and cookies for lunch.

In the afternoon, we built a big sandcastle. My brother found a beautiful shell near the water. We took many pictures together.

At five o'clock, we packed our bags and went home. We were tired but very happy. It was a wonderful day.

## 3. Key Vocabulary

| Word | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Beach | An area beside the sea |
| Sea | A large area of salt water |
| Sand | Small grains found on beaches |
| Shell | A hard covering from a sea animal |
| Umbrella | Something that protects you from the sun or rain |
| Sandcastle | A model made from wet sand |
| Swim | Move through water |
| Collect | Bring things together |
| Pack | Put things into a bag |
| Picture | A photograph or drawing |
| Arrive | Reach a place |
| Tired | Needing rest |
