# Chapter 31 — Sports and Exercise

## 1. Learning Objectives

- Recognize common sports vocabulary.
- Understand a text about sports and exercise.
- Identify sports equipment and activities.
- Describe favorite sports.
- Write about physical activities.

## 2. Reading Passage

### My Favorite Sport

My name is Lucas, and I love sports. My favorite sport is basketball.

I play basketball three times a week at the sports center near my house. I usually play with my school friends. Our coach teaches us how to pass the ball, run, and work as a team.

My sister likes tennis. She plays tennis every Sunday with her friend. She has a racket and several tennis balls.

My father enjoys swimming. He goes to the swimming pool twice a week. My mother prefers walking in the park.

On weekends, we sometimes exercise together. We ride our bikes or go for a long walk.

I think sports are fun. They help us stay active and spend time with other people.

## 3. Key Vocabulary

| Word | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Sport | An activity that involves physical skill |
| Exercise | Physical activity to improve fitness |
| Basketball | A game played by two teams with a ball and hoops |
| Tennis | A game played with rackets and a ball |
| Swimming | Moving through water |
| Coach | A person who trains a sports team |
| Team | A group of players |
| Pass | Send a ball to another player |
| Racket | Equipment used to hit a ball |
| Sports center | A place for sports and exercise |
| Swimming pool | A place filled with water for swimming |
| Active | Doing physical activities |
| Fitness | The condition of being physically healthy |
| Twice | Two times |
| Together | With other people |
