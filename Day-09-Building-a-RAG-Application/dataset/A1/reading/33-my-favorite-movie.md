# Chapter 33 — My Favorite Movie

## 1. Learning Objectives

- Recognize common movie vocabulary.
- Understand a simple description of a favorite movie.
- Identify movie characters and genres.
- Describe entertainment preferences.
- Write about a favorite movie.

## 2. Reading Passage

### The Movie I Love

My name is Mia, and I love watching movies. My favorite movie is an animated adventure called The Little Explorer.

The story is about a young girl named Lily. She lives in a small village and dreams of exploring the world.

One day, Lily finds an old map in her grandfather's house. She decides to follow the map and discover a hidden garden.

During her journey, she meets a friendly dog and a clever bird. They become her friends and help her solve problems.

I like this movie because it is funny, colorful, and exciting. The characters are interesting, and the music is beautiful.

I usually watch movies with my family on Friday evenings. We make popcorn, sit on the sofa, and enjoy the story together.

I want to watch The Little Explorer again because it always makes me smile.

## 3. Key Vocabulary

| Word | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Movie | A story shown on a screen |
| Animated | Made using drawings or computer images |
| Adventure | An exciting experience |
| Story | A description of events |
| Character | A person or animal in a story |
| Explorer | A person who travels to discover places |
| Village | A small community |
| Dream | Something you hope to do |
| Map | A drawing that shows places |
| Discover | Find something new |
| Hidden | Difficult to see or find |
| Journey | Travel from one place to another |
| Solve | Find an answer to a problem |
| Exciting | Making you feel interested or happy |
| Funny | Making you laugh |
| Scene | One part of a movie |
| Actor | A person who performs in a movie or play |
| Music | Sounds used in a movie or performance |
