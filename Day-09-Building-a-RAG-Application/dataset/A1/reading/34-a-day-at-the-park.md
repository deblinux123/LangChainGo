# Chapter 34: A Day at the Park

## Learning Objectives

By the end of this chapter, you can:
- Read about activities in a park.
- Understand common outdoor activity vocabulary.
- Describe what people do in a park.
- Write about a day outdoors.

## Reading Passage

### A Day at the Park

My name is Emma. Last Sunday, I went to the park with my family. The weather was sunny and warm.

We arrived at the park at ten o'clock in the morning. My brother played soccer with my father. My mother sat under a big tree and read a book. I rode my bicycle around the park.

After an hour, we had lunch. We ate sandwiches, apples, and cookies. We also drank water and orange juice.

Then, my brother and I played on the swings. We saw many birds in the trees. Some children were playing with their dogs.

At four o'clock, we went home. We were tired but happy. It was a wonderful day!

## Key Vocabulary

| Word | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Park | A public place with trees and grass |
| Sunny | Having a lot of sunlight |
| Warm | A little hot |
| Arrive | To get to a place |
| Bicycle | A vehicle with two wheels |
| Ride | To travel on a bicycle or horse |
| Tree | A tall plant |
| Swing | A seat that moves forward and backward |
| Bird | An animal with wings |
| Tired | Needing rest |
| Wonderful | Very good |
