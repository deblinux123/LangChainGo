# Chapter 12 — My Favorite Food

## 1. Learning Objectives

- Identify common foods and drinks.
- Understand a simple text about food preferences.
- Describe favorite meals and eating habits.
- Answer reading comprehension questions.
- Write about favorite foods.

## 2. Reading Passage

### Food I Love

My name is Emma, and I love food. My favorite meal is lunch because I can eat many delicious things.

My favorite food is chicken and rice. My mother cooks it every Sunday. She adds vegetables and a little salt. It smells wonderful, and it tastes delicious.

I also like fruit. Apples, bananas, and strawberries are my favorite fruits. I usually eat an apple after lunch.

For breakfast, I often have eggs, bread, and milk. Sometimes I eat cereal with yogurt. I drink water during the day because it is healthy.

I do not like very spicy food. My brother loves spicy food, but I prefer simple meals.

On Fridays, my family sometimes makes pizza at home. We put cheese, tomatoes, and vegetables on it. We enjoy eating together.

Good food makes me happy, but I also know that eating healthy food is important.

## 3. Key Vocabulary

| Word | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Meal | Food eaten at a particular time |
| Breakfast | The first meal of the day |
| Lunch | A meal eaten in the middle of the day |
| Dinner | The main evening meal |
| Delicious | Very good to eat |
| Vegetable | A plant used as food |
| Fruit | Sweet food that grows on trees or plants |
| Spicy | Having a hot or strong flavor |
| Healthy | Good for your health |
| Rice | Small grains used as food |
| Chicken | Meat from a chicken |
| Yogurt | A soft food made from milk |
| Smell | To notice an odor |
| Taste | The flavor of food |
| Prefer | To like one thing more than another |

## 4. Reading Comprehension

### Exercise A — Multiple Choice

1. What is Emma's favorite meal?
   - A. Breakfast
   - B. Lunch
   - C. Dinner

2. What is her favorite food?
   - A. Fish and potatoes
   - B. Chicken and rice
   - C. Pizza

3. When does her mother cook chicken and rice?
   - A. Every Sunday
   - B. Every Monday
   - C. Every Friday

4. Which fruit does Emma usually eat after lunch?
   - A. An orange
   - B. A banana
   - C. An apple

5. What does Emma drink during the day?
   - A. Water
   - B. Soda
   - C. Coffee

6. Who loves spicy food?
   - A. Her mother
   - B. Her brother
   - C. Her father

### Exercise B — True or False

1. Emma loves food.
2. Her favorite meal is dinner.
3. Her mother cooks chicken and rice on Sundays.
4. Emma likes apples and strawberries.
5. Emma drinks coffee every day.
6. Her family sometimes makes pizza at home.
