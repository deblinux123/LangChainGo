# Chapter 36: My Country

## Learning Objectives

By the end of this chapter, you can:
- Read a simple description of a country.
- Understand vocabulary about places and nationalities.
- Find information about cities, food, and culture.
- Write a short description of a country.

## Reading Passage

### My Country

My country is Iran. It is in Asia. The capital city is Tehran. Tehran is a large and busy city.

Iran has many beautiful places. There are high mountains, green forests, deserts, and beaches. Mount Damavand is the highest mountain in Iran.

People in Iran speak Persian. The country has many traditional foods. Rice, bread, and kebabs are popular. My favorite food is rice with chicken.

Iran has four seasons: spring, summer, fall, and winter. The weather is different in different parts of the country.

I love my country because of its history, beautiful nature, and friendly people. I enjoy learning about its cities and traditions.

## Key Vocabulary

| Language | A system of communication |
