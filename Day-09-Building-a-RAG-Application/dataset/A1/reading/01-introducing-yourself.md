# Chapter 01 — Introducing Yourself

## 1. Learning Objectives

By the end of this chapter, you will be able to:

- Understand a short text about a person's life.
- Identify basic personal information.
- Understand simple sentences about family, work, and hobbies.
- Answer basic reading comprehension questions.
- Recognize common words used when introducing yourself.

## 2. Reading Passage

### My Name Is Alex

Hello! My name is Alex Miller. I am 24 years old. I am from Chicago, Illinois, but I live in New York now.

I am a student at a university. I study computer science. I like learning about computers and technology.

I live in a small apartment with my friend, David. He is very friendly. We sometimes cook dinner together.

I have one sister. Her name is Emma. She is 19 years old, and she lives with our parents in Chicago.

In my free time, I enjoy listening to music, playing soccer, and reading books. My favorite food is pizza, and my favorite color is blue.

I am happy to meet new people and make new friends!

## 3. Key Vocabulary

| Word or Phrase | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Name | The word people use to identify you |
| Age | How old someone is |
| From | The place where someone comes from |
| Live | To have your home in a place |
| Student | A person who studies |
| University | A place where people study after high school |
| Study | To learn about a subject |
| Friendly | Kind and pleasant |
| Apartment | A home in a building |
| Sister | A female sibling |
| Parents | Your mother and father |
| Free time | Time when you do not work or study |
| Enjoy | To like doing something |
| Favorite | The thing you like most |
| Meet | To see or talk to someone for the first time |
