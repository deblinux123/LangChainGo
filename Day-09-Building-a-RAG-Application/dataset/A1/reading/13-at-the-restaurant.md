# Chapter 13 — At the Restaurant

## 1. Learning Objectives

- Recognize common restaurant vocabulary.
- Understand a simple restaurant conversation.
- Identify food, drinks, and prices.
- Understand how customers order meals.
- Write about a restaurant visit.

## 2. Reading Passage

### Dinner at Sunny Restaurant

Last Saturday, my family and I went to Sunny Restaurant. It is a small restaurant near our house.

When we arrived, a waiter showed us a table by the window. We sat down and looked at the menu.

My father ordered a hamburger and French fries. My mother wanted a chicken salad. I ordered a cheese pizza. My younger sister asked for a sandwich.

We also ordered drinks. My father had orange juice, my mother had water, and I had lemonade.

The food arrived after fifteen minutes. Everything was fresh and delicious. The waiter was friendly and helpful.

After dinner, we ordered chocolate cake for dessert. We enjoyed our meal and thanked the waiter.

It was a wonderful evening. We want to visit Sunny Restaurant again.

## 3. Key Vocabulary

| Word | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Restaurant | A place where people buy and eat meals |
| Waiter | A person who serves food in a restaurant |
| Menu | A list of food and drinks |
| Order | To ask for food or drinks |
| Table | Furniture used for eating |
| Customer | A person who buys something |
| Hamburger | A sandwich with a cooked meat patty |
| French fries | Long pieces of fried potato |
| Salad | A dish made with vegetables |
| Dessert | Sweet food eaten after a meal |
| Bill | A document showing how much you must pay |
| Delicious | Very good to eat |
| Fresh | Recently made or prepared |
| Juice | A drink made from fruit |
| Arrive | To reach a place |
