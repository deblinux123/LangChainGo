# Chapter 14 — Shopping for Clothes

## 1. Learning Objectives

- Identify common clothes.
- Understand a simple shopping experience.
- Recognize colors, sizes, and prices.
- Describe clothes and shopping preferences.
- Write about buying clothes.

## 2. Reading Passage

### A New Jacket

My name is Daniel. Yesterday, I went shopping with my sister because I needed a new jacket.

We visited a clothing store at the shopping mall. There were many jackets, shirts, pants, and shoes.

I saw a blue jacket near the entrance. It looked very nice. I asked a store assistant about the price. It cost sixty dollars.

I wanted to try it on, so the assistant gave me a large size. The jacket was comfortable, but it was a little too big. Then I tried a medium size. It fit perfectly.

My sister also bought a pair of black shoes. They were on sale, so she paid only thirty dollars.

We paid at the checkout counter and took our shopping bags home. I was happy with my new jacket.

## 3. Key Vocabulary

| Word | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Jacket | Clothing worn on the upper body |
| Shirt | Clothing worn on the upper body |
| Pants | Clothing worn on the legs |
| Shoes | Things worn on the feet |
| Store | A place where people buy things |
| Shopping mall | A large building with many stores |
| Size | How large or small something is |
| Price | The amount of money something costs |
| Try on | To put on clothes to see if they fit |
| Assistant | A person who helps customers in a store |
| Comfortable | Making you feel relaxed |
| Fit | To be the correct size |
| Sale | A time when things cost less |
| Checkout counter | The place where customers pay |
| Entrance | A place where people enter |
