# Chapter 38: A Visit to the Doctor

## Learning Objectives

By the end of this chapter, you can:
- Read about a visit to a doctor.
- Understand basic health vocabulary.
- Identify common symptoms and simple advice.
- Write about a doctor's visit.

## Reading Passage

### At the Doctor's Office

Yesterday, Anna did not feel well. She had a headache and a sore throat. She also felt very tired.

Her father took her to the doctor's office at ten o'clock in the morning.

The doctor welcomed Anna and asked, "How do you feel?"

Anna said, "I have a headache, and my throat hurts."

The doctor checked her temperature and listened to her breathing. Then, the doctor said, "You have a cold. You should drink plenty of water and get some rest."

Anna thanked the doctor. After the visit, she went home with her father. She stayed in bed and drank warm tea.

The next day, Anna felt better. She was happy because she could go back to school soon.

## Key Vocabulary

| Word | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Doctor | A person who helps sick people |
| Office | A place where a doctor works |
| Headache | Pain in your head |
| Sore throat | Pain in your throat |
| Temperature | A measurement of how hot or cold something is |
| Breathing | The process of taking air in and out |
| Cold | A common illness |
| Rest | Time to relax |
| Symptom | A sign of an illness |
| Feel better | To become healthier |
| Sick | Not feeling well |
