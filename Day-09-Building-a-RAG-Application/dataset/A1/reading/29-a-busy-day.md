# Chapter 29 — A Busy Day

## 1. Learning Objectives

- Understand a story about a busy day.
- Recognize daily activity vocabulary.
- Identify the order and time of events.
- Describe a daily schedule.
- Write about a busy day.

## 2. Reading Passage

### Monday Is Busy

My name is Ryan. Monday is always a busy day for me.

I wake up at six thirty and get ready for school. I have breakfast at seven o'clock and leave home at seven thirty.

My classes start at eight. I study English, math, science, and history. At noon, I have lunch with my classmates.

School finishes at three o'clock. After school, I go to the library to study for one hour.

At five o'clock, I return home. I have a snack and then help my father in the garden.

In the evening, I do my homework. My family has dinner together at seven thirty.

After dinner, I take a shower and read a book for twenty minutes. I go to bed at nine thirty.

I am tired at the end of the day, but I feel good because I finish my work.

## 3. Key Vocabulary

| Word | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Busy | Having many things to do |
| Schedule | A plan of activities and times |
| Class | A lesson at school |
| Classmate | A person in the same class |
| Finish | Complete something |
| Return | Go back |
| Snack | A small amount of food |
| Garden | An area where plants grow |
| Together | With other people |
| Shower | Washing your body with water |
| Homework | Schoolwork done outside class |
| Tired | Needing rest |
| Activity | Something you do |
| Study | Spend time learning |
| Ready | Prepared |
