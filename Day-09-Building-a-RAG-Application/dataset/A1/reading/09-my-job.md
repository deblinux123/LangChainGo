# Chapter 09 — My Job

## 1. Learning Objectives

By the end of this chapter, you will be able to:

- Understand a simple description of someone's job.
- Recognize common jobs and occupations.
- Identify basic workplace vocabulary.
- Understand simple sentences about work routines.
- Answer reading comprehension questions.
- Write a short paragraph about a job.

## 2. Reading Passage

### My Father's Job

My father's name is David. He is a doctor. He works at a hospital in our city.

He works five days a week, from Monday to Friday. He usually starts work at eight o'clock in the morning and finishes at four o'clock in the afternoon.

Every day, he helps sick people. He talks to his patients and checks their health. Sometimes, he gives them medicine and tells them how to stay healthy.

My father's hospital is large and clean. There are many doctors, nurses, and other workers there. He works with a friendly team.

My mother is a teacher. She works at an elementary school. She teaches children English and science.

My parents like their jobs because they enjoy helping people.

I am a student, but I want to be an engineer in the future. I like computers, machines, and technology. I study hard because I want to have a good job one day.

| Occupation | A person's job or profession |
| Doctor | A person who treats sick people |
| Teacher | A person who helps students learn |
| Engineer | A person who designs or builds things |
| Nurse | A person who cares for sick people |
| Hospital | A place where sick people receive medical care |
| School | A place where students learn |
| Patient | A person who receives medical care |
| Medicine | Something used to treat illness |
| Health | The condition of your body and mind |
| Team | A group of people who work together |
| Worker | A person who works |
| Workplace | A place where someone works |
| Help | To make something easier for someone |
| Teach | To give someone knowledge or skills |
| Treat | To give medical care to someone |
| Future | The time that will come |
| Technology | The use of scientific knowledge and tools |
| Elementary school | A school for young children |
