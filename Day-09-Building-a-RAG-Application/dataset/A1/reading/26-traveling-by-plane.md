# Chapter 26 — Traveling by Plane

## 1. Learning Objectives

- Recognize common airport vocabulary.
- Understand a simple airplane journey.
- Identify basic airport activities.
- Describe a flight.
- Write about traveling by plane.

## 2. Reading Passage

### My First Flight

Last month, I traveled by plane for the first time. I went to Chicago with my parents.

We arrived at the airport three hours before our flight. First, we checked in at the airline counter and gave our bags to the airport staff.

Then we went through security. After that, we looked at the departure board to find our gate.

We waited near the gate for about thirty minutes. Soon, the airline staff announced that our flight was ready for boarding.

We walked onto the airplane and found our seats. I sat next to the window. I was excited because I could see the clouds and the land below us.

The flight took two hours. The flight attendants were friendly and helpful. When we arrived in Chicago, we collected our luggage and met my uncle.

It was an exciting experience, and I want to fly again.

## 3. Key Vocabulary

| Word | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Airport | A place where airplanes arrive and leave |
| Airplane | A vehicle that flies |
| Flight | A journey by airplane |
| Airline | A company that operates airplanes |
| Check in | Register before a flight |
| Security | A safety check at an airport |
| Gate | The place where passengers enter an airplane |
| Boarding | Getting onto an airplane |
| Departure | The act of leaving |
| Arrival | The act of reaching a place |
| Luggage | Bags and suitcases used for travel |
| Flight attendant | A person who helps passengers on a plane |
| Passenger | A person traveling in a vehicle |
| Seat | A place to sit |
| Window | An opening that lets you see outside |
