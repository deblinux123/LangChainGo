# Chapter 39: A Special Day

## Learning Objectives

By the end of this chapter, you can:
- Read about a memorable day.
- Understand vocabulary about emotions and experiences.
- Follow events in chronological order.
- Write about a special day in your life.

## Reading Passage

### The Day I Got My First Job

Last Monday was a very special day for me. I got my first job at a small bookstore near my home.

I woke up early because I was excited. I put on my best clothes and ate breakfast with my family.

I arrived at the bookstore at nine o'clock. The manager welcomed me and showed me around. She explained my work. I needed to organize books, help customers, and keep the store clean.

At first, I felt a little nervous. But the other workers were friendly and helpful. At lunchtime, we ate sandwiches together.

My first day was busy, but I enjoyed it. When I came home, I told my family everything. They were proud of me.

I will always remember that day because it was the beginning of a new experience.

## Key Vocabulary

| Word | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Special | Important or different |
| Memorable | Easy to remember |
| Job | Work that a person does |
| Bookstore | A shop that sells books |
| Manager | A person who is responsible for a business |
| Customer | A person who buys something |
| Organize | To put things in order |
| Nervous | Worried or afraid |
| Proud | Happy about someone's success |
| Experience | Something that happens to you |
| Remember | To keep something in your mind |
