# Chapter 40: A1 Reading Final Review

## Learning Objectives

By the end of this chapter, you can:
- Understand short texts about everyday life.
- Identify important details in simple passages.
- Understand basic vocabulary from A1 topics.

- Answer different types of reading questions.
- Write a short paragraph about familiar topics.

## Reading Passage 1: My Daily Life

My name is Lucas. I am twenty years old. I live with my parents in a small apartment.

I usually wake up at seven o'clock. I eat breakfast and go to work by bus. I work in a supermarket. I start work at nine and finish at five.

After work, I sometimes meet my friends. We talk, drink coffee, or walk in the park. In the evening, I have dinner with my family. I usually go to bed at eleven.

### Questions

#### A. Multiple Choice

1. How old is Lucas?
   - a) 18
   - b) 20
   - c) 25

2. How does Lucas go to work?
   - a) By car
   - b) By bicycle
   - c) By bus

3. Where does Lucas work?
   - a) In a supermarket
   - b) In a school
   - c) In a hospital

4. What time does he finish work?
   - a) Three o'clock
   - b) Four o'clock
   - c) Five o'clock

#### B. True or False

1. Lucas lives alone.
2. He usually wakes up at seven.
3. He works in a supermarket.
4. He has dinner with his friends every evening.

## Reading Passage 2: A Weekend Trip

Last weekend, Mia went to the beach with her sister. They traveled by train on Saturday morning.

The weather was sunny and warm. They walked on the beach, swam in the sea, and took many pictures.

At lunchtime, they ate sandwiches and fruit. In the afternoon, they visited a small shop and bought some souvenirs.

They returned home on Sunday evening. Mia was tired, but she was very happy. She wants to visit the beach again next summer.

### Questions

#### A. Multiple Choice

1. Where did Mia go?
   - a) To the mountains
   - b) To the beach
   - c) To a museum

2. Who went with Mia?
   - a) Her sister
   - b) Her brother
   - c) Her mother

3. How did they travel?
   - a) By bus
   - b) By plane
   - c) By train

4. What did they buy?
   - a) Books
   - b) Souvenirs
   - c) Clothes

#### B. True or False

1. Mia traveled on Saturday morning.
2. The weather was cold and rainy.
3. They swam in the sea.
4. They returned home on Monday morning.

#### C. Short Answers

1. What did Mia and her sister do on the beach?
2. What did they eat for lunch?
3. When did they return home?

## Reading Passage 3: At a New School

Tom started at a new school last month. On his first day, he met a friendly student named Ben.

Ben showed Tom the library, the cafeteria, and the playground. They had lunch together and talked about their hobbies.

Tom likes reading and playing basketball. Ben likes soccer and music. They have different hobbies, but they enjoy spending time together.

Now, Tom feels happy at school. He has many new friends.

### Questions

#### A. Multiple Choice

1. When did Tom start at the new school?
   - a) Yesterday
   - b) Last month
   - c) Last year

2. Who did Tom meet?
   - a) Ben
   - b) Alex
   - c) Lucas

3. What does Tom like?
   - a) Cooking and swimming
   - b) Reading and basketball
   - c) Soccer and music

4. How does Tom feel now?
   - a) Sad
   - b) Nervous
   - c) Happy

#### B. True or False

1. Ben showed Tom around the school.
2. Tom and Ben have exactly the same hobbies.
3. They had lunch together.
4. Tom has new friends.

## Reading Passage 4: A Healthy Week

Sofia wants to have a healthy lifestyle. She eats fruit and vegetables every day. She drinks plenty of water and does not drink much soda.

On Monday and Wednesday, she goes swimming. On Tuesday and Thursday, she walks in the park. On Saturday, she rides her bicycle with her brother.

Sofia also sleeps eight hours every night. She knows that healthy food, exercise, and good sleep are important.

### Questions

#### A. Multiple Choice

1. What does Sofia eat every day?
   - a) Candy
   - b) Fruit and vegetables
   - c) Fast food

2. When does she go swimming?
   - a) Monday and Wednesday
   - b) Tuesday and Thursday
   - c) Friday and Saturday

3. Who rides a bicycle with Sofia?
   - a) Her sister
   - b) Her father
   - c) Her brother

4. How many hours does Sofia sleep?
   - a) Six
   - b) Seven
   - c) Eight

#### B. True or False

1. Sofia drinks plenty of water.
2. She goes swimming every day.
3. She walks in the park on Tuesday and Thursday.
4. She sleeps four hours every night.

## Reading Passage 5: A Visit to the Doctor

Yesterday, Mark had a stomachache. He stayed home from work and called the doctor's office.

The doctor asked him to come in at eleven o'clock. At the office, the doctor asked Mark some questions and checked him.

The doctor told Mark to drink water, eat simple food, and get some rest. Mark went home and followed the advice.

Today, Mark feels much better.

### Questions

#### A. Multiple Choice

1. What was wrong with Mark?
   - a) He had a headache.
   - b) He had a stomachache.
   - c) He had a sore throat.

2. What time did Mark visit the doctor?
   - a) Nine o'clock
   - b) Ten o'clock
   - c) Eleven o'clock

3. What did the doctor tell Mark to do?
   - a) Play soccer
   - b) Get some rest
   - c) Go shopping

#### B. True or False

1. Mark went to work yesterday.
2. The doctor asked Mark some questions.
3. Mark feels better today.

## Grammar in Reading

Choose the correct option.

1. She ________ to school every day.
   - a) go
   - b) goes
   - c) going

2. They ________ at home yesterday.
   - a) was
   - b) were
   - c) are

3. I ________ a movie last night.
   - a) watched
   - b) watch
   - c) watching

4. He is ________ a book now.
   - a) read
   - b) reads
   - c) reading

5. We ________ visit our grandparents tomorrow.
   - a) are going to
   - b) going
   - c) goes to

6. You ________ drink more water.
   - a) should
   - b) shouldn't
   - c) are

7. There ________ two chairs in the room.
   - a) is
   - b) are
   - c) am

8. My birthday is ________ June.
   - a) at
   - b) on
   - c) in

9. The cat is ________ the table.
   - a) under
   - b) yesterday
   - c) quickly

10. I like tea ________ coffee.
   - a) but
   - b) and
   - c) because
