- Write a short paragraph describing your classroom.

## 2. Reading Passage

### Our English Classroom

My name is Lily, and I am a student at Green Valley School. My favorite classroom is our English classroom.

It is on the second floor of the school. The room is large, clean, and bright. There are twenty desks and twenty chairs for the students.

The teacher's desk is at the front of the room. There is a computer and a small lamp on it. A large whiteboard is behind the teacher's desk. Our teacher writes new words and sentences on the whiteboard.

There is a clock above the whiteboard. We can see the time during our lessons.

The classroom has four big windows. There are some plants near the windows, and there are colorful pictures on the walls.

My English teacher, Mr. Brown, is very friendly. He speaks slowly and explains new words. We usually read stories, learn grammar, and practice speaking English.

I sit next to my friend, Anna. We share books and help each other with our exercises.

I like our English classroom because it is a comfortable place to learn.
