# Chapter 22 — Seasons of the Year

## 1. Learning Objectives

- Identify the four seasons.
- Recognize common seasonal weather.
- Describe activities in different seasons.
- Understand simple seasonal vocabulary.
- Write about your favorite season.

## 2. Reading Passage

### My Favorite Season

There are four seasons in a year: spring, summer, fall, and winter. Each season has different weather and activities.

Spring is a beautiful season. The weather becomes warmer, flowers grow, and trees become green. I like walking in the park and looking at the flowers.

Summer is usually hot and sunny. My family often goes to the beach. We swim in the ocean, play games, and eat ice cream.

Fall comes after summer. The weather becomes cooler, and leaves change color. They turn yellow, orange, and red. I enjoy walking outside and collecting colorful leaves.

Winter is the coldest season. Sometimes it snows, and the streets become white. I wear a warm coat, gloves, and a hat. My family likes drinking hot chocolate at home.

My favorite season is fall because I love the colorful trees and cool weather.

## 3. Key Vocabulary

| Word | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Season | One of the four parts of the year |
| Spring | The season after winter |
| Summer | The warmest season in many places |
| Fall | The season after summer; also called autumn |
| Winter | The coldest season in many places |
| Flower | The colorful part of some plants |
| Beach | An area of sand or small stones beside water |
| Ocean | A very large area of salt water |
| Leaf | One flat green part of a plant |
| Leaves | More than one leaf |
| Collect | To bring things together |
| Change | To become different |
| Gloves | Clothing worn on the hands |
| Coat | Warm clothing worn over other clothes |
| Snow | Soft white ice that falls from clouds |
| Warm | A little hot |
| Cool | A little cold |
