# Chapter 18 — Asking for Directions

## 1. Learning Objectives

- Understand simple directions.
- Recognize direction vocabulary.
- Identify locations and landmarks.
- Follow a simple route.
- Write directions to a familiar place.

## 2. Reading Passage

### Where Is the Library?

Emma is new in town. She wants to visit the public library, but she does not know the way.

She sees a woman near the bus stop and asks, "Excuse me, where is the library?"

The woman smiles and says, "Go straight along this street. Turn left at the traffic light. Walk past the supermarket. The library is next to the bank."

Emma says, "Is it far from here?"

The woman answers, "No, it is about five minutes away on foot."

Emma thanks the woman and follows the directions. She walks straight, turns left, and passes the supermarket.

Soon, she sees the bank. The library is right next to it. Emma is happy because she finds the library easily.

## 3. Key Vocabulary

| Word or Phrase | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Direction | Information about how to get somewhere |
| Straight | Without turning left or right |
| Turn left | Change direction to the left |
| Turn right | Change direction to the right |
| Traffic light | A light that controls road traffic |
| Walk past | Walk beyond a place |
| Next to | Beside something |
| Across from | On the opposite side |
| Between | In the middle of two things |
| Near | A short distance away |
| Far | A long distance away |
| On foot | Walking |
| Bus stop | A place where buses stop |
| Landmark | A recognizable place or object |
| Excuse me | A polite expression used to get attention |
