# Chapter 35: Celebrations and Birthdays

## Learning Objectives

By the end of this chapter, you can:
- Read about a birthday party.
- Understand common celebration vocabulary.
- Identify people, activities, and times in a story.
- Write about a special celebration.

## Reading Passage

### My Birthday Party

My birthday is on June 15. Last Saturday, I had a birthday party at my house. I invited ten friends and my family.

My mother made a chocolate cake. My father bought some balloons and decorations. My sister helped me prepare the food.

The party started at three o'clock. My friends arrived with beautiful presents. We listened to music, played games, and took pictures.

At four o'clock, we ate the birthday cake. There were fifteen candles on the cake. Everyone sang "Happy Birthday" to me.

I opened my presents after the cake. I got a new book, a blue shirt, and a board game.

The party ended at six o'clock. I thanked everyone for coming. It was a very special day!

## Key Vocabulary

| Word | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Birthday | The day you were born |
| Party | A social event |
| Invite | To ask someone to come |
| Present | Something you give someone |
| Balloon | A colorful object filled with air |
| Decoration | Something used to make a place beautiful |
| Cake | A sweet food for celebrations |
| Candle | A small object that gives light |
| Celebrate | To do something special for an event |
| Gift | Another word for present |
| Special | Different and important |
