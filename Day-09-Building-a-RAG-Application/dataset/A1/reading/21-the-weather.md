# Chapter 21 — The Weather

## 1. Learning Objectives

- Recognize common weather words.
- Understand a simple weather report.
- Describe weather conditions.
- Connect weather with daily activities.
- Write about today's weather.

## 2. Reading Passage

### Weather in My Town

The weather changes every day in my town. I like sunny days because I enjoy spending time outside.

On Monday, it is sunny and warm. I go to the park with my friends. We play soccer and ride our bikes.

On Tuesday, it is cloudy and cool. I wear a light jacket when I go to school.

On Wednesday, it rains in the morning. I take an umbrella with me. The streets are wet, and the sky is gray.

On Thursday, it is windy. The trees move in the wind, and some leaves fall to the ground.

On Friday, the weather is sunny again. I feel happy because I can play outside after school.

My favorite weather is warm and sunny, but every kind of weather is interesting.

## 3. Key Vocabulary

| Word | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Weather | The condition of the air outside |
| Sunny | With a lot of sunlight |
| Cloudy | With many clouds |
| Rainy | With rain |
| Windy | With a lot of wind |
| Snowy | With snow |
| Warm | A little hot |
| Cool | A little cold |
| Cold | Having a low temperature |
| Hot | Having a high temperature |
| Umbrella | An object used to stay dry in the rain |
| Wet | Covered with water |
| Dry | Not wet |
| Sky | The space above the earth |
| Forecast | A prediction about future weather |
