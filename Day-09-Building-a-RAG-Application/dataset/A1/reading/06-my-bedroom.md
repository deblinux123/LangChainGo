# Chapter 06 — My Bedroom

## 1. Learning Objectives

By the end of this chapter, you will be able to:

- Understand a description of someone's bedroom.
- Identify common bedroom furniture and objects.
- Understand simple sentences about the location of objects.
- Recognize vocabulary about colors, sizes, and personal belongings.
- Answer reading comprehension questions.
- Write a short description of your own bedroom.

## 2. Reading Passage

### My Favorite Place

My name is Emma, and my bedroom is my favorite place in my house. It is not very big, but it is comfortable and bright.

The walls are light blue, and the floor is brown. There is a large window on the right side of my room. I have white curtains on the window. Every morning, sunlight comes into my bedroom.

My bed is next to the window. It has a blue blanket and two white pillows. There is a small nightstand beside my bed. I keep my alarm clock and a book on it.

My desk is near the door. I study there every evening. There is a computer, a lamp, and some notebooks on my desk. My chair is black and very comfortable.

I also have a bookshelf next to my desk. It has many books, a few photos, and some small toys.

I clean my bedroom every Saturday. I put my clothes in the closet and organize my books. I like keeping my room clean and tidy.

My bedroom is special to me because I can study, read, listen to music, and relax there.
