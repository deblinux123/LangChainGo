## 2. Reading Passage

### A Day in My Life

My name is Sarah, and I am a teacher. I live in a small apartment in Boston.

I usually wake up at 6:30 in the morning. First, I get out of bed and brush my teeth. Then, I take a shower and get dressed.

At 7:00, I have breakfast. I usually eat eggs and toast and drink a cup of coffee.

I leave home at 7:30 and take the bus to work. My school is not far from my apartment. I start work at 8:00.

I teach English to children. I have lunch at 12:30 with my coworkers. We usually eat in the school cafeteria.

I finish work at 3:30 in the afternoon. I take the bus home and relax for a while.

In the evening, I sometimes go for a walk or watch TV. I also prepare dinner and read a book.

I usually go to bed at 10:00. I like my daily routine because it is simple and organized.
