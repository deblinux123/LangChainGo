# Chapter 28 — My Morning

## 1. Learning Objectives

- Understand a morning routine.
- Recognize daily routine vocabulary.
- Identify times and activities.
- Describe your own morning.
- Write a simple daily routine.

## 2. Reading Passage

### A Good Start to My Day

My name is Sophie. I am a high school student. I usually wake up at six thirty in the morning.

First, I get out of bed and open my bedroom window. Then I make my bed and brush my teeth.

At seven o'clock, I take a shower and get dressed. I usually wear jeans, a T-shirt, and comfortable shoes.

After that, I go to the kitchen. My mother prepares breakfast for the family. I usually eat toast, eggs, and fruit. I drink a glass of milk.

At seven thirty, I pack my schoolbag and check my homework. Then I leave home and walk to the bus stop.

My school bus arrives at seven forty-five. I get on the bus and meet my friends.

I like my morning routine because it helps me start the day feeling ready and happy.

## 3. Key Vocabulary

| Word or Phrase | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Morning | The early part of the day |
| Wake up | Stop sleeping |
| Get out of bed | Leave your bed |
| Make the bed | Arrange the bed neatly |
| Brush | Clean using a brush |
| Take a shower | Wash your body under running water |
| Get dressed | Put on clothes |
| Prepare | Make something ready |
| Breakfast | The first meal of the day |
| Pack | Put things into a bag |
| Schoolbag | A bag used to carry school items |
| Leave | Go away from a place |
| Arrive | Reach a place |
| Routine | Things you do regularly |
| Ready | Prepared for something |
