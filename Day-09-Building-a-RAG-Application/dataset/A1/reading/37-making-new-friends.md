# Chapter 37: Making New Friends

## Learning Objectives

By the end of this chapter, you can:
- Read a simple story about meeting someone.
- Understand vocabulary about friendship.
- Identify questions and answers in a conversation.
- Write about a friend.

## Reading Passage

### My New Friend

My name is Daniel. I am a student at a new school. On my first day, I felt a little nervous because I did not know anyone.

During the break, I sat under a tree and ate my sandwich. A boy came to me and said, "Hello! My name is Alex. What is your name?"

I smiled and said, "I'm Daniel. Nice to meet you."

Alex asked me about my hobbies. I told him that I liked soccer, music, and video games. He liked soccer, too.

We talked for ten minutes. Then, Alex invited me to play soccer with his friends. I said yes.

At the end of the day, we exchanged phone numbers. Now, Alex is one of my good friends. I am happy at my new school.

## Key Vocabulary

| Word | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Friend | A person you like and know well |
| Nervous | Worried or a little afraid |
| Student | A person who studies |
| Break | A short rest from work or school |
| Smile | To show happiness with your face |
| Meet | To see someone for the first time |
| Hobby | An activity you enjoy |
| Invite | To ask someone to join you |
| Exchange | To give something and receive something |
| Together | With another person |
| Friendly | Kind and pleasant |
