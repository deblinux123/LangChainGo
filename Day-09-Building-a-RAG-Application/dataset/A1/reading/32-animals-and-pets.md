# Chapter 32 — Animals and Pets

## 1. Learning Objectives

- Recognize common animal names.
- Understand a text about pets.
- Describe animals and their habits.
- Identify basic animal care activities.
- Write about a favorite animal.

## 2. Reading Passage

### My Pet Dog

My name is Noah, and I have a pet dog named Max. Max is three years old. He is brown and white, and he has a long tail.

Max is very friendly and playful. He loves running in the garden and playing with his red ball.

Every morning, I give him food and fresh water. My father takes him for a walk before work. In the afternoon, I play with him after school.

Max likes meeting other dogs in the park. Sometimes, he runs after a small ball, and I throw it for him.

I also like visiting the zoo. I enjoy seeing lions, elephants, monkeys, and giraffes. They are very interesting animals.

I love animals because they make our world beautiful. I think people should be kind to animals and take care of them.

## 3. Key Vocabulary

| Word | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Animal | A living creature |
| Pet | An animal kept at home |
| Dog | A common domestic animal |
| Cat | A small domestic animal |
| Tail | The part at the back of an animal's body |
| Friendly | Kind and pleasant |
| Playful | Enjoying games and play |
| Garden | An area where plants grow |
| Feed | Give food to an animal |
| Walk | A journey on foot |
| Zoo | A place where people can see different animals |
| Lion | A large wild cat |
| Elephant | A very large animal with a long trunk |
| Monkey | An animal that can climb trees |
| Giraffe | A tall animal with a very long neck |
| Take care of | Look after someone or something |
