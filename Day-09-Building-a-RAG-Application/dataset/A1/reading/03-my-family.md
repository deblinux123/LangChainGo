# Chapter 03 — My Family

## 1. Learning Objectives

By the end of this chapter, you will be able to:

- Understand a short text about a family.
- Identify family members and their relationships.
- Find personal information in a reading passage.
- Understand basic vocabulary about family life.
- Answer reading comprehension questions.
- Write a short paragraph about your family.

## 2. Reading Passage

### A Big Happy Family

My name is Michael Brown. I am 28 years old, and I live in a house with my family in Denver, Colorado.

There are five people in my family: my father, my mother, my younger sister, my younger brother, and me.

My father's name is Robert. He is 55 years old. He works at a hospital. He is a doctor, and he loves his job.

My mother's name is Linda. She is 52 years old. She is a teacher. She works at an elementary school. She likes reading books and cooking.

My sister's name is Emily. She is 22 years old. She is a university student. She studies biology.

My younger brother, Jack, is 15 years old. He goes to high school. He enjoys playing basketball and video games.

We are a happy family. On weekends, we usually have breakfast together. Sometimes, we visit our grandparents. My grandparents live near our house.

I love my family because we help each other and spend time together.
