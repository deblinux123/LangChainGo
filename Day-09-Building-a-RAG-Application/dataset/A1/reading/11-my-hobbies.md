# Chapter 11 — My Hobbies

## 1. Learning Objectives

By the end of this chapter, you will be able to:

- Understand a simple text about hobbies.
- Recognize common free-time activities.
- Describe what people like and enjoy doing.
- Identify how often someone does an activity.
- Answer reading comprehension questions.
- Write a short paragraph about your hobbies.

## 2. Reading Passage

### My Favorite Hobbies

My name is Alex, and I am thirteen years old. I am a student, and I have many hobbies. I enjoy doing different activities in my free time.

My favorite hobby is reading. I like reading adventure stories and funny books. I usually read for thirty minutes before I go to bed. My favorite book is about a young boy who travels around the world.

I also enjoy playing soccer. I play soccer with my friends every Saturday afternoon. We usually play at the park near my house. My best friend, Jack, is on my team.

Another hobby I like is drawing. I often draw pictures of animals, trees, and houses. I use pencils, colored pens, and paper. Sometimes, I give my drawings to my family.

On Sundays, I like listening to music. My favorite music is pop music. I also sometimes watch movies with my sister.

My hobbies make me happy. They help me relax, learn new things, and spend time with my friends and family.
