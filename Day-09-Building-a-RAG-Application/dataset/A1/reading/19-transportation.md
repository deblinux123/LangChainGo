# Chapter 19 — Transportation

## 1. Learning Objectives

- Identify common means of transportation.
- Understand how people travel.
- Recognize transportation vocabulary.
- Describe simple journeys.
- Write about your favorite way to travel.

## 2. Reading Passage

### How I Get to School

My name is Ben, and I live about three miles from my school.

Every morning, I take the school bus. The bus comes to a stop near my house at seven thirty. I get on the bus with some of my friends.

My father drives to work in his car. He usually leaves home early because there is a lot of traffic.

My mother prefers riding her bicycle. She works at a small store near our home, so she does not need a car.

On weekends, my family sometimes takes the train to visit my grandparents. The train station is not far from our house.

I like traveling by train because I can look out of the window and enjoy the view.

Different types of transportation help people travel from one place to another.

## 3. Key Vocabulary

| Word | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Transportation | The movement of people or things |
| Bus | A large vehicle that carries passengers |
| Train | A vehicle that travels on railway tracks |
| Car | A road vehicle |
| Bicycle | A vehicle with two wheels |
| Airplane | A vehicle that flies |
| Subway | An underground train system |
| Station | A place where trains or buses stop |
| Traffic | Vehicles moving on roads |
| Passenger | A person traveling in a vehicle |
| Driver | A person who drives a vehicle |
| Get on | To enter a bus, train, or plane |
| Get off | To leave a bus, train, or plane |
| Journey | Travel from one place to another |
| View | What you can see from a place |
