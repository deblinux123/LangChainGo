# Chapter 30 — Healthy Lifestyle

## 1. Learning Objectives

- Recognize vocabulary about healthy habits.
- Understand a simple text about healthy living.
- Identify good daily habits.
- Describe personal routines for staying healthy.
- Write about a healthy lifestyle.

## 2. Reading Passage

### Healthy Habits

My name is Olivia, and I try to have a healthy lifestyle.

Every morning, I wake up early and drink a glass of water. Then I eat a healthy breakfast with eggs, whole-grain bread, and fruit.

I walk to school because it is good exercise. After school, I sometimes ride my bicycle or play basketball with my friends.

I also like eating vegetables. My favorite vegetables are carrots, tomatoes, and broccoli. I do not eat too many sweets because I want to take care of my health.

At night, I usually stop using my phone before bedtime. I read a book and go to sleep at ten o'clock.

I believe that healthy food, regular exercise, and enough sleep are important for a healthy life.

## 3. Key Vocabulary

| Word | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Healthy | Good for your health |
| Lifestyle | The way a person lives |
| Habit | Something you do regularly |
| Exercise | Physical activity that keeps you active |
| Whole-grain | Made from grains that keep their natural parts |
| Vegetable | A plant used as food |
| Sweet | Food with a sugary taste |
| Health | The condition of your body and mind |
| Regular | Happening often or at fixed times |
| Enough | As much as needed |
| Sleep | Rest when your body and mind are inactive |
| Bedtime | The time you usually go to bed |
| Take care of | Protect or look after something |
| Active | Moving or doing physical activities |
| Believe | Think that something is true |
