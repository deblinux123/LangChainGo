# Chapter 17 — Places in Town

## 1. Learning Objectives

- Recognize common places in town.
- Understand what people do in different places.
- Identify services in a town.
- Describe places near your home.
- Write about places in your town.

## 2. Reading Passage

### A Day in Town

My name is Oliver. Today, I am spending the day in town with my father.

First, we go to the bank. My father needs to get some money. Then we visit the post office because he wants to send a package to my grandmother.

After that, we go to the library. I borrow two books about animals. My father reads a newspaper while I look for books.

At noon, we have lunch at a small restaurant. The food is very good.

In the afternoon, we visit the bookstore. I buy a new notebook and a pencil. Then we walk to the pharmacy to buy some toothpaste.

Before going home, we stop at the park. I play on the playground while my father sits on a bench.

I enjoy visiting different places in town because every place has a different purpose.

## 3. Key Vocabulary

| Word | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Bank | A place where people keep or get money |
| Post office | A place for sending letters and packages |
| Library | A place where people borrow books |
| Bookstore | A store that sells books |
| Pharmacy | A store that sells medicine and health products |
| Restaurant | A place where people buy and eat food |
| Playground | An outdoor area where children play |
| Bench | A long seat for several people |
| Package | Something wrapped for sending |
| Borrow | To take something for a short time and return it |
| Newspaper | A publication with news |
| Purpose | The reason something is used |
| Town | A place where people live, smaller than a city |
| Send | To make something go to another person or place |
| Visit | To go to a place or person |
