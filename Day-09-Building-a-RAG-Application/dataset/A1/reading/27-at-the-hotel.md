# Chapter 27 — At the Hotel

## 1. Learning Objectives

- Recognize common hotel vocabulary.
- Understand a hotel check-in experience.
- Identify hotel rooms and services.
- Describe a hotel stay.
- Write about staying at a hotel.

## 2. Reading Passage

### Our Hotel Room

Last summer, my family stayed at a hotel near the beach. The hotel was large and beautiful.

When we arrived, my father went to the front desk. He gave the receptionist our reservation information and received the room key.

Our room was on the third floor. It had two beds, a television, a small refrigerator, and a private bathroom.

The room also had a balcony with a beautiful view of the sea. My sister loved sitting there in the morning.

Every day, we had breakfast in the hotel restaurant. The food was fresh, and there were many choices.

The hotel staff were friendly. They helped us when we needed information about the town.

We stayed at the hotel for four nights. We enjoyed our vacation and hope to visit again.

## 3. Key Vocabulary

| Word | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Hotel | A place where travelers stay |
| Receptionist | A person who welcomes visitors |
| Front desk | The main service counter in a hotel |
| Reservation | An arrangement to use a room |
| Room key | A key used to enter a room |
| Floor | One level of a building |
| Balcony | A small outdoor area connected to a room |
| Refrigerator | A machine that keeps food cold |
| Private bathroom | A bathroom used by one room or group |
| Staff | People who work at a place |
| View | What you can see from a place |
| Guest | A person staying at a hotel |
| Check in | Register when arriving at a hotel |
| Check out | Complete the departure process |
| Accommodation | A place to stay |
