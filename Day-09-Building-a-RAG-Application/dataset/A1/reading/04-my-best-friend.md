# Chapter 04 — My Best Friend

## 1. Learning Objectives

By the end of this chapter, you will be able to:

- Understand a short text about a person's best friend.
- Identify basic information about another person.
- Recognize simple adjectives that describe people.
- Understand vocabulary about friendship and free-time activities.
- Answer reading comprehension questions.
- Write a short paragraph describing a friend.

## 2. Reading Passage

### My Best Friend, Daniel

My best friend's name is Daniel. He is 25 years old, and he lives in Austin, Texas. We are neighbors, and we have known each other for five years.

Daniel is tall and has short brown hair and green eyes. He is friendly, funny, and helpful. He always makes me laugh.

Daniel works at a bookstore. He likes reading books, especially adventure stories. He also enjoys listening to music and playing soccer.

We usually meet after work or on weekends. Sometimes, we go to a coffee shop and talk. Other times, we watch movies at home or play video games.

Daniel is a good friend because he always listens to me. When I have a problem, he tries to help me. I also help him when he needs something.

We have different hobbies, but we enjoy spending time together. I am happy to have a friend like Daniel.

| Coffee shop | A place where people buy and drink coffee |
| Problem | Something difficult that needs a solution |

| Hobby | An activity you enjoy in your free time |
