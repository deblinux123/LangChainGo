# Chapter 20 — My Neighborhood

## 1. Learning Objectives

- Understand a description of a neighborhood.
- Recognize common places near a home.
- Describe neighbors and local facilities.
- Use simple location vocabulary.
- Write about your neighborhood.

## 2. Reading Passage

### My Friendly Neighborhood

I live in a quiet neighborhood with my parents and my younger brother.

Our house is on a small street. There is a grocery store across from our house and a bakery next to it. I often walk to the bakery with my mother to buy fresh bread.

There is a small park at the end of our street. Children play there after school, and older people walk in the morning.

My neighbors are very friendly. Mr. Green lives next door. He has a beautiful garden with flowers and trees. Sometimes, he gives us fresh vegetables from his garden.

There is also a bus stop near our home. My father takes the bus to work every morning.

I like my neighborhood because it is safe, clean, and peaceful. Many people know each other and help their neighbors.

## 3. Key Vocabulary

| Word | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Neighborhood | An area where people live |
| Neighbor | A person who lives near you |
| Grocery store | A store that sells food |
| Bakery | A place where bread and cakes are made or sold |
| Across from | On the opposite side |
| Next door | In the house or building beside yours |
| At the end of | In the final part of something |
| Garden | An outdoor area where plants grow |
| Flower | The colorful part of some plants |
| Safe | Protected from danger |
| Clean | Not dirty |
| Peaceful | Quiet and calm |
| Local | Related to a nearby area |
| Community | People living in the same area |
| Fresh | Recently made or picked |
