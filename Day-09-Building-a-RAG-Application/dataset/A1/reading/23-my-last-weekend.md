# Chapter 23 — My Last Weekend

## 1. Learning Objectives

- Understand a simple story about past activities.
- Recognize common past-tense verbs.
- Identify the order of past events.
- Describe what happened last weekend.
- Write about a past experience.

## 2. Reading Passage

### A Wonderful Saturday

My name is Daniel. Last weekend was wonderful. On Saturday morning, I woke up at eight o'clock and had breakfast with my family.

After breakfast, I visited my friend Mike. We played soccer in the park for two hours. Then we bought sandwiches and ate lunch together.

In the afternoon, I went shopping with my mother. We bought some clothes and a new pair of shoes. I was very happy with my new shoes.

In the evening, my family watched a funny movie. We ate popcorn and drank orange juice.

On Sunday, I stayed at home. I cleaned my room, read a book, and finished my homework. In the evening, I prepared my schoolbag for Monday.

I enjoyed my weekend because I spent time with my family and friends.

## 3. Key Vocabulary

| Word or Phrase | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Last weekend | The weekend before this one |
| Wonderful | Very good |
| Woke up | Stopped sleeping |
| Visited | Went to see someone |
| Played | Took part in a game |
| Bought | Paid money for something |
| Together | With another person |
| Popcorn | A snack made from corn |
| Stayed | Remained in a place |
| Cleaned | Made something clean |
| Finished | Completed something |
| Prepared | Got something ready |
