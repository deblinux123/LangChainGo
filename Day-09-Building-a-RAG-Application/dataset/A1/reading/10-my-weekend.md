## 2. Reading Passage

### A Fun Weekend

My name is Emily. I am a student, and I love weekends. I do not go to school on Saturday and Sunday.

On Saturday, I usually wake up at nine o'clock. I have breakfast with my family. We eat eggs, bread, and fruit. After breakfast, I clean my bedroom and help my mother in the kitchen.

In the afternoon, I meet my best friend, Sarah. We sometimes go to the park and ride our bikes. We also like walking and talking together. When the weather is bad, we stay at home and watch movies.

On Saturday evening, my family and I have dinner together. Sometimes, we order pizza. After dinner, I read a book or play a board game with my brother.

On Sunday morning, I visit my grandparents. They live near our house. My grandmother makes delicious cookies, and my grandfather tells us funny stories.

In the afternoon, I do my homework and prepare my schoolbag for Monday. In the evening, I take a shower and go to bed early.

My weekends are relaxing and fun. I enjoy spending time with my family and friends.
