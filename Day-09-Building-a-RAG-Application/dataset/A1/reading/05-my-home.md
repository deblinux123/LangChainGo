# Chapter 05 — My Home

## 1. Learning Objectives

By the end of this chapter, you will be able to:

- Understand a short text describing a home.
- Identify different rooms in a house.
- Recognize common furniture and household objects.
- Understand simple descriptions of rooms and locations.
- Answer reading comprehension questions.
- Write a short paragraph describing your home.

## 2. Reading Passage

### Welcome to My House

My name is Olivia, and I live in a comfortable house with my parents and my younger brother. Our house is in a quiet neighborhood in Portland, Oregon.

Our house has two floors, three bedrooms, two bathrooms, a kitchen, and a living room.

On the first floor, there is a large living room. It has a comfortable sofa, two chairs, a television, and a small table. We usually sit together in the living room and watch movies.

The kitchen is next to the living room. It is bright and clean. There is a refrigerator, an oven, a stove, and a big table. My mother usually cooks dinner in the kitchen.

There are three bedrooms on the second floor. My parents have a large bedroom. My brother has a small bedroom with a blue bed and a desk.

My bedroom is my favorite room. It has a white bed, a desk, a chair, and a bookshelf. There are some books on the bookshelf. I also have a window next to my bed. I like reading near the window.

Behind the house, there is a small garden. My father grows flowers and vegetables there.

I love our house because it is comfortable, clean, and full of happy memories.
